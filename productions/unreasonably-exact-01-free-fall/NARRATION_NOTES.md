Ye notes sirf recording ke liye hain. Bolne ke alfaaz [SCRIPT.md](SCRIPT.md) se aate hain. Ye guide meri craft ki raaye hai, **measured nahi**: kisi retention data se sabit nahi hua ke ye lehja behtar chalta hai. Pehli recording ke baad apne kaan par bharosa karein.

## Awaaz ka lehja

Ek samajhdaar dost jo halka mazaak karta hai lekin chehra seedha rakhta hai. Jokes ko hansi ke saath na bolein. "Trailer voice" nahi.

## Raftaar

- Script ~890 words ki hai.
- Normal hissa: ~3 words per second (aaram se).
- Numbers aur jokes: aur dheemay.
- Pehle is raftaar par record karein, phir sunein. Agar thaka hua lage to thora tez karein. Asli runtime recording ke baad hi pata chalega; heading ke timestamps sirf andaza hain.

## Hook (0:00–0:31), sab se zaroori

| Line | Kaise |
|---|---|
| "You step off into space." | Pehle 1 sec khamoshi. Phir dheemay, nichi awaaz. "Aap" se baat, seedha sunne wale se. |
| "Four hundred kilometres up. Nothing under your feet." | Do chhote jumlay, beech mein pause. "Nothing" par zor. |
| "For four minutes, nothing happens." | Is ke baad poora 1.5 sec ruk jayein. Ye khamoshi hi suspense hai. |
| "Then you hit the air at more than eight thousand kilometres an hour." | Thora tez. "Eight thousand" par zor. Ke baad 1 sec poori khamoshi (cut to black). |
| "Everyone knows the rule: fall from higher, and you hit the ground harder." | Awaaz **halki aur maamooli**, jaise aam si baat. Hook ka contrast yehi hai: pehle dramatic, ab normal. |
| "So here's the test. One metre, a hundred metres, all the way back up to here…" | Thori tez. "Somewhere on this ladder, the rule breaks" par ruk kar, thora nicha. |
| "Does a higher fall always hit harder?" | Sab se saaf, sab se dheemay. Ye title hai, ye poori video ka waada hai. |

Hook ko sab se zyada baar record karein (5+ takes). Baqi hissa 2–3 takes mein theek ho jata hai.

## Recording ka tareeqa

- Poori video ek saath na parhein. **Hissa ba hissa** record karein, har hissa 2–3 takes.
- Har take ke shuru aur aakhir mein 1 sec khamoshi (room tone) chhorein, taake edit mein jagah ho.
- Pehle hook record karein jab awaaz taazi ho.
- Ek naram, band jagah, mic munh se ek hi doori par. Takes ke beech awaaz ki doori na badlein.
- Ghalti ho to ruk kar **us jumle ko dobara shuru se** bolein, beech mein se nahi. Edit mein asaani hoti hai.
- Pani paas rakhein. Lambi recording mein awaaz thak kar tez ho jati hai.
