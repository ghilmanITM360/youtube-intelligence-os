# Titles and hooks

- **Recommended:** Why Can a Skydiver Slow Down Without a Parachute? Accurate because the script explains a possible slowdown in a fixed-posture model. Risk: it does not mean every skydiver slows under all conditions.
- **Alternative:** How Can You Slow Down While Falling? Accurate for the depicted air-drag example; risk: no atmosphere is specified in the title.
- **Alternative:** Falling Faster, Then Slower: What Changed? Accurate for the model's speed history; risk: does not identify the skydiver until the opening image.

**Hook A, recommended:** Gravity is pulling you down. There's no parachute slowing you. So why is your speed dropping? It states the apparent contradiction resolved by net force; the model conditions follow immediately.

**Hook B:** The ground is getting closer. Your speedometer is falling. What is slowing you down? It makes direction and speed visual; the next line must establish the fall and lack of parachute deployment.

No title or hook predicts audience performance.
