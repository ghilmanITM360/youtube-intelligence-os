# Titles and hooks

- **Recommended:** Why Does a Signal Take a Day to Reach Voyager 1? Accurate near the dated milestone; the delay changes.
- **Alternative:** A Message to Voyager Takes a Day. Why? Same dated approximation; avoid presenting it as timeless.
- **Alternative:** How Far Away Is One Light-Day? Accurate because the explanation defines the distance using Voyager; it is broader than the mission.

**A:** Send a message to Voyager 1, and the signal sets off at the speed of light. It still needs about a day to get there. Accurate near the milestone.

**B:** A signal can cross the distance to the Moon in just over a second. Reaching Voyager takes about a day. Both figures are approximate and date the second in the narration.
