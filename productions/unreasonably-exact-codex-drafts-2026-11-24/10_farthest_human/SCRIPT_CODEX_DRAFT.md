## Voiceover

Who has been farthest away from the nearest other person? That is a different record from being farthest from Earth.

Guinness World Records credits Apollo 15 astronaut Al Worden. In 1971 he orbited the Moon alone while crewmates David Scott and James Irwin were on the lunar surface. Guinness gives his greatest distance from the nearest other person as about 3,596 kilometres.

The geometry helps us picture it. When the Moon is between an orbiting astronaut and people on its far side, their separation can be roughly the Moon's diameter plus the spacecraft's altitude. That is our illustration, not a reconstruction of Guinness's measurement.

A later crew could travel farther from Earth without breaking this particular record: if its members travel together, each person's nearest neighbour is inside the same spacecraft.

The question isn't simply how far humanity travelled. It is how far one person got from everybody else.
