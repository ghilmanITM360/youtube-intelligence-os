## Voiceover

What is the farthest anyone has been from every other human being? There's a catch: flying farther from Earth doesn't automatically win. If another astronaut is beside you, your nearest neighbour is right there.

During Apollo 15 in 1971, astronaut Al Worden orbited the Moon alone while David Scott and James Irwin were down on its surface. As Worden travelled behind the Moon, a whole world stood between him and his crewmates.

Guinness World Records credits Worden with a maximum distance from the nearest other human of about three thousand six hundred kilometres. Picture the Moon's diameter, plus some altitude: that's the scale, although it isn't a reconstruction of the record calculation.

In 2026, the four Artemis Two astronauts travelled farther from Earth than any earlier crew. But they travelled together, so that is a different record.

The surprising measure isn't how far a spacecraft went. It's how far one person was from the next person, in every direction.
