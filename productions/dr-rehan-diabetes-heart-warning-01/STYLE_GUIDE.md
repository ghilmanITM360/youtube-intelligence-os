# Original art direction: warm clinical editorial

1080 × 1920 portrait master. Warm ivory, deep ink #203336, muted teal #397c7b, terracotta #c3654f, ochre #d9aa55. Rounded-rectangular heads, open white almond eyes and dark pupils, articulated simplified hands with a thumb and unified fingers, substantial trouser legs. These deliberately differ from Casual Economics round heads, highlighted black oval eyes, mitts and stick legs. Head occupies about 40% of figure height. Outlines 5 px in 600 × 900 character sources, scaled with the figure. Flat fills with one shadow plane. No brand/logo copying.

Detailed original home and clinic: skirting, flooring, shelves, plants, framed abstract prints, window mullions, seating, curtains, furniture. Separate far/background, furnishing and foreground layers. Reuse sets rather than changing environment on each phrase. Concept diagrams use ivory/white; a single charcoal chapter reset introduces risk groups. No stock icons or SaaS cards.

Text: Arial bold, system fallback sans-serif; SVG text stays editable. Delivery PNGs fix current font appearance. Handoff must recheck typography if rendered with another font. Hero 76–88 px; explanatory copy 48–56 px; source shorthand 34 px. Main messages inside x=90..920, y=180..1530; rightmost 160 px and bottom 300 px reserved for platform chrome. Source shorthand is supplementary, never the only place a safety caveat appears. Final platform overlay varies; test actual upload preview.

Use one proposition per state; 3–6 s recommended reading holds, 6–9 s for urgent-care explanatory state. These are suggestions, not audio timing. No forced 60-second duration. Expression/pose changes are hold swaps; no mouth animation, walk cycle, camera rig or physics. Warning emphasis is calm, no flashing heart or fabricated ECG.

Approval: internally reviewed draft direction; owner art-direction approval pending. All delivered artwork is original procedural vector drawing authored for this package, not AI raster generation or traced source imagery.
