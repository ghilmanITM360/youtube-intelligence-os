## 0:00 — Hook `[SIMULATION]`
*Vertical frame. A suited figure at 10 km. SPEED readout: 0. Chip: SIMULATION · ±15%.*

Jump from ten kilometres up, and for twenty seconds you speed up to about three hundred and twenty kilometres an hour.

*The readout climbs to 320 and holds for a beat.*

Then you slow down. While you're still falling.


## 0:10 — Why `[THE MATH]`
*Thermometer chip: −50 °C. Two arrows, WEIGHT down and AIR up. The AIR arrow starts small and grows as the figure drops.*

Ten kilometres up, the air is thin and about minus fifty. It barely pushes back, so you keep speeding up.

But as you drop, the air gets thicker, and thicker air pushes harder.

*Readout: 320 → 265 at 5 km → 204.*

By five kilometres you're down to about two hundred and sixty-five. Near the ground, about two hundred. That's the same speed you'd reach after dropping from one kilometre.


## 0:31 — The punch `[THE MATH]`
*Two lines on the HUD: PEAK (tall, rising) and LANDING (flat at 204). The figure lands.*

Peak speed climbs with height. Landing speed doesn't.

You fall faster on the way down than you land. The air does the braking.

*End card: "The full ladder: 1 metre to 400 km" with the Day 1 title.*
