## Design and motion direction

Use an original shopper named **Remy**: broad solid torso, swept plum curls, angular warm-brown face, teal sweater, cream cuffs and solid hands. Preserve head shape, hair mass, eye spacing and palette. This is distinct from the competitor's round peach stick-figure host and from the existing playbook's Mira/Nori pilots. No competitor SVG, MP4, logo or illustration is imported.

Color grammar: navy `#142A38` for structure/text; teal `#147D83` for the offer and repayment path; coral `#F16850` for the conditional charge; cream `#FFF5DF` for paper; gold `#E8BC62` for time. Meaning also appears in words and symbols, never color alone.

Make Remy investigate, point, transfer a payment and visibly relax or pause when the condition resolves. Avoid static host-plus-card repetition. Specific hand contacts, eye direction and a readable hold address the prior pilot's documented acting and staging gaps. No parity with Casually Finance is claimed from two still images.

During the mechanism, reduce the promotional balance as payments arrive while interest entries accumulate separately. At full compliant payoff, erase/waive the accumulated interest; at missed full payoff, transfer the accrued interest to the bill. The receipt is explicitly schematic and contains no monetary values. Do not imply accrual is undisclosed or that the original balance remains unchanged.
