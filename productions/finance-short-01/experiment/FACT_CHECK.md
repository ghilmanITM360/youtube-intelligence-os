# Deferred-interest fact check

Checked October 13, 2026. Scope: U.S. deferred-interest credit-card promotions. This is an educational mechanism, not individualized borrowing advice. The script makes no claim about every lender, country or installment product.

## Primary sources

- **S1 — CFPB consumer explanation**, reviewed January 22, 2024; page modified February 2, 2024. [Full page](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/i-got-a-credit-card-promising-no-interest-for-a-purchase-if-i-pay-in-full-within-12-months-how-does-this-work-en-40/). Relevant sections: calculation, minimum payments, five payoff tips.
- **S2 — CFPB promotion comparison**, published June 8, 2017; page footer modified June 25, 2026. The older publication date remains authoritative for its original example. [Full page](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/archive/blog/how-understand-special-promotional-financing-offers-credit-cards/). Relevant section: differences between zero interest and deferred interest.
- **S3 — Synchrony product explanation**, undated, accessed October 13, 2026. [Full page](https://www.synchrony.com/consumer-resources/deferred-interest). Relevant sections: definition and How It Works. First-party lender documentation corroborates the product mechanism; it is not an independent consumer-outcome study.
- **S4 — CFPB Regulation Z, §1026.16(h)**, current online text accessed October 13, 2026. [Advertising provision](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/16/). Required disclosure explains the conditional obligation from the transaction date. Used to verify scope, not to imply an unlawful or hidden advertisement.

No numerical interest calculation is needed. Dollar amounts, APR averages, catastrophe forecasts and invented customer losses are excluded. The only spoken duration is the clearly illustrative twelve-month offer. No claim from a competitor script is used as financial proof.

## Important omitted complexity

Late payments, multiple balances and payment allocation can affect outcomes. The script retains the terms qualifier and does not prescribe a payment schedule. S1 discusses these details; they can be linked in a future video description. Do not add a slogan suggesting that paying the minimum always guarantees the promotion or that paying an arbitrary amount early guarantees allocation.
