## Production choices (not recovered competitor specifications)
1080×1920, 30fps. Ink #292A2D, teal #327C79, ochre #E6AF53, coral #C86855, skin #EDC3A2, paper white. Human figures have actual sleeves, trousers, hands and shoes, differing from the source stick narrator. Hand-shaped contours use cubic paths rather than perfect UI boxes. Larger faces and small expressive brows. Character eye/head dimensions remain fixed across poses. Handwritten-style headlines, short functional labels, generous white space. No financial logos, copied characters, medical coats implying the fictional host's credentials, or diagnostic ECGs.

## Clinical visual boundaries
Heart is a symbolic silhouette, not clinical anatomy. Highlight rings mark joints without illustrating damage. Rating is explicitly the speaker's opinion, not an efficacy score. Calendar claims stay attributed and review-gated. Water supports the swimmer but joints visibly articulate. No disease cure, guaranteed outcome, universal training prescription, or mortality chart is invented. Proposed correction overlays remain optional and identified in claim registers; the audio is unchanged.

## Keyframes and readability
Frame positions are rounded from ASR word/phrase timing and need listening alignment. Tracks use explicit integer frame, x/y, scale, rotation, opacity, hold and easing. World coordinates top-left; rotation around registered local pivot; z sorted ascending. UI labels stay outside character transforms. Use a 6–10 frame entrance, then hold the meaningful pose. No compulsory motion quota. The opening phrase is illustrated immediately; preserve clipped source openings rather than invent speech. Full source duration, including non-speaking tail, is covered.
