## Title
Why Is Rent So High? How the Official Rent Number Is Measured

## Description
Two published rent numbers, from two different sources, can sound like the same thing and are not. This video shows how the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics builds its CPI rent number (a sample of homes, each priced every six months) and how Zillow's rent index differs (a level in dollars, plus a change in that level).

What the video says: in the year to September 2026 the BLS rent index rose 2.8% and Zillow's index rose 2.7%. In October 2026 Zillow's smoothed U.S. rent index was $1,913 against $1,859 a year earlier (+2.9%). The BLS did not print an October 12-month rent change because October 2025 survey data were not collected. The video does not say what your rent will be and is not financial advice. It describes how two published measures are built.
