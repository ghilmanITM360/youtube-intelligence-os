## 1. BLS factsheet "Measuring Price Change in the CPI: Rent and Rental Equivalence"
- URL: https://www.bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/owners-equivalent-rent-and-rent.htm  (page footer: Last Modified Date: February 13, 2026)
- "The CPI program collects rent data from each sampled unit every 6 months." (Price Change and Quality Adjustment) -> C-07, S020
- Six subsamples ("panels"); "The rents for panel 1 are collected in January and July; panel 2, in February and August, etc." -> R7 75-122 s panel scenes
- Age adjustment: "an age-bias factor is applied to the previous rent" -> S023 area
- Vacant units: "The CPI program assumes that sample units reported to be vacant are transitioning to new tenants ... The estimated current rent of a vacant unit is its previous rent times the average rent change of newly occupied units." -> S024-S026 (vacancy)
- Sample: "BLS data collectors visit a small number (usually 5) of renter-occupied housing units selected to represent each segment." The BLS prices a SAMPLE of units, not every rental. This is why the title must not imply every rental is measured.
- Table A "Relative importance, December 2025": Shelter 35.625; Rent of primary residence 7.840; OER of residences 26.204. NOTE: these are December 2025 weights, a different vintage from the Aug 2026 weights on screen (below). No on-screen weight mixes the two.
- Search of the factsheet text for a sentence saying CPI rent "trails" or lags market rents: none found (C-13 claim "we looked, did not find").

## 3. CPI news release, October 2026 data (released Nov 10, 2026)

- Section "October 2026 12-Month Percent Changes": "BLS did not collect survey data for October 2025 because of a lapse in appropriations. For most CPI series, BLS could not publish October 2025 indexes. As a result, BLS cannot publish 12-month percent changes for October 2026 for indexes where October 2025 data are unavailable." Tables carry "NOTE: The Oct 2025 data values are not available due to the 2025 lapse in appropriations."  C-18.
- Precise reading: the missing data is the OCTOBER 2025 reference period. BLS did collect October 2026 data (it printed the one-month +0.2%). It cannot publish an Oct 2026 12-month change.
