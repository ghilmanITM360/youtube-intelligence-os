## REF-MOON-018  `moon_limb_large_clean_edge_earth_behind_artemis2_nasa_art002e009287.jpg`
- source: https://images.nasa.gov/details/art002e009287 (NASA)
- licence: PUBLIC_DOMAIN_NASA NASA / Artemis II crew
- perspective: large Moon limb with a razor-sharp edge against black, half-lit; tiny Earth crescent behind the limb
- what is visible: Moon limb, large: a half-lit grey globe fills the frame from the bottom with the curved limb as a sharp, perfectly clean edge against black space (no haze). Dense overlapping craters, a few large basins, lit from the top so the shadowed lower part fades to black along an irregular terminator. A tiny crescent Earth (blue-white) sits just behind the limb at upper left, almost touching it. No stars.
- influences: Settles the Moon limb treatment for MOON_001/004: clean antialiased edge, NO rim glow, terminator follows terrain (jagged), shadowed side almost black; Earth is only a few percent of the Moon diameter here.
- assets: MOON_001_GLOBE MOON_004_GLOBE_SHADE

- source: https://images.nasa.gov/details/iss039e001440 (NASA)
- licence: PUBLIC_DOMAIN_NASA NASA / ISS Expedition 39 crew

- what is visible: A small, pale-blue/white Moon with faint maria, flattened into a squat dome shape (the bottom is cut flat and tinted pink-orange) by atmospheric refraction near the horizon, floating in a smooth vertical gradient from dark navy at top to brighter blue at the bottom (the airglow/atmosphere seen from orbit). ## REF-MOON-001  `moon_nearside_fullmoon_lro_svs_nasa_e001861.jpg`
- source: https://images.nasa.gov/details/GSFC_20171208_Archive_e001861 (NASA GSFC Scientific Visualization Studio (LRO LOLA/LROC data))
- licence: PUBLIC_DOMAIN_NASA Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio
- perspective: near-side full disc, north up, full phase; computer visualization from LRO data, not a photograph
- what is visible: Complete lit disc on black, sharp limb. Large dark grey maria blotches fill the upper left and the left-centre (a very big dark region with irregular edges), a smaller round dark mare near the upper-right limb, and mid-grey patches in the lower centre; the rest is pale grey-white highland full of craters, densest in the south (bottom). Bright ray craters stand out: one near the centre-left with long radial rays, plus a smaller one beside it and a bright dot near the left, which by position match Copernicus, Kepler and Aristarchus (not labelled in the source). Bright rays also fan from the bright southern region. Relief is flat at full phase, since the Sun is behind the viewer, with only the last few percent at the limb darker. The whole disc is neutral grey with a slight warm tint.
- influences: Texture target for MOON_001: two-tone maria/highlands, strong ray systems, craters get denser toward the south. For the big full-disc beats, keep the shading subtle at full phase (flat), and use a strong terminator for any phase other than full.
- assets: MOON_001_GLOBE MOON_004_GLOBE_SHADE

## REF-MOON-002  `moon_phase_firstquarter_lro_svs_nasa_e001863.jpg`
- source: https://images.nasa.gov/details/GSFC_20171208_Archive_e001863 (NASA GSFC Scientific Visualization Studio)
- licence: PUBLIC_DOMAIN_NASA Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio
- perspective: near-side disc, first quarter: right half lit, terminator running diagonally from upper centre to lower left
- what is visible: Right half of the disc is lit; the terminator is a slightly jagged, tilted line from the top centre-right down to the bottom-left (not vertical). Along the terminator, craters are shown with deep black shadows and bright rims, and relief is highly visible. Maria on the lit side are dark grey (large rounded one at the top right, a circular one at the right limb), highlands bright. The unlit left half is almost black but a faint dark-grey disc outline is still visible (earthshine-like rendering). The limb on the dark side is nearly invisible against black.
- influences: MOON_004 mask: hard-edged terminator, only a few pixels of softness (no atmosphere), slightly irregular because of relief; the terminator tilts as seen from Earth (here about 20-30 degrees from vertical). Unlit disc may carry a very faint dark-grey earthshine fill, not pure black, if the dark half should be seen.
- assets: MOON_004_GLOBE_SHADE MOON_001_GLOBE

## REF-MOON-003  `moon_phase_waxingcrescent_lro_svs_nasa_e001864.jpg`
- source: https://images.nasa.gov/details/GSFC_20171208_Archive_e001864 (NASA GSFC Scientific Visualization Studio)
- licence: PUBLIC_DOMAIN_NASA Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio
- perspective: near-side disc, waxing crescent; only a right-hand crescent lit
- what is visible: A thin lit crescent along the right edge, bounded on the left by a curved diagonal terminator. The dark disc is rendered as a nearly black, faint, round silhouette. The crescent shows pale grey highlands, a round dark mare at the right limb and a large darker region toward the centre, with craters and shadow-edged relief close to the terminator. - licence: PUBLIC_DOMAIN_NASA Credit: NASA
- perspective: oblique photo of the Moon's limb and terminator from the Orion window during the lunar flyby
- what is visible: A curved limb runs across the upper right with a black sky above. Most of the frame is the dark unlit Moon (barely distinguishable from the sky, but dark navy-black). - perspective: oblique LRO view of the central peak complex at lunar sunrise
- what is visible: A bright, steep, conical peak with long streaky downslope striations catches the light in the centre; an enormous perfectly black shadow of the summit falls toward the upper left; surrounding melt-floor terrain is hummocky grey with many black-floored hollows. ## REF-MOON-010  `moon_regolith_bootprint_apollo11_nasa_as11-40-5878.jpg`
- source: https://images.nasa.gov/details/as11-40-5878 (NASA / Apollo 11 crew)
- licence: PUBLIC_DOMAIN_NASA Credit: NASA
- perspective: close-up oblique macro of the soil (70 mm lunar surface camera per the caption)
- what is visible: Black-and-white close-up of grey fine-grained soil, covered with tiny pebbles and clods; a bootprint in the lower right shows crisp tread ridges, with sharp-walled edges and a deep black shadow filling the heel.
