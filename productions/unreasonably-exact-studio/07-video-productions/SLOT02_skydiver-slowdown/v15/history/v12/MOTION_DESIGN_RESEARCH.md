# V12 motion design research → decisions

Sources: `research/VISUAL_REFERENCE_NOTES.md` (HumanCraft guides and the local xkcd What If forensic audits; every principle there is cited by file and section) and `research/LIBRARY_REVIEW.md`.

| Principle found | Applied in V12 |
|---|---|
| Life comes from a new visual event about every 2 s, not constant wiggle; most frames are holds | Character motion cut to calm roll, bob and breathing. Purposeful events instead: arm lift, glance, blink, arrows drawing on, lens opening, trail marks arriving. |
| Diagrams build progressively; arrows grow over a few frames; nothing just fades in | `grow_arrow` trim-path arrows. Streamlines draw on line by line (staggered). Graph axes, then curve with a tracking point, then "top speed", then the "not zero" bracket. |
| Accumulate instead of replacing | Equal-time trail keeps every mark. Graph (Q1) returns in S1 and gains the alternative curves. |
| Comparisons by layout; change one variable at a time; lock the camera when comparing | J1: same body, same air, only speed changes (×1 → ×2), and drag follows ×k². The lens compares two lengths on one magnified scale. |
| Arrows must be to scale or marked schematic | Weight and drag share one scale (L = 118 units = weight). The lens says "×30" on its rim. Airflow is marked "schematic". The speed arrow is a separate dashed lane. |
| Minimal on-screen text; numbers on screen only when said | 39 V11 text items audited (`TEXT_REDUCTION_AUDIT.csv`). Brand, banners, footnotes and the inset caption removed. The only number shown is "10 km", which the narration says. |
| Negative space: most of the frame empty | Full-frame white cards removed. The graph and trail sit on softened sky, and the lens is about 15% of the frame. |
| Camera moves must have a reason | A1 dolly toward "you". B2 push toward the wrist the character looks at. C1 punch on the face for "Wait, what?". K1 push toward the tips, then the lens. R1 pull-back for "this is a model". O1 tilt to the ground for "the ground gets closer". |
| Foreground element crossing the lens about 1/3 of the time | A1 near clouds; streamline front pass in E1; wisps in tracking shots. |
| Humour sits on a reasoning chain that still works without the joke | D1: "Did gravity change its mind?" is answered by re-measuring the same arrow against a tick ("same"). "Did the air grow brakes?" gets a gust of streaks. P1: the deadpan push on the closed pack. |
| Endings are a button, not a summary | S1 ends on the curves splitting as the narration names other conditions, with a small end mark. |

**Not adopted:** impact shake (no impacts are depicted), and 3D engines (the brief favours keeping the 2D look).
