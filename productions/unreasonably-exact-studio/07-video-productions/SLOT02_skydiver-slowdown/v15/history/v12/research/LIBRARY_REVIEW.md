## Techniques worth adopting (Cairo re-implementations)

1. **Smootherstep camera glides of 18-40 frames, hard cut only for big jumps** (mromidev/claude-animated-shorts, SKILL.md "Camera"). `6t^5-15t^4+10t^3` on the camera matrix.
2. **No dead holds: a slow push of about 0.15-0.35 % scale per frame on every held shot, bigger on punch words** (mromidev/claude-animated-shorts). Keeps a still parachute-free fall from reading as a freeze.
3. **Impact kicks that flip sign each frame and decay over about 7 frames** (mromidev/claude-animated-shorts) — for the exit jump only, not during the fall.
4. **Foreground element passing near the lens in about 1/3 of shots** (mromidev/claude-animated-shorts) — e.g. a cloud wisp crossing the camera to sell parallax and speed.
5. **Ink/linework as a separate pass composited before blur or haze** (mromidev/claude-animated-shorts, "fixes") — keep arrows and labels sharp when the background gets atmospheric blur.
6. **Trim-path reveals on every arrow, streamline and the graph curve; strokes grow in drawing order, never fade in** (alexgreensh/anidoodle "It draws, then it moves"; storytold/effectcraft shape-layer trim paths). Cairo: walk the flattened path to arc-length `s*L`.
7. **Streamlines as trim paths with a moving offset** (storytold/effectcraft trim-path offset) — dash-offset animation on curved paths gives flowing air without particles.
8. **Reading-time rule for any on-screen text: hold letters/15 + 1.5 s after it lands; captions at least 1.8 s** (lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar DIRECTOR.md §7).
9. **Pace curve: one clear acceleration, one clear deceleration, one held breath** (lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar §7). The subject itself does this; let edit rhythm mirror the speed graph.
10. **At least four distinct camera moves and one signature shot; transitions built from the medium** (lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar §8) — e.g. the speed-graph line becoming the horizon, or a dial face match-cutting to the graph axis.
11. **One world-to-screen function shared by every overlay** so force arrows, labels and dial stay attached after camera moves (lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar §10 failures list).
12. **Blend poses through easing; never switch pose on an `if`** (lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar §9) — body-arch changes and arm micro-adjustments interpolate.
13. **Word-level timestamps to land reveals on spoken keywords** (openai/whisper `word_timestamps=True`; AgriciDaniel/claude-shorts boundary snapping to word ends/silences).
14. **Accumulating "semantic islands" instead of scene replacement** (hi-nikola/hand-drawn-explainer-video-nikola) — the graph panel stays on screen and gains each new phase rather than being redrawn.
15. **Deterministic frame = f(t), plus a delivery check: -14 LUFS, no black/NaN frames, text not cut by frame edge** (heygen-com/hyperframes "Deterministic"; kajisho5/ffmpeg-skill loudness; lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar §11).
