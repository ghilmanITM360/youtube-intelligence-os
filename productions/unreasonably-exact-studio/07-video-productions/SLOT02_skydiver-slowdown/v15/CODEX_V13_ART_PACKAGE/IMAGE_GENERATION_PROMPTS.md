# Image generation
Built-in image generation was used with transparent-background requests, followed by local registration, masks, cropping, and assembly. Originals are in sources/.

- Three-quarter: edit only both grey gloves to bare hands matching warm skin and dark outlines, preserve position and cream cuffs, make mouth neutral, preserve all else.
- Back: edit only both grey gloves to warm light skin bare hands, preserve five fingers, poses, cuffs, illustration and transparent background.
- Three elbow angles: edit the respective V12 rotated pose crop; repair the near elbow using compressed cream sleeve folds, close the upper notch and lower slit with continuous ink, no disc; preserve the pose and device.
- Neck: generate a fixed underlay of skin, cream collar, and dark shoulder harness behind the helmet, using the supplied crop and original illustration style.

The generated images altered surrounding artwork; only local areas were registered/masked into candidates. These image edits and their compositing do not establish acceptance.
