# Casually Finance — Channel Editing DNA (PRELIMINARY; statistics refreshed at n = 22 of 48 local videos; snapshot 2026-10-20)

Source: `EDITING_TIMING_STATISTICS.csv`, `MOTION_OPERATION_TAXONOMY.csv`, `evidence/pooled_motion_classes.json`, `evidence/measured_easing_summary.json`, per-video `videos/<id>/*`. **Denominator:** 22 validated Shorts (26.5 min, 45,019 native frames) of 48 local videos (10 long-form + 38 Shorts) of 210 public. All 22 are Shorts; no long-form has been analysed in this phase, so nothing below describes the long-form videos. Depth mix: 2 × A, 16 × B, 4 × C (327 `UNRESOLVED_CHANGE` events across 13 videos; 17-22 % of frames in `-Vr8JhCIv08`, `s3_3SFxtt14` and `mT2hOLrCYSk`, 13 % in `ud1ESmogrFY`). Selection was by public views and layout diversity, **not random**; every statement is a sample finding, not a channel-wide law. Evidence labels: `DIRECTLY_OBSERVED`, `MEASURED`, `ART_DIRECTION_INFERENCE`, `PLAUSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS`, `UNKNOWN`. The 16 videos of the earlier Oct-12 corpus (`codex/casually-finance-forensic-research`) are `REFERENCE_SUPPORTED` context and are not merged into these numbers.

## A. Patterns seen in all 22 validated videos
| Pattern | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **One-frame composition replacement is the dominant edit.** 423 `HARD_CUT_REPLACE` events (22/22 videos, plus one cut through a blank frame): every one swaps the whole stage between two consecutive frames, no fade, wipe or blend. | `MEASURED`: from ~7 % of the frame (sparse scenes) to >50 % (screenshot scenes) changes on one frame, on a mostly white canvas. 424 cuts of 1,348 events; 956 of 1,348 events last one frame. |
| **Cut rate 10-29 per minute, median 15.9.** Scene median 1.7-5.7 s (median of medians 3.1 s). | `MEASURED`, per-video table. Layout split (n = 22: 10 banded, 12 full-bleed): banded median 14.2/min (range 9.8-29.0), scene median 3.5 s, static share 77 %; full-bleed median 16.5/min (range 11.8-25.6), scene median 2.9 s, static 72 %; ranges overlap, so this is a tendency in a small sample, not a rule. The fastest videos are `Qof71Ef-3EU` (29.0/min) and `ud1ESmogrFY` (25.6/min, 105 table rows/min). |
| **Pops, not tweens, build explanations.** 216 `POP_IN` (20 videos) + 92 `OVERLAY_POP` (19 videos): props, numbers, X marks, bubbles and bills appear fully formed in one frame (2-4 frames when sequenced). | `MEASURED` counts; no scale/opacity easing visible at 30 fps sampling in the one-frame cases. |
| **A static canvas most of the time.** 74 % of all frames contain no stage change (range 50-92 % per video; pooled 74.1 %). | `MEASURED` frame-weighted; holds sit between authored events (QA check). The 50 % minimum (`YST8eMG3_Z8`) is caused by slow highlight growth and a scrolling viewer counted as motion, not by a higher event rate. |
| **Visuals meet or lead the voice.** Hard cuts precede the nearest ASR word onset by a median 73 ms (p10 -189, p90 +66; 76 % lead; 58 % within ±100 ms; n = 424 cuts in 22 videos); captions land on ASR onsets (median +20 to +53 ms and 64-83 % within ±100 ms in each of the 11 videos with a caption band). | `MEASURED`, ASR uncertainty ~100 ms; sign convention: negative = visual first. |
| **White canvas, hand-lettered text, flat vector props and characters.** | `DIRECTLY_OBSERVED` in 22/22 (counterexamples: dark quote cards, black frames, photographs, logos used as inserts). |
| **Source evidence inside the explanation.** Screenshots/charts/documents appear in 22/22 videos (see `ASSET_FAMILY_LIBRARY.csv`) with push-in, scroll, highlight or pop-on callouts. | `DIRECTLY_OBSERVED`. |

## B. Conditional patterns
- **Scroll/zoom of an evidence screenshot** (60 `CAMERA_SCROLL_ZOOM` events in 18 videos, 2,473 event-frames, the largest resolved motion budget): used when a document or chart must be read; precedes or accompanies a presenter pop-in. Of 18 fitted scale curves, 9 are **linear** (SUPPORTED; for example 1.69x over 54 frames, 1.91x over 35, 1.85x over 52), 5 **decelerate** (ease-out quad or cubic; for example `Qof71Ef-3EU` 1.00→1.43 in 1.7 s, `ji2qFfcgSB4` 2.07x over 31 frames, `ZDoeEd8fMdo` 1.89x over 56 frames), 3 are ambiguous and 1 ease-in fit is tentative (`MEASURED`; the pan channels agreed with the scale fit in the cases inspected). Both easings are used, so there is no single house curve.
- **Pointer arrows and characters that travel** (8 `POINTER_TRANSLATE` events in 5 videos): where fitted, the glide is linear on 7 of 10 x-curves (for example a red arrow across a bar chart over 73 frames, `_3tM1d-WeUI`), usually timed to the phrase being spoken.
- **Progressive stroke/line draw** (17 events in 8 videos): falling price lines and chart lines are drawn left to right; the measured draw (`7bm7XYssqlU`, f0-29) is **linear** at ~0.027 frame widths per frame; a red timeline in `xrNPr3YrWdw` grows monotonically over 86 frames while year labels pop.
- **Counting by sequential pops** (bills on a timeline, DEBT documents, buildings, houses, calendar icons): each unit appears on a keyword; 1 unit per 6-25 frames.
- **Highlight sweeps** over quoted text (35 events in 13 videos): 18 sweeps in 5 videos were measured by highlighter colour area (`scripts/measure_color_area.py`); all grow monotonically (monotone fraction 0.87-1.0) over 1.2-7.7 s; where checked against the ASR they sit on the phrase being read and finish near its end (one covers only the first part of its sentence). The gray stage-change detector misses these (a few pixels per frame), so they are found only with the colour measurement.
- **Callback repetition** of the same two scenes for a repeated sentence (`Qof71Ef-3EU`, 0 s and 26 s) or a running gag (microphone and '*crickets*' in `_3tM1d-WeUI`, stall composition twice in `zkeA5cH9nyM`).
- **Layout dependent features** (see D).

## C. Rare / specialised (more complex animation)
- **Money-rain overlay** (`mT2hOLrCYSk`): dozens of overlapping banknote layers change every frame for 85-115 frames, three times; **pixel-identical across the three segments** (first-frame correlation 1.000) so a single clip is reused, trimmed to the phrase (`MEASURED`). Not found in any other analysed video at that time (maximum correlation 0.61); not re-tested against the 10 later videos.
- **Looped overlays** (5 `OVERLAY_CLIP_LOOP` events in 3 videos: the three money-rain segments above, lightning bolts in `_3tM1d-WeUI` and sparkles around a logo bubble in `ud1ESmogrFY`): `PLAUSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS` that the last two are looped clips; no pixel-identity test.
- **Door swings** (2), **torn bond tag** (1), **knife rotation** (measured: -60° in 7 frames then +48° in 8 frames, two opposite easing directions in one gesture; `RB5zVghPor8`), **bag-character run cycle** (1).
- **Screen-recording-like inserts** (8 events in 3 videos: a hover tooltip in `Qof71Ef-3EU`; a PDF viewer with toolbar and thumbnail strip scrolling through a report in `YST8eMG3_Z8` and `xrNPr3YrWdw`): `PLAUSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS` that they are recordings; a keyframed tall screenshot cannot be excluded.
- **Opacity fade** (1): black circle to white, 22 frames, linear in 10.7 gray-level steps (`MEASURED`, `DEgQTSsSX9U`).

## D. Counterexamples and drifts
- **Layouts (sample, not strictly eras).** Banded layout = persistent headline + bounded stage + caption line: upper-header variant (headline at y 0.20-0.30; `Qof71Ef-3EU` 09-08, `DEgQTSsSX9U` 09-15 and six others dated 09-05..09-14), lower-header 720p variant (headline at y 0.24-0.37; `ZDoeEd8fMdo`, `_3tM1d-WeUI`, both 08-20). Full-bleed layout = no persistent headline, hand-lettered captions that move per scene (08-19, 08-21..08-28, 09-02, 09-19 onward); `ud1ESmogrFY` (08-19) starts with a headline and drops it at 8.1 s. Layout assignments come from automatic layout profiling plus viewing of the 22 validated videos; the other 26 local videos have not been analysed. The banded headline equals another local upload's public title in 2 of 10 banded videos (relation `UNKNOWN`). `DIRECTLY_OBSERVED`/`MEASURED`; sample only, not a creator-stated change.
- **Frame cadence.** Of 15 videos delivered at 30 fps, 10 show a measured 24-to-30 fps conform (duplicate frames repeating about every fifth frame, dated 08-19 to 09-15, among them the 08-19 to 08-21 720p Shorts) and 5 are unresolved for lack of motion or phase (`s3_3SFxtt14`, `zkeA5cH9nyM`, `ji2qFfcgSB4`, `YST8eMG3_Z8`, `UJEqqBpH18k`); the 6 videos dated 09-19 to 10-18 are native 24 fps and `RB5zVghPor8` (24 fps file) is unresolved. In several files the duplicate phase changes between scenes (e.g. 33 of 111 duplicates share the dominant phase in `_3tM1d-WeUI`). Author at the target's delivery rate and expect duplicates if you export 30 fps.
- **Sparse versus dense screens.** `44ggG2I6JPY` has only ~7 % of the frame changing at cuts (small objects on white) versus 25-60 % on screenshot-heavy Shorts, so any pixel-based cut threshold must be per-video.
- **No fade/dissolve or camera shake was found between scenes** in 22/22 (one-frame black flashes in `ud1ESmogrFY` are hard cuts); the only gradual effects are the zooms, strokes, highlights, arrow glides and one opacity fade above.

## Keyframe-compatible versus complex motion (duration-weighted; user hypothesis 70-80 % / 10-20 %)
Frame-weighted over all 45,019 frames (22 videos): static hold 74.1 %, plausibly conventional 2D keyframe/compositing 19.6 %, complex continuous animation 0.9 %, undetermined method 4.4 %, screen recording 0.9 %. The result **depends on how unresolved changes are treated** (`evidence/pooled_motion_classes.json`):
| Stratum | Videos | Moving frames | Conventional | Complex | Undetermined | Screen rec. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Depth A/B only (changes individually reviewed) | 18 | 9,838 | **86.7 %** | 1.2 % | 7.8 % | 4.3 % |
| All 22 (depth C counts unresolved changes as undetermined) | 22 | 11,639 | 75.9 % | 3.5 % | 17.0 % | 3.6 % |
Event counts (1,348): conventional 1,003, undetermined 330, complex 7, screen recording 8; **generated/video-based: none identified in any stratum.** Reading: where changes were actually reviewed, 87 % of moving time is ordinary keyframe/compositing; the only complex-animation signal is the reused money-rain overlay (`mT2hOLrCYSk`, depth C) plus doors, a bond tear, a run cycle and unresolved overlays. The owner's 70-80 % conventional figure is consistent with the all-22 total (75.9 %); the 10-20 % complex-or-generated share is **not** supported by the reviewed stratum (complex 1.2 %, even with screen-recording-like frames 5.5 %), and the unresolved 7.8-17.0 % cannot be assigned to either side. A final MP4 does not reveal the method; labels are appearance-based (`ART_DIRECTION_INFERENCE`).

## Confidence
Cut and pop timing: high (frame-exact, 22 videos). Easing: moderate for camera scale (18 fits: 9 linear, 5 ease-out, 3 ambiguous, 1 tentative), lower for pointer and stroke paths (10 x-fits: 7 linear). Asset reuse: moderate (analyst IDs). Audio: unlistened (`SOUND_DESIGN_SPEC.md`). Everything here must be re-run when more videos validate (`scripts/aggregate_channel.py`, `scripts/aggregate_audio.py`).
