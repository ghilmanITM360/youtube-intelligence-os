# Casually Finance — Channel Editing DNA (PRELIMINARY; statistics refreshed at n = 38 of 48 local videos; snapshot 2026-10-20 night)

Source: `EDITING_TIMING_STATISTICS.csv`, `MOTION_OPERATION_TAXONOMY.csv`, `evidence/pooled_motion_classes.json`, `evidence/measured_easing_summary.json`, `evidence/audio_aggregate.json`, per-video `videos/<id>/*`. **Denominator:** 38 validated Shorts (44.0 min, 74,035 native frames) of 48 local videos (10 long-form + 38 Shorts) of 210 public. All 38 are Shorts, i.e. every local Short is now covered at depth A, B or C; no long-form has been analysed in this phase, so nothing below describes the long-form videos. Depth mix: 2 × A, 32 × B, 4 × C (645 `UNRESOLVED_CHANGE` events in 28 videos; 17-22 % of frames in `-Vr8JhCIv08`, `s3_3SFxtt14` and `mT2hOLrCYSk`, 13 % in `ud1ESmogrFY`). Selection was by public views and layout diversity, **not random**; every statement is a sample finding, not a channel-wide law. Evidence labels: `DIRECTLY_OBSERVED`, `MEASURED`, `ART_DIRECTION_INFERENCE`, `PLAUSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS`, `UNKNOWN`. The 16 videos of the earlier Oct-12 corpus (`codex/casually-finance-forensic-research`) are `REFERENCE_SUPPORTED` context and are not merged into these numbers. Reviewer: one agent, no second reviewer, no listening.

## B. Conditional patterns
- **Scroll/zoom of an evidence screenshot** (100 `CAMERA_SCROLL_ZOOM` events in 30 videos, 3,938 event-frames, the largest resolved motion budget after highlight sweeps): used when a document or chart must be read; precedes or accompanies a presenter pop-in. Measured scale changes run 1.3-2.0x over 16-63 frames (for example 1.96x over 27 frames, 1.72x over 63, 1.60x over 31, 1.85x over 43, 1.55x over 30, 1.67x over 16; newer: 1.38x over 23 frames on a chart in `6S3PzAUFnXQ`, and a 126-frame whole-composition push-in of x1.61 in `PxgCsLSf-w4`, the longest continuous camera move measured so far). **Easing, corrected at n = 38:** of 31 camera moves with a net scale change of at least 5 %, 21 have a SUPPORTED scale curve: 13 **linear** and 8 **decelerating** (5 ease-out quad, 3 ease-out cubic), none accelerating or ease-in-out; 8 are ambiguous, 1 low-sample and 1 unresolved (`MEASURED`). The earlier tally (29 of 37 supported, including 5 ease-in and 3 ease-in-out) is **withdrawn**: all eight ease-in and ease-in-out scale fits belonged to pans whose net scale changed by only 0.9925-1.0278, where the scale channel is noise. For horizontal shifts of at least 20 px (analysis size), 31 of 38 moves have a SUPPORTED pan curve: 18 linear, 6 ease-in-out, 6 ease-out, 1 ease-in (5 ambiguous, 2 unresolved). Several moves are two or three stages on one image (a 1.96x zoom, a 170 px pan over 57 frames, a 165 px pan back over 15 frames in `bF9WVzzS4ds`). All easings are used for pans, so there is no single house curve; linear is the most common single choice (13 of 21 supported scale fits, 62 %; 18 of 31 supported pans, 58 %).
- **Read-along page pans with a highlighter** (`2EZzwupIa80`, 8 constant-velocity passes; `YGC3ao005as` 74-frame quote scroll twice): a text page scrolls left at a **constant per-frame step** (-2.0 to -4.7 px per frame at the 180-px analysis size, i.e. -34 to -138 px per line) for 15-69 frames while a yellow highlight follows the line, then returns to the line start at a faster constant speed in 5-11 frames (+4.7 to +21.3 px per frame); the pass starts 0.12-0.38 s before the first narrated word of the line and ends with it (`MEASURED`; step sizes from per-frame tracker output, `motion/camera_*.json`).
- **Pointer arrows and characters that travel** (10 `POINTER_TRANSLATE` events in 7 videos): where fitted, the glide is linear on 8 of 11 x-curves (for example a red arrow along a yield curve over 87 frames from (0.17,0.49) to (0.80,0.42), `06pN9dDvVVU`; a red arrow across a bar chart over 73 frames, `_3tM1d-WeUI`), usually timed to the phrase being spoken.
- **Progressive stroke/line draw** (30 `STROKE_REVEAL` events in 17 videos): falling price lines, arrows, circles and row underlines are drawn left to right; measured draws are **linear** (`7bm7XYssqlU` f0-29 at ~0.027 frame widths per frame; a red down arrow over 36 frames, 123 to 2,174 red px, `bF9WVzzS4ds`); a red timeline in `xrNPr3YrWdw` grows monotonically over 86 frames while year labels pop; chart lines are drawn in over 34 frames (ease-in-out, `SUPPORTED`) and ~41 frames (linear, ambiguous) in `rSa2aziEsAI`, and red boxes are drawn in two strokes over 38 frames (red area 0 to 311 px).
- **Counting by sequential pops** (bills on a timeline, DEBT documents, buildings, houses, calendar icons, bond papers): each unit appears on a keyword; 1 unit per 5-25 frames (bond papers at f882, 888, 893, 899, 905 in `Kq5VMugQMHw`; three calendar icons on 'about three weeks'; yen notes at gaps of 4-5 frames and bank buildings at 10-frame gaps in `rSa2aziEsAI`; four moneybags at gaps of 4, 3 and 4 frames ending 30 ms after 'growth' in `PxgCsLSf-w4`).
- **Highlight sweeps** over quoted text (60 events in 22 videos): sweeps were measured by highlighter colour area (`scripts/measure_color_area.py`) in 11 videos at n = 32 and in the later Shorts; they last 0.4-8.7 s (the longest, 209 frames, is three lines of a WSJ page in `2EZzwupIa80`); where checked against the ASR they start on the first word of the quoted sentence and finish near its end (for example a yellow highlight from 30 ms before 'Stan's' to the end of the quote in `m8NC4L8hmeE`, the highlighter growing while the quote text pans 185 px; from ASR 'doubling' to 'buyback' in `YGC3ao005as`; 0.12 s before 'I' to the end of 'makers.' in `2EZzwupIa80`). The gray stage-change detector misses them (a few pixels per frame), so they are found only with the colour measurement.
- **Slide-ins and rises** (75 `SLIDE_IN` events in 21 videos, 34 `CHARACTER_RISE_IN` in 20): cards, notes, news pages and characters enter from an edge over 9-60 frames (portraits in `H3PRCteue9Y` over 15-33 frames); where fitted the pan is ease-in-out or linear.
- **Prop drifts** (64 `PROP_DRIFT` events in 19 videos, 1,688 event-frames): bags, papers and notes glide between objects over 6-60 frames; paths were not tracked.
- **Scale tweens** (38 events in 22 videos): a building, photo, coin, map character or box grows over 23-145 frames (the Fed building grows over 46-48 frames in `bF9WVzzS4ds` and `gGNVpOo7BK8`, ink area linear in one and ease-out cubic in the other; a black box grows linearly over 145 frames in `ODMyV3F_G2g`; a percent coin grows x2.2 in 23 frames in both `PxgCsLSf-w4` and `YGC3ao005as`, orange area 439-446 to 2,345-2,349 px, Pearson r = 0.986; a map character grows over 26 frames at f556-581 and f735-760 of `6S3PzAUFnXQ` with r = 0.957 between the two changed-pixel series).
- **Callback repetition** of the same two scenes for a repeated sentence (`Qof71Ef-3EU`, 0 s and 26 s) or a running gag (microphone and '*crickets*' in `_3tM1d-WeUI`, stall composition twice in `zkeA5cH9nyM` and in `JztI-l-0-x4`).
- **Title/time cards that sit on the pun word**: a green palm-leaf 'TWO WEEKS LATER' card shows f175-219 (5.83-7.30 s) in `bF9WVzzS4ds` while ASR 'later' is spoken at 6.92 s, and appears 1.1 s before ASR 'later' (24.48 s) in `gGNVpOo7BK8`; a gold 'THE NEXT DAY' card appears 0.15 s before ASR 'Then' and goes after 'day' (`MEASURED`).
- **Low-frame-rate clip inserts** (9 `VIDEO_CLIP_INSERT` events in 8 videos): a third-party clip (a shop-counter clip in `bF9WVzzS4ds` and `gGNVpOo7BK8`, a cartoon-sponge meme in `JztI-l-0-x4`, a movie still in `06pN9dDvVVU`, a dog clip in `m8NC4L8hmeE`) whose region changes about every third frame (10 updates per second), alternating 2- and 3-frame gaps on the 24 fps file; held static after its last update (`MEASURED`; origin of the clips `UNKNOWN`). The latest Shorts add a monitor clip that updates on 32 of 64 frames at irregular 1-5-frame gaps (~12 per second, `6S3PzAUFnXQ`), a reality-TV meme clip at 15 pictures per second (2-frame gaps on a 30 fps file, `rSa2aziEsAI`) and a heart-shaped box opening at ~10 updates per second (`JsK53moCyZA`); a starburst alternating between two shapes every 4 frames (6 pictures per second, `rSa2aziEsAI`) shows the same reduced-rate look.
- **Layout dependent features** (see D).

## C. Rare / specialised (more complex animation)
- **Money-rain overlay** (`mT2hOLrCYSk`): dozens of overlapping banknote layers change every frame for 85-115 frames, three times; **pixel-identical across the three segments** (first-frame correlation 1.000) so a single clip is reused, trimmed to the phrase (`MEASURED`). Not found in any other analysed video at that time (maximum correlation 0.61); not re-tested against the 26 later videos.
- **Looped overlays and loops** (6 `OVERLAY_CLIP_LOOP` events in 4 videos; 9 `CHARACTER_LOOP_ANIMATION` events in 9 videos: periodic pose changes every 14 frames of a lectern caricature in `JztI-l-0-x4`, a dark 'monster' scene changing every frame for ~43 frames in `Kq5VMugQMHw`; a similar red-vignette monster scene of 118 frames and a 76-frame paper-pile scene (21-33 k changed px per frame, 37-58 % of the frame) in `rSa2aziEsAI`, 10.7 % of that video's frames; a character that swaps poses exactly every 10 frames 22 times in `JsK53moCyZA`): `PLAUSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS` that they are looped clips; a frame-correlation test of the red-vignette scene against `Kq5VMugQMHw` gave <= 0.50, so the two compositions are not the same frames.
- **Turning rings** (10 `ROTATION` events in 7 videos): a ring of three red arrows changes every frame for 97 and 36 frames in `-WJDfT-MwmY` and for ~115 frames in `Kq5VMugQMHw`, popped in and removed in one frame in the first (~550-570 changed px per frame); rotation versus a looped clip not resolved.
- **Door swings** (2), **torn bond tag** (1), **knife rotation** (measured: -60° in 7 frames then +48° in 8 frames, two opposite easing directions in one gesture; `RB5zVghPor8`), **bag-character run cycle** (1).
- **Screen-recording-like inserts** (9 events in 4 videos: a hover tooltip in `Qof71Ef-3EU`; a PDF viewer with toolbar and thumbnail strip scrolling through a report in `YST8eMG3_Z8` and `xrNPr3YrWdw`; an interactive FRED GDP chart with a cursor that scrolls left ~215 px over 92 frames with hover tooltips every 22-24 frames in `6S3PzAUFnXQ`, updating on every frame at 24 fps): `PLAUSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS` that they are recordings; a keyframed tall screenshot cannot be excluded.
- **Opacity fades** (7 events in 7 videos): a black '?' disc that turns grey and then light over ~26 frames to reveal a definition card (`PxgCsLSf-w4`); black circle to white, 22 frames, linear in 10.7 gray-level steps (`DEgQTSsSX9U`); a black box fades into a photo over 25 frames at a constant ~2.4 gray levels per frame (`ODMyV3F_G2g`); a glowing red card fades to white over 20 frames (`Kq5VMugQMHw`) (`MEASURED`).
- **Typed-on text** (8 `TEXT_WRITE_ON` events in 8 videos): '48 hours later...' appears in steps every ~3 frames (7 steps over f720-739 after a cut that lands mid-typing, `bF9WVzzS4ds`); a red hand-lettered phrase is written in over 5 frames, ending 50 ms before its first word (`H3PRCteue9Y`).

## D. Counterexamples and drifts
- **Layouts (sample, not strictly eras).** Banded layout = persistent headline + bounded stage + caption line: upper-header variant (headline at y 0.20-0.30; `Qof71Ef-3EU` 09-08, `DEgQTSsSX9U` 09-15 and others dated 09-03..09-21), lower-header 720p variant (headline at y 0.24-0.37; `ZDoeEd8fMdo`, `_3tM1d-WeUI`, both 08-20). Full-bleed layout = no persistent headline, hand-lettered captions that move per scene (08-19, 08-21..08-28, 09-02, 09-19 onward). **Five videos drop their headline part-way through**: `ud1ESmogrFY` (8.1 s), `gGNVpOo7BK8` (13.5 s), `06pN9dDvVVU` (16.3 s), `f_ZCoPKLpyU` (19.3 s) and `JztI-l-0-x4` (27.3 s). `JsK53moCyZA` (2026-06-01, the oldest upload analysed) uses a **small centred stage**: every change happens in rows 0.366-0.722 of the frame, no headline, a small caption at the lower edge of that band; no other analysed video does this and the other June Shorts are not profiled. Layout assignments come from automatic layout profiling plus viewing of the 38 validated videos; the 10 long-form local videos have not been analysed. The banded headline equals another local upload's public title in 2 of 13 banded videos (relation `UNKNOWN`; the headline of `JztI-l-0-x4` paraphrases its own title, those of `06pN9dDvVVU` and `gGNVpOo7BK8` match no local title). `DIRECTLY_OBSERVED`/`MEASURED`; sample only, not a creator-stated change.
- **Frame cadence.** Of 25 videos delivered at 30 fps, 12 show a measured 24-to-30 fps conform (duplicate frames repeating about every fifth frame; among them `06pN9dDvVVU` and the 2026-06-01 `JsK53moCyZA`) and 13 are unresolved for lack of motion or phase (including `rSa2aziEsAI`); of 13 videos delivered at 24 fps, 8 are native and 5 are unresolved (`RB5zVghPor8`, `m8NC4L8hmeE`, `6S3PzAUFnXQ`, `PxgCsLSf-w4`, `YGC3ao005as`). In several files the duplicate phase changes between scenes (e.g. 33 of 111 duplicates share the dominant phase in `_3tM1d-WeUI`). Author at the target's delivery rate and expect duplicates if you export 30 fps.
- **Sparse versus dense screens.** `44ggG2I6JPY` has only ~7 % of the frame changing at cuts (small objects on white) and `JsK53moCyZA` 2-11 % (small centred stage; no frame reaches 20 %) versus 25-60 % on screenshot-heavy Shorts (up to 70 % in `6S3PzAUFnXQ`), so any pixel-based cut threshold must be per-video.
- **No fade/dissolve or camera shake was found between scenes** in 38/38 (one-frame black flashes in `ud1ESmogrFY` are hard cuts); the only gradual effects are the zooms, strokes, highlights, arrow glides and the opacity fades above.
- - **Caption-first cuts.** In some Shorts the caption block is cleared exactly one frame (33-42 ms) before the new picture appears: 3 of 19 cuts in `6S3PzAUFnXQ`, 2 of 17 in `PxgCsLSf-w4`, 6 of 10 in `JsK53moCyZA` (one of them 2 frames early), none in `2EZzwupIa80` or `rSa2aziEsAI`; the other cuts change caption and picture in the same frame (`MEASURED`; earlier videos not tested).
- **Script and kit reuse across Shorts.** Pairs with identical ASR runs: `bF9WVzzS4ds` and `gGNVpOo7BK8` (39 words; same props, different layouts), `-WJDfT-MwmY`: none; `Kq5VMugQMHw` and `f_ZCoPKLpyU` (16 words), `JztI-l-0-x4` and `zkeA5cH9nyM` (15 words), `PxgCsLSf-w4` and `YGC3ao005as` (13 words, the same CNBC card, coin and bond paper, and the matching coin growth above; frame correlation <= 0.11, so compositions differ), `PxgCsLSf-w4` and `-Vr8JhCIv08` (14 words, the '$40 trillion' sentence), `2EZzwupIa80` and `m8NC4L8hmeE` (18 words, Druckenmiller photo and LinkedIn card at correlation 0.80, not identical), `rSa2aziEsAI` and `f_ZCoPKLpyU` (18 words, cliff scene correlation 0.57), plus the earlier `NgryCruZegs`/`Qof71Ef-3EU` and `xrNPr3YrWdw`/`44ggG2I6JPY` pairs (`MEASURED` on ASR text with difflib; frame-level reuse tested only for the earlier pairs).

## Keyframe-compatible versus complex motion (duration-weighted; user hypothesis 70-80 % / 10-20 %)
Frame-weighted over all 74,035 frames (38 videos): static hold 71.8 %, plausibly conventional 2D keyframe/compositing 22.5 %, complex continuous animation 1.0 %, undetermined method 4.0 %, screen recording 0.7 %. The result **depends on how unresolved changes are treated** (`evidence/pooled_motion_classes.json`):
| Stratum | Videos | Moving frames | Conventional | Complex | Undetermined | Screen rec. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Depth A/B only (changes individually reviewed) | 34 | 19,072 | **85.7 %** | 2.2 % | 9.3 % | 2.8 % |
| All 38 (depth C counts unresolved changes as undetermined) | 38 | 20,873 | 79.8 % | 3.4 % | 14.2 % | 2.6 % |
Event counts (2,495): conventional 1,808, undetermined 666, complex 12, screen recording 9; **generated/video-based: none identified in any stratum.** Reading: where changes were actually reviewed, 85.7 % of moving time is ordinary keyframe/compositing; the complex-animation signals are the reused money-rain overlay (`mT2hOLrCYSk`, depth C), the two continuous high-motion scenes of `rSa2aziEsAI` (194 frames), doors, a bond tear, a run cycle, looped characters and unresolved overlays. The owner's 70-80 % conventional figure is consistent with the all-38 total (79.8 %); the 10-20 % complex-or-generated share is **not** supported by the reviewed stratum (complex 2.2 %, even with screen-recording-like frames 5.0 %), and the unresolved 9.3-14.2 % cannot be assigned to either side. A final MP4 does not reveal the method; labels are appearance-based (`ART_DIRECTION_INFERENCE`).

## Confidence
Cut and pop timing: high (frame-exact, 38 videos). Easing: moderate for camera scale (31 fits with a real zoom, 21 supported: 13 linear, 8 ease-out) and for pans (38 fits, 31 supported: 18 linear, 6 ease-in-out, 6 ease-out, 1 ease-in), lower for pointer and stroke paths (11 x-fits: 8 linear, 3 other). Asset reuse: moderate (analyst IDs). Audio: unlistened (`SOUND_DESIGN_SPEC.md`). Everything here must be re-run when more videos validate (`scripts/aggregate_channel.py`, `scripts/aggregate_audio.py`).
