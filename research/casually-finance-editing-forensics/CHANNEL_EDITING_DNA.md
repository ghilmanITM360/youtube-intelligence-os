# Casually Finance — Channel Editing DNA (PRELIMINARY; statistics refreshed at n = 32 of 48 local videos; snapshot 2026-10-20 evening)

Source: `EDITING_TIMING_STATISTICS.csv`, `MOTION_OPERATION_TAXONOMY.csv`, `evidence/pooled_motion_classes.json`, `evidence/measured_easing_summary.json`, `evidence/audio_aggregate.json`, per-video `videos/<id>/*`. **Denominator:** 32 validated Shorts (37.7 min, 64,400 native frames) of 48 local videos (10 long-form + 38 Shorts) of 210 public. All 32 are Shorts; no long-form has been analysed in this phase, so nothing below describes the long-form videos. Depth mix: 2 × A, 26 × B, 4 × C (500 `UNRESOLVED_CHANGE` events in 22 videos; 17-22 % of frames in `-Vr8JhCIv08`, `s3_3SFxtt14` and `mT2hOLrCYSk`, 13 % in `ud1ESmogrFY`). Selection was by public views and layout diversity, **not random**; every statement is a sample finding, not a channel-wide law. Evidence labels: `DIRECTLY_OBSERVED`, `MEASURED`, `ART_DIRECTION_INFERENCE`, `PLAUSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS`, `UNKNOWN`. The 16 videos of the earlier Oct-12 corpus (`codex/casually-finance-forensic-research`) are `REFERENCE_SUPPORTED` context and are not merged into these numbers. Reviewer: one agent, no second reviewer, no listening.

## A. Patterns seen in all 32 validated videos
| Pattern | Evidence |

## B. Conditional patterns
- **Scroll/zoom of an evidence screenshot** (79 `CAMERA_SCROLL_ZOOM` events in 25 videos, 3,199 event-frames, the largest resolved motion budget): used when a document or chart must be read; precedes or accompanies a presenter pop-in. Measured scale changes run 1.3-2.0x over 16-63 frames (for example 1.96x over 27 frames, 1.72x over 63, 1.60x over 31, 1.85x over 43, 1.55x over 30, 1.67x over 16). Of 37 fitted scale curves, 29 are SUPPORTED: 13 **linear**, 8 **decelerating** (ease-out quad/cubic), 5 **accelerating** (ease-in quad), 3 **ease-in-out**; 6 are ambiguous and 2 low-sample (`MEASURED`; the pan channels agreed with the scale fit in the cases inspected). Several moves are two or three stages on one image (a 1.96x zoom, a 170 px pan over 57 frames, a 165 px pan back over 15 frames in `bF9WVzzS4ds`). All easings are used, so there is no single house curve; linear is the most common single choice (45 % of supported fits).
- **Pointer arrows and characters that travel** (10 `POINTER_TRANSLATE` events in 7 videos): where fitted, the glide is linear on 8 of 11 x-curves (for example a red arrow along a yield curve over 87 frames from (0.17,0.49) to (0.80,0.42), `06pN9dDvVVU`; a red arrow across a bar chart over 73 frames, `_3tM1d-WeUI`), usually timed to the phrase being spoken.
- **Progressive stroke/line draw** (23 `STROKE_REVEAL` events in 12 videos): falling price lines, arrows, circles and row underlines are drawn left to right; measured draws are **linear** (`7bm7XYssqlU` f0-29 at ~0.027 frame widths per frame; a red down arrow over 36 frames, 123 to 2,174 red px, `bF9WVzzS4ds`); a red timeline in `xrNPr3YrWdw` grows monotonically over 86 frames while year labels pop.
- **Counting by sequential pops** (bills on a timeline, DEBT documents, buildings, houses, calendar icons, bond papers): each unit appears on a keyword; 1 unit per 5-25 frames (bond papers at f882, 888, 893, 899, 905 in `Kq5VMugQMHw`; three calendar icons on 'about three weeks').
- **Highlight sweeps** over quoted text (49 events in 19 videos): 31 sweeps in 11 videos were measured by highlighter colour area (`scripts/measure_color_area.py`); they last 0.5-7.7 s; where checked against the ASR they start on the first word of the quoted sentence and finish near its end (for example a yellow highlight from 30 ms before 'Stan's' to the end of the quote in `m8NC4L8hmeE`; the highlighter grows while the quote text pans 185 px). The gray stage-change detector misses them (a few pixels per frame), so they are found only with the colour measurement.
- **Slide-ins and rises** (47 `SLIDE_IN` events in 17 videos, 24 `CHARACTER_RISE_IN` in 14): cards, notes, news pages and characters enter from an edge over 9-60 frames (portraits in `H3PRCteue9Y` over 15-33 frames); where fitted the pan is ease-in-out or linear.
- **Prop drifts** (48 `PROP_DRIFT` events in 15 videos, 1,327 event-frames): bags, papers and notes glide between objects over 6-60 frames; paths were not tracked.
- **Scale tweens** (32 events in 18 videos): a building, photo or box grows over 46-145 frames (the Fed building grows over 46-48 frames in `bF9WVzzS4ds` and `gGNVpOo7BK8`, ink area linear in one and ease-out cubic in the other; a black box grows linearly over 145 frames in `ODMyV3F_G2g`).
- **Callback repetition** of the same two scenes for a repeated sentence (`Qof71Ef-3EU`, 0 s and 26 s) or a running gag (microphone and '*crickets*' in `_3tM1d-WeUI`, stall composition twice in `zkeA5cH9nyM` and in `JztI-l-0-x4`).
- **Title/time cards that sit on the pun word**: a green palm-leaf 'TWO WEEKS LATER' card shows f175-219 (5.83-7.30 s) in `bF9WVzzS4ds` while ASR 'later' is spoken at 6.92 s, and appears 1.1 s before ASR 'later' (24.48 s) in `gGNVpOo7BK8`; a gold 'THE NEXT DAY' card appears 0.15 s before ASR 'Then' and goes after 'day' (`MEASURED`).
- **Low-frame-rate clip inserts** (6 `VIDEO_CLIP_INSERT` events in 5 videos): a third-party clip (a shop-counter clip in `bF9WVzzS4ds` and `gGNVpOo7BK8`, a cartoon-sponge meme in `JztI-l-0-x4`, a movie still in `06pN9dDvVVU`, a dog clip in `m8NC4L8hmeE`) whose region changes about every third frame (10 updates per second), alternating 2- and 3-frame gaps on the 24 fps file; held static after its last update (`MEASURED`; origin of the clips `UNKNOWN`).
- **Layout dependent features** (see D).

## C. Rare / specialised (more complex animation)
- **Money-rain overlay** (`mT2hOLrCYSk`): dozens of overlapping banknote layers change every frame for 85-115 frames, three times; **pixel-identical across the three segments** (first-frame correlation 1.000) so a single clip is reused, trimmed to the phrase (`MEASURED`). Not found in any other analysed video at that time (maximum correlation 0.61); not re-tested against the 20 later videos.
- **Looped overlays and loops** (5 `OVERLAY_CLIP_LOOP` events in 3 videos; 6 `CHARACTER_LOOP_ANIMATION` events in 6 videos: periodic pose changes every 14 frames of a lectern caricature in `JztI-l-0-x4`, a dark 'monster' scene changing every frame for ~43 frames in `Kq5VMugQMHw`): `PLAUSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS` that they are looped clips; no pixel-identity test.
- **Turning rings** (10 `ROTATION` events in 7 videos): a ring of three red arrows changes every frame for 97 and 36 frames in `-WJDfT-MwmY` and for ~115 frames in `Kq5VMugQMHw`, popped in and removed in one frame in the first (~550-570 changed px per frame); rotation versus a looped clip not resolved.
- **Door swings** (2), **torn bond tag** (1), **knife rotation** (measured: -60° in 7 frames then +48° in 8 frames, two opposite easing directions in one gesture; `RB5zVghPor8`), **bag-character run cycle** (1).
- **Screen-recording-like inserts** (8 events in 3 videos: a hover tooltip in `Qof71Ef-3EU`; a PDF viewer with toolbar and thumbnail strip scrolling through a report in `YST8eMG3_Z8` and `xrNPr3YrWdw`): `PLAUSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS` that they are recordings; a keyframed tall screenshot cannot be excluded.
- **Opacity fades** (6 events in 6 videos): black circle to white, 22 frames, linear in 10.7 gray-level steps (`DEgQTSsSX9U`); a black box fades into a photo over 25 frames at a constant ~2.4 gray levels per frame (`ODMyV3F_G2g`); a glowing red card fades to white over 20 frames (`Kq5VMugQMHw`) (`MEASURED`).
- **Typed-on text** (8 `TEXT_WRITE_ON` events in 8 videos): '48 hours later...' appears in steps every ~3 frames (7 steps over f720-739 after a cut that lands mid-typing, `bF9WVzzS4ds`); a red hand-lettered phrase is written in over 5 frames, ending 50 ms before its first word (`H3PRCteue9Y`).

## D. Counterexamples and drifts
- **Layouts (sample, not strictly eras).** Banded layout = persistent headline + bounded stage + caption line: upper-header variant (headline at y 0.20-0.30; `Qof71Ef-3EU` 09-08, `DEgQTSsSX9U` 09-15 and others dated 09-03..09-21), lower-header 720p variant (headline at y 0.24-0.37; `ZDoeEd8fMdo`, `_3tM1d-WeUI`, both 08-20). Full-bleed layout = no persistent headline, hand-lettered captions that move per scene (08-19, 08-21..08-28, 09-02, 09-19 onward). **Five videos drop their headline part-way through**: `ud1ESmogrFY` (8.1 s), `gGNVpOo7BK8` (13.5 s), `06pN9dDvVVU` (16.3 s), `f_ZCoPKLpyU` (19.3 s) and `JztI-l-0-x4` (27.3 s). Layout assignments come from automatic layout profiling plus viewing of the 32 validated videos; the other 16 local videos have not been analysed. The banded headline equals another local upload's public title in 2 of 13 banded videos (relation `UNKNOWN`; the headline of `JztI-l-0-x4` paraphrases its own title, those of `06pN9dDvVVU` and `gGNVpOo7BK8` match no local title). `DIRECTLY_OBSERVED`/`MEASURED`; sample only, not a creator-stated change.
- **Frame cadence.** Of 23 videos delivered at 30 fps, 11 show a measured 24-to-30 fps conform (duplicate frames repeating about every fifth frame; among them `06pN9dDvVVU`) and 12 are unresolved for lack of motion or phase; of 9 videos delivered at 24 fps, 7 are native and 2 are unresolved (`RB5zVghPor8`, one more). In several files the duplicate phase changes between scenes (e.g. 33 of 111 duplicates share the dominant phase in `_3tM1d-WeUI`). Author at the target's delivery rate and expect duplicates if you export 30 fps.
- **Sparse versus dense screens.** `44ggG2I6JPY` has only ~7 % of the frame changing at cuts (small objects on white) versus 25-60 % on screenshot-heavy Shorts, so any pixel-based cut threshold must be per-video.
- **No fade/dissolve or camera shake was found between scenes** in 32/32 (one-frame black flashes in `ud1ESmogrFY` are hard cuts); the only gradual effects are the zooms, strokes, highlights, arrow glides and the opacity fades above.
- **Script and kit reuse across Shorts.** Pairs with identical ASR runs: `bF9WVzzS4ds` and `gGNVpOo7BK8` (39 words; same props, different layouts), `-WJDfT-MwmY`: none; `Kq5VMugQMHw` and `f_ZCoPKLpyU` (16 words), `JztI-l-0-x4` and `zkeA5cH9nyM` (15 words), plus the earlier `NgryCruZegs`/`Qof71Ef-3EU` and `xrNPr3YrWdw`/`44ggG2I6JPY` pairs (`MEASURED` on ASR text with difflib; frame-level reuse tested only for the earlier pairs).

## Keyframe-compatible versus complex motion (duration-weighted; user hypothesis 70-80 % / 10-20 %)
Frame-weighted over all 64,400 frames (32 videos): static hold 73.6 %, plausibly conventional 2D keyframe/compositing 20.8 %, complex continuous animation 0.6 %, undetermined method 4.3 %, screen recording 0.7 %. The result **depends on how unresolved changes are treated** (`evidence/pooled_motion_classes.json`):
| Stratum | Videos | Moving frames | Conventional | Complex | Undetermined | Screen rec. |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Depth A/B only (changes individually reviewed) | 28 | 15,209 | **86.0 %** | 0.8 % | 10.4 % | 2.8 % |
| All 32 (depth C counts unresolved changes as undetermined) | 32 | 17,010 | 78.7 % | 2.4 % | 16.4 % | 2.5 % |
Event counts (2,020): conventional 1,489, undetermined 516, complex 7, screen recording 8; **generated/video-based: none identified in any stratum.** Reading: where changes were actually reviewed, 86 % of moving time is ordinary keyframe/compositing; the only complex-animation signals are the reused money-rain overlay (`mT2hOLrCYSk`, depth C) plus doors, a bond tear, a run cycle, looped characters and unresolved overlays. The owner's 70-80 % conventional figure is consistent with the all-32 total (78.7 %); the 10-20 % complex-or-generated share is **not** supported by the reviewed stratum (complex 0.8 %, even with screen-recording-like frames 3.6 %), and the unresolved 10.4-16.4 % cannot be assigned to either side. A final MP4 does not reveal the method; labels are appearance-based (`ART_DIRECTION_INFERENCE`).

## Confidence
Cut and pop timing: high (frame-exact, 32 videos). Easing: moderate for camera scale (37 fits, 29 supported: 13 linear, 8 ease-out, 5 ease-in, 3 ease-in-out), lower for pointer and stroke paths (11 x-fits: 8 linear, 3 other). Asset reuse: moderate (analyst IDs). Audio: unlistened (`SOUND_DESIGN_SPEC.md`). Everything here must be re-run when more videos validate (`scripts/aggregate_channel.py`, `scripts/aggregate_audio.py`).
