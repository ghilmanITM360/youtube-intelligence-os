# Casually Finance — Visual Storytelling DNA (PRELIMINARY, n = 10 Shorts of 48 local; narration = unlistened ASR)

Sources: each video's `STORY_STRUCTURE.md`, `NARRATION_VISUAL_ALIGNMENT.csv`, `SCENE_TIMELINE.csv`. Narration phrases come from Whisper small.en (names and numbers uncertified; on-screen text is the better source). "Purpose" statements are `ART_DIRECTION_INFERENCE` unless the creator said it (no creator statement was found).

## Recurring explanation shapes (observed in the 10 videos)
1. **Claim → source → consequence → callback.** Open with a claim (often a number), put the cited document or chart on screen, show the consequence with props, then repeat the opening image on the repeated sentence (`Qof71Ef-3EU` S01-S02 reused at S11-S12; `44ggG2I6JPY` returns to its country ensemble at S26).
2. **Expectation set up, then denied** (`Es5c2MKzbDg`: 'you'd think prices fall' → red falling line → 'except they haven't' + red word; `HvnVO1_YrMQ`: 'Target One clears, right up until you check where the bar was set'; `7IMFHclDn8k`: patience vs 'zero sense'). The reversal sentence gets a red hand-lettered word or a green check that is then undermined.
3. **Progressive additive diagrams.** Units are added one at a time as the narration names them: bills on a bond timeline (`DEgQTSsSX9U`, `Qof71Ef-3EU`), DEBT documents (`Es5c2MKzbDg`), buildings (`7bm7XYssqlU`), barrels and gas cylinders (`HvnVO1_YrMQ`). The previous object stays; the new object pops on its keyword.
4. **Metaphor objects enact the verb.** A knife swings for 'cut' (`RB5zVghPor8`), a door swings for 'door number two', a bond is torn for 'cut in half' (`DEgQTSsSX9U`), documents travel to a bank for 'handing cheap debt back' (`ffIiiPLDFdM`), a '?' circle fades for 'has now disappeared'.
5. **Countries and institutions as characters.** Green country-map characters (8 of 10 videos) carry eyes and legs and speak in bubbles; the Earth is a character in 3; an official figure (grey hair, green tie) recurs in 3 videos with quote bubbles.
6. **Evidence-then-reaction.** A screenshot or chart is shown, then a presenter or surprised figure pops in (2-4 frames) to react or point (`Es5c2MKzbDg`, `7bm7XYssqlU`, `7IMFHclDn8k`).
7. **Named-person quote with highlight.** A bubble holds the quote text, the text scrolls, a yellow/green highlight sweeps the key clause (`RB5zVghPor8`, `ffIiiPLDFdM`).
8. **Pop-culture beat.** Meme photo, Avengers logo, Shaq dunk, The Office sign, pixel sunglasses appear as one-scene jokes (5 of 10 videos). Rights unverified; treat as inspiration only.
9. **Closing line paired with an image of the thesis object**, not a summary card: builder figure (`7bm7XYssqlU`), crystal-ball figure (`RB5zVghPor8`), worried host over '+1.8%' (`7IMFHclDn8k`), black circle fading (`DEgQTSsSX9U`), newspaper reader (`44ggG2I6JPY`).

## Narration-to-visual timing (MEASURED, n = 203 cuts, 427 caption words)
- The cut or pop usually arrives **on or slightly before** the spoken keyword: hard cuts median -80 ms against the nearest ASR word onset (p10 -199, p90 +62); captions median +33 ms (79 % within ±100 ms).
- 10 of 33 cuts in `Qof71Ef-3EU` and 13 of 18 pauses in `7bm7XYssqlU` coincide with the end of a >=0.25 s pause: new phrase, new scene (`MEASURED`, one video each).
- Reading holds: scene median 1.7-4.1 s across the sample; dense source pages in a closing montage were held 0.35-0.7 s (`Qof71Ef-3EU`, f1936-2048), i.e. too short to read and used as texture (`ART_DIRECTION_INFERENCE`).
- Compositions reset on every hard cut; between cuts the composition only grows (no objects are removed except by the next cut), with exceptions: a character that slides out and back (`Es5c2MKzbDg`), a prop that slides behind a figure, a chart that is recoloured, and an opacity fade.
