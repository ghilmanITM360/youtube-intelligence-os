## Observed practices (this video only)
- A single source document recurs as the visual spine while scenes about each number are inserted around it.
- Authority first, then finding, then reversal; the pivot sentence ("opposite effect") gets its own icon-and-arrow scene.
- Props enact verbs (documents handed back, a seal arriving, an ice cube for a cooled market).
