## Recurring practices observed here (single video; not yet population claims)
- A repeated narration sentence (0.0 s and 26.2 s) is paired with the same visuals both times (Earth, then orange figure): a deliberate visual callback (`DIRECTLY_OBSERVED`).
- Evidence screenshots persist while characters change (S04-S05 chart), or are pushed in to guide reading (ME014, ME045), and are followed by a presenter figure popping in to point (ME015, ME046).
- Reaction beats use a small cast of figures with different faces (smile, frown, neutral); they carry emphasis only and never move (`DIRECTLY_OBSERVED`: no limb motion in S14, S16, S18, S22, S24).
- 10 of 33 hard cuts fall within 120 ms of the end of a >=0.25 s narration pause; the median cut precedes the nearest ASR word onset by 87 ms (`MEASURED`, ASR +-~100 ms).
- Reading time: scenes hold a median 1.72 s (p10 0.43 s, p90 4.36 s); source pages in the closing montage are held 0.35-0.7 s, which is too short to read and so work as texture/proof rather than as readable evidence (`ART_DIRECTION_INFERENCE`).
