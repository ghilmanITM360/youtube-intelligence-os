## Observed practices (this video only)
- Concept built from an invented character ('Dwight') and a recurring prop (the check), then the character's obligation is kept visually constant while the noise (arguments, photos) is shown to be irrelevant.
- The ending is an opacity fade of the 'secret force' object: the disappearance is acted literally.
- Hard cuts sit close to ASR word onsets (median -13 ms, p10 -96, p90 139; n = 25) rather than leading them by 70-125 ms as in the full-bleed Shorts.
