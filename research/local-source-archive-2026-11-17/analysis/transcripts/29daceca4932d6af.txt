you no have cave money now, so you promise give cave money every full moon. For next, 500 full moon.

long laugh and disappear in smoke cloud. When smoke go, me see new cave with sign on top.

million cave money over many moon. Me say, well, two only bid bigger than one, so seem pretty good deal.

at me with thousand yard stare, so me have no choice. Me sign many stone slab. Then boom, me have cave.

haha, me take cave back. Enjoy sleep under tree. Me say, no. Crud try hard. So me hunt, me gather,
