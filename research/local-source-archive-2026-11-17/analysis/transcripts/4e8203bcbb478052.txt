Long time ago, tribe do trade like normal.
Me give you goat, you give me stick.
We both happy.
Plus me can now use stick to take goat.
But one day, clever weasel man say,
why carry goat when me can carry coin?
Soon, whole tribe use coin.
Then other tribes start use.
Then all tribes say, who got most coin?
He tribe chief now.
This not barter, this capitalism.
Capitalism mean caveman own stuff.
Not tribe stuff, not share stuff.
Stuff mine.
You want water, me sell.
You want cave, me rent.
You want air, you get free.
But give time, me figure out how sell.
System run on three thing.
Coin, market, greed.
Greed, not bad though.
You want more stuff, you work harder.
If you big brain, you get rich.
If you know big brain, you become employee.
Employee, just caveman who dig hole for other caveman coin.
But what happen if people make thing together?
That called corporation.
It's big tribe with leader who sit and take all coin.
Corporations sometimes buy other corporation.
They marry it, eat it, and wear its skin like loincloth.
Some say capitalism bring choice, freedom, invention.
Others say it create poor, stress, and deep spiritual hole where cave soul used to be.
Capitalism not perfect.
It grow fast, but leave some behind.
Like tribe member who twist ankle on hunt, but too big for upy upy.
So lesson is, coin like evil god.
It control all.
But if you worship, you end up empty.
With many thing, but no peace.
Baby, get what you need.
Give when you can.
And remember, you no take coin to afterlife.
Only smile and memory.
