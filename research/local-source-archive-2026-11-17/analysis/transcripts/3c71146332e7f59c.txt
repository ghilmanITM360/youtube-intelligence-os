The target isn't 3 million barrels of oil, it's 3 million barrels of oil equivalent,
which means natural gas counts. Besant said, "We're going to grow our way out of this."
The strategy he laid out for achieving this is called 3-3-3: 3% real GDP growth,
the federal deficit cut to 3% of GDP, and 3 million more barrels of oil a day,
or the equivalent from other energy sources. On crude alone, this isn't close. Crude output is
already at record levels, and forecasts estimate marginal gains at best. But natural gas is also
at an all-time high, and it's climbed almost every year for the better part of a decade.
Stack the two together, and the combined number is set to clear the target. So target 1 clears,
right up until you check where the bar was set. Over Biden's four years, American oil and gas
production rose about 4.5 million barrels a day of oil equivalent. Over Trump's first term,
before the pandemic, in under three years, it rose about 8.5 million. The target for Besant's
master plan to fix the economy is 3 million. So the goal was never ambitious. It would be like
if Shaq set a goal to dunk on an eight-foot basketball hoop. Besant picked a target for
energy that was going to be hit, whether anybody did anything or not.
