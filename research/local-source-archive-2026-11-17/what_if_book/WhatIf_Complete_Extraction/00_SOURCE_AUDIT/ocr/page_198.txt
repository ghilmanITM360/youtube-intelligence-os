LOST IMMORTALS

Q. If two immortal people were
placed on opposite sides of an
uninhabited Earthlike planet,
how long would it take them
to find each other?
100,000 years?
1,000,000 years?
100,000,000,000 years?

— Ethan Lake

A. WE'LL START WITH THE simple, physicist-style’ answer: 3000 years.
That’s about how long it would take two people to find each other, assuming
that they were walking around at random over a sphere for 12 hours per day and

had to get within a kilometer to see each other.
