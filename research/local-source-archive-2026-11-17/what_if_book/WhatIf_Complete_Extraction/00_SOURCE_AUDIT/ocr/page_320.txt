COLOPHON

The body of this text was set Adobe Caslon Pro,
a Caslon variant designed by Carol Twombly

in 1990 based on Caslon’s specimen pages.

‘The section headings and question titles
were set in Univers, a realist sans serif

designed by Adrian Frutiger in 1954.

The questions were set in Glypha,
a square serif typeface designed

by Adrian Frutiger in 1977.
