FACEBOOK OF THE DEAD

Q. When, if ever, will Facebook
contain more profiles of dead
people than of living ones?

—Emily Dunham

A. EITHER THE 2060s or the 2130s.

There are not a lot of dead people on Facebook.’ The main reasons for this is
that Facebook—and its users—are young. The average Facebook user has gotten
older over the last few years, but the site is still used at a much higher rate by the
young than by the old.

The past

Based on the site’s growth rate, and the age breakdown of its users over time,’
there are probably 10 to 20 million people who created Facebook profiles who
have since died.
