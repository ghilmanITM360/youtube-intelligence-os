So, as far as swimming safety goes, the bottom line is that you'd probably be
OK, as long as you didn’t dive to the bottom or pick up anything strange.

But just to be sure, I got in touch with a friend of mine who works at a re-
search reactor, and asked him what he thought would happen to someone who
tried to swim in their radiation containment pool.

“In our reactor?” He thought about it for a moment. “You'd die pretty quickly,

before reaching the water, from gunshot wounds.”
