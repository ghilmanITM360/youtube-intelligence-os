As the minutes stretch into hours and the arrow slows down more and more,
the airflow changes.

Air has very little viscosity. That is, it’s not gooey. That means things flying
through the air experience drag because of the momentum of the air they're shov-

ing out of the way—not from cohesion between the air molecules. It’s more like

pushing your hand through a bathtub full of water than a bathtub full of honey.
