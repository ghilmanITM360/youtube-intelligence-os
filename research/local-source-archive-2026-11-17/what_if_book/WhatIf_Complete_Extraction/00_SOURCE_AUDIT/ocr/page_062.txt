A MOLE OF MOLES

Q. What would happen if you
were to gather a mole (unit of
measurement) of moles (the small
furry critter) in one place?

—Sean Rice

A. THINGS GET A BIT gruesome.

First, some definitions.

A mole is a unit. It’s not a typical unit, though. It’s really just a num-
ber—like “dozen” or “billion.” If you have a mole of something, it means you
have 602,214,129,000,000,000,000,000 of them (usually written 6.022 x 10%). It’s
such a big number’ because it’s used for counting numbers of molecules, which
there are a lot of.
