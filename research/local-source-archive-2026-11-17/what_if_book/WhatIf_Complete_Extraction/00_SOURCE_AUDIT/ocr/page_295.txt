NEUTRON BULLET

Q. If a bullet with the density of
a neutron star were fired from a
handgun (ignoring the how)
at the Earth's surface, would
the Earth be destroyed?

— Charlotte Ainsworth

A. A BULLET WITH THE density of a neutron star would weigh about as much
as the Empire State Building.
Whether we fired it from a gun or not, the bullet would fall straight through
the ground, punching through the crust as if the rock were wet tissue paper.

What are neutron stars?
