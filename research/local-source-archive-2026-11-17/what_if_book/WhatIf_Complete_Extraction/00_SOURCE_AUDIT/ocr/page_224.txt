DRAIN THE OCEANS

By this point, most of the major oceans have become disconnected and
stopped draining. The exact locations and sizes of the various inland seas are
hard to predict; this is only a rough estimate.

DRAINED

This is what the map looks like when the drain finally empties. There’s a sur-
prising amount of water left, although much of it consists of very shallow seas,
with a few trenches where the water is as deep as 4 or 5 kilometers.

Vacuuming up half the oceans would massively alter the climate and ecosys-
tems in ways that are hard to predict. At the very least, it would almost certainly
involve a collapse of the biosphere and mass extinctions at every level.
