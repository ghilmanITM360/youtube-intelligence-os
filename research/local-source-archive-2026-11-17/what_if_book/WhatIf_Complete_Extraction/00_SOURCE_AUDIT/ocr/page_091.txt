And two million years later, your frozen body, still moving along steadily at a
foot per second, would pass through the heliopause into interstellar space.

Clyde Tombaugh, the astronomer who discovered Pluto, died in 1997. A por-
tion of his remains were placed on the New Horizons spacecraft, which will fly
past Pluto and then continue out of the solar system.

It’s true that your hypothetical foot-per-second trip would be cold, unpleas-
ant, and rapidly fatal. But when the Sun becomes a red giant in four billion years
and consumes the Earth, you and Clyde would be the only ones to escape.
