Around this time, the glass on the left starts to visibly lift into the air.
The air pressure is trying to squeeze the glass and water together. This is the

force we think of as suction. The vacuum on the right didn’t last long enough for
the suction to lift the glass, but since air can’t get into the vacuum on the left, the

glass and the water begin to slide toward each other.

=—_

The boiling water has filled the vacuum with a very small amount of water

vapor. As the space gets smaller, the buildup of water vapor slowly increases the
pressure on the water’s surface. Eventually, this will slow the boiling, just like

higher air pressure would.
