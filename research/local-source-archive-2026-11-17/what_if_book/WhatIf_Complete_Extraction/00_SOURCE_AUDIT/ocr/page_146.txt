Q. This may be a bit gruesome,
but ...if someone’s DNA
suddenly vanished, how long
would that person last?

— Nina Charest

A. IF YOU LOST YOUR DNA, you would instantly be about a third of a pound
lighter.

Losing a third of a pound
I don’t recommend this strategy. There are easier ways to lose a third of a pound,

including:

* Taking off your shirt

* Peeing

* Cutting your hair (if you have very long hair)

* Donating blood, but putting a kink in the IV once they drain 150 mL and
refusing to let them take any more

* Holding a 3-foot-diameter balloon full of helium

* Removing your fingers
