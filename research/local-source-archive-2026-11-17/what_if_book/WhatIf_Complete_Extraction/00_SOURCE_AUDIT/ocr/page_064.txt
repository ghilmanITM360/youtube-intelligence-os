Let’s get some better numbers.

An eastern mole (Scalopus aquaticus) weighs about 75 grams, which means a

mole of moles weighs:
(6.022 x 10”) x 75g = 4.52 x 10” kg

That’s a little over half the mass of our moon.

Mammals are largely water. A kilogram of water takes up a liter of volume,
so if the moles weigh 4.52 x10” kilograms, they take up about 4.52 x10” liters of
volume. You might notice that we’re ignoring the pockets of space between the
moles. In a moment, you'll see why.

The cube root of 4.52 x 10” liters is 3562 kilometers, which means we're talking
about a sphere with a radius of 2210 kilometers, or a cube 2213 miles on each edge.’
