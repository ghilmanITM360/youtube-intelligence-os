Which brings us back to the original question: Could you boil tea if you just
stirred it hard enough?

No.

The first problem is power. The amount of power in question, 700 watts, is
about a horsepower, so if you want to boil tea in two minutes, you'll need at least

one horse to stir it hard enough.

You can reduce the power requirement by heating the tea over a longer period

of time, but if you reduce it too far the tea will be cooling as fast as you're heat-
ing it.

Even if you could churn the spoon hard enough—tens of thousands of stirs
per second—fluid dynamics would get in the way. At those high speeds, the tea
would cavitate; a vacuum would form along the path of the spoon and stirring
would become ineffective.°

And if you stir hard enough that your tea cavitates,
its surface area will increase very rapidly, and it will
cool to room temperature in seconds.

No matter how hard you stir your tea, it’s not going
to get any warmer.
