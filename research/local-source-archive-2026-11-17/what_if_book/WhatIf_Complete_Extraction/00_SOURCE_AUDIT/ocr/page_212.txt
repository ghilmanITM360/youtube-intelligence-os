Which brings us to wingsuits—the halfway point between parachute pants
and parachutes.

Wingsuits let you fall much more slowly. One wingsuit operator posted track-
ing data from a series of jumps. It shows that in a glide, a wingsuit can lose alti-
tude as slowly as 18 meters per second—a huge improvement over 55.
