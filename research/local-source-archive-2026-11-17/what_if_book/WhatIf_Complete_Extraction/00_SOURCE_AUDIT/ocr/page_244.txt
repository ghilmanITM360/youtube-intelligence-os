NOT A SUNSET

Sunset starts the instant the Sun touches the horizon, and ends when it dis-
appears completely. If the Sun touches the horizon and then lifts back up, the
sunset is disqualified.

For a sunset to count, the Sun has to set behind the idealized horizon, not just

behind a nearby hill. This is not a sunset, even though it seems like one:
