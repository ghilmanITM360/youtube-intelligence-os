STIRRING TEA

Q. | was absentmindedly stirring
a cup of hot tea, when I got to
thinking, “Aren't I actually adding
kinetic energy into this cup?”

I know that stirring does help
to cool down the tea, but
what if I were to stir it faster?
Would I be able to boil a cup
of water by stirring?

— Will Evans

A. No.

The basic idea makes sense. Temperature is just kinetic energy. When you stir
tea, you're adding kinetic energy to it, and that energy goes somewhere. Since the
tea doesn’t do anything dramatic like rise into the air or emit light, the energy
must be turning to heat.
