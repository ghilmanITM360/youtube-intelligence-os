Q. Is there enough energy to
move the entire current human
population off-planet?

—Adam

A. THERE ARE A BUNCH of science fiction movies where, because of pollu-
tion, overpopulation, or nuclear war, humanity abandons Earth.

lation, is launching the whole human race into space physically possible? Let’s
not even worry about where we’re headed —we’ll assume we don’t have to find a
new home, but we can’t stay here.
