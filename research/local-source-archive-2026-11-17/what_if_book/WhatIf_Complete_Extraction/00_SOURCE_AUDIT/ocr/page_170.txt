The upshot is that to overcome Earth’s gravity using traditional rocket fuels,
a 1-ton craft needs 20 to 50 tons of fuel. Launching all of humanity (total weight:
around 400 million tons) would therefore take tens of trillions of tons of fuel.
That’s a lot; if we were using hydrocarbon-based fuels, it would represent a de-
cent chunk of the world’s remaining oil reserves. And that’s not even worrying
about the weight of the ship itself, food, water, or our pets.’ We'd also need fuel
to produce all these ships, to transport people to the launch sites, and so forth.
It’s not necessarily completely impossible, but it’s certainly outside the realm of
plausibility.
