# WI-0044: Yoda

**Question:** How much Force power can Yoda output?

**Submitted by:** Ryan Finnie

**PDF pages:** 158–160

## Answer summary

The X-wing lift gives a lower bound around 19 kW, roughly 25 horsepower.

## Scientific explanation

The author uses original-trilogy footage, ignoring prequels. X-wing mass is estimated by cubically scaling a 19 m, 19,700 kg F-22 to 12.5 m. Timing the front strut emerging from water gives about 1.4 m rise in 3.6 s. Wookieepedia supplies Dagobah gravity of 0.9g. Gravitational work per time gives 19.2 kW, enough for a block of homes or an electric Smart Car. Emperor lightning might resemble 10 kW pulsed Tesla-coil displays, but a sustained arc could imply megawatts. Luke's brief lightsaber retrieval is estimated at 400 W. At world electricity demand near 2 TW, about 100 million Yodas would be needed.
