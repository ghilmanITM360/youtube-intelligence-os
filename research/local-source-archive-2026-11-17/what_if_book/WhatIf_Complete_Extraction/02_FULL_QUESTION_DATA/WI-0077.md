# WI-0077: Sunless Earth

**Question:** What would happen to the Earth if the Sun suddenly switched off?

## Answer summary

A satirical inventory of minor benefits ends with the overriding result: everyone freezes and dies.

## Scientific explanation

['The Sun is assumed to become a cold inert sphere, retaining a gravitational presence rather than disappearing.
