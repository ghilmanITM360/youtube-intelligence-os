# WI-0060: Orbital Speed

**Question:** Could a (small) rocket (with payload) be lifted to a high point in the atmosphere where it would only need a small rocket to get to escape velocity?

**Submitted by:** Kenny Van de Maele

**PDF pages:** 202–206

**Status:** VISUALLY_REVIEWED; SOURCE_OCR_NEEDS_PROOFREAD

## Answer summary

A high starting point saves relatively little compared with the velocity requirement.

## Scientific explanation

The chapter distinguishes being high from moving fast enough to remain in space. Lifting a rocket near the 100 km space boundary does not supply the large horizontal velocity needed for orbit or the still greater energy needed for escape. Most launch energy and propellant expenditure is associated with velocity, so elevation alone does not eliminate the large rocket requirement.
