# WI-0045: Flyover States

**Question:** Which US state is actually flown over the most?

**Submitted by:** Jesse Ruderman

**PDF pages:** 161–164

**Status:** VISUALLY_REVIEWED; SOURCE_OCR_NEEDS_PROOFREAD

## Answer summary

In the author's historical route sample Virginia wins total overflights; Delaware wins ratio; Hawaii is least flown over and most flown under.

## Scientific explanation

Over 10,000 air-traffic routes are mapped to states crossed, excluding the state where each flight takes off or lands. Virginia leads despite hosting two DC airports that remove many arrivals from the overflight count. Atlanta's hub produces heavy northeast traffic, but Toronto Pearson contributes even more Virginia overflights than JFK and LaGuardia combined, partly through Caribbean and South American routes. Maryland, North Carolina and Pennsylvania also rank highly. By overflights-to-arrivals ratio Delaware wins because the data interval has no scheduled airline at New Castle; a footnote notes service resumed in 2012 and was omitted. Hawaii is least overflown due to isolated geography; California is least among non-island states, with post-9/11 routing policy offered as explanation. Hawaii wins antipodal flights because its opposite point in Africa has more air traffic than the Indian Ocean opposite most US states.
