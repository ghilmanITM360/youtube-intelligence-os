# WI-0057: Lost Immortals

**Question:** If two immortal people were placed on opposite sides of an uninhabited Earthlike planet, how long would it take them to find each other? 100,000 years? 1,000,000 years? 100,000,000,000 years?

## Answer summary

A naive random-walk estimate is 3000 years, but cooperative search and persistent trail markers can reduce the time to years or decades.

## Scientific explanation

A simple model assumes random wandering 12 hours per day on a sphere and detection within 1 km, yielding about 3000 years. Terrain and visibility undermine uniform detection, while intentional search undermines random wandering. Prior coordination allows a rendezvous at a pole or conspicuous landmark. Without it, following coasts reduces a two-dimensional search to a line but equal-direction travel can prevent a meeting indefinitely. Randomly reversing after each circuit helps if both follow the algorithm. The author's preferred ant-like strategy leaves dated stone markers pointing onward, rests three days per walking day, and follows an unfamiliar trail quickly when found. Searchers need intersect a past trail rather than the other person's present location; faster following than trail-laying makes eventual contact plausible.
