# WI-0059: Orbital Speed

**Question:** Is it possible for a spacecraft to control its reentry in such a way that it avoids the atmospheric compression and thus would not require the expensive (and relatively fragile) heat shield on the outside?

## Answer summary

Shares the chapter's speed-versus-altitude explanation and propulsive-braking constraint.

## Scientific explanation

Avoiding high-speed atmospheric compression requires shedding orbital velocity before encountering thick air. Rockets can conceptually do this, but their propellant must itself be launched, producing exponential mass penalties. The source contrasts this with using atmospheric drag and a heat shield to dispose of kinetic energy.
