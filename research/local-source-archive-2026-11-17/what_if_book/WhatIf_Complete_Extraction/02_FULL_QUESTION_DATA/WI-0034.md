# WI-0034: Hockey Puck

**Question:** How hard would a puck have to be shot to be able to knock the goalie himself backward into the net?

**Submitted by:** Tom

**PDF pages:** 127–128

**Status:** VISUALLY_REVIEWED; SOURCE_OCR_NEEDS_PROOFREAD

## Answer summary

Enough nominal momentum requires hypersonic speed, but the puck and impact do not behave like a cartoon shove.

## Scientific explanation

A geared goalie outweighs a puck by about 600 times and can brace on the ice. The author estimates a 165 g puck needs Mach 2–8 to knock the goalie backward, with bracing increasing the requirement and an upward strike reducing it. A light-gas gun could launch it, but compressive heating and drag rapidly alter and decelerate it. At impact it would burst apart with explosive energy rather than produce a clean puck-shaped hole or translate the goalie intact; the analogy is a tomato thrown at a cake.
