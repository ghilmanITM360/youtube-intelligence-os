## Hook rules (Hook guide + Viral Checklist), mapped to our 4-part formula
1. **Context lean = everyday situation.** Within the first sentence the viewer must think "this is about me / my parent". Lead with the pain (dizzy on standing), not the topic (hypertension).
2. **Scroll-stop interjection = "But…".** In the user's example: "That sounds like good news. **But** now, every time you stand up, you feel dizzy."
3. **Contrarian snapback = reason to watch.** Lower BP is not always better. That is the real tension of this video.
4. **Staccato sentences** in the hook, longer ones later.
5. **Visual hook:** 3–5 bold words on screen in the first second (e.g. `LOWER… TOO LOW?`).
6. **Specificity and numbers:** one concrete number early (a big BP reading), but no unexplained number chains.
7. Skip from the Viral Checklist for a medical video: "urgency", "credibility hacks with celebrities" and "enhancer words like secret". They hurt trust.

## Story flow (BUT / THEREFORE)
- Replace every "and then" with a **BUT** (complication) or **THEREFORE** (consequence) beat, roughly every 8–12 s in narrator lines.
- The spine of this video:
  - BP causes heart disease, **therefore** treat it;
  - **but** elderly people fall when BP drops;
  - **therefore** doctors once avoided treatment;
  - **but** a big trial showed treatment helps;
  - **therefore** treat, but choose the medicine to fit the person.
- The bridges and the takeaway follow this spine.

## Typography (VIDEO EDITING transcripts, CapCut motion typography)
- Clean grotesks: **Figtree / Inter / Manrope**. One word or phrase per beat.
- Text enters about **13 frames** after the cut/beat (do not land on the same frame).
- Motion: scale-from-zoom background + text slide/mask. "Fuzzy zoom" (blur into focus) as a reveal.
- Every text move carries a sound: visuals are only half of it.

## Sound design (UE Sound Design training)
- **Music only under the talking head, around −19 dB, never under graphics.** Graphics are carried by foley and textures.
- Three passes in this order: whooshes (movement) → textures (pops, clicks, reveals on the event frame) → risers and hits. Music goes last.
- A riser **about 5 s after the hook** that ends on the cut into the body, with a hit underneath.
- Whoosh peak on the fastest frame. Whooshes never above −10 dB. Nothing over the voice louder than the voice.
- Never a whoosh for a pop, never a pop for a slide.

## What we do NOT take
- Algorithm transcripts: creator opinion. Not used for script decisions.
- Sharp viral tactics (FOMO, "secret", celebrity name-drops): wrong for an elderly patient audience and for medical trust.
