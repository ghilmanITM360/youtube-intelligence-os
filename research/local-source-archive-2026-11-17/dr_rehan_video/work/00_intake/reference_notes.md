# Reference notes

## A. `input/references/example_narration_style.md` (the user's own example): **the format standard**
- 5 narrator blocks with placement notes: cold open + intro (before 0:00, ~30 s), bridge (after 1:28), orthostatic clarification (~1:20), evidence update (after 2:49), final takeaway.
- Tone: calm, second person ("You're 72…"), short sentences, no hype. It corrects facts gently ("One important point needs clarification").
- Visual note from the user:
  - a big BP reading;
  - then an older adult standing up;
  - then the two competing concerns side by side (protect the heart vs avoid dizziness and falls);
  - the target comparison **labelled by guideline and age group**.
- The intro wording "Dr. Rehan Omer, Consultant Interventional Cardiologist and Diabetologist" matches `kit/05`.

| Look | What it implies for motion |

| Hand silhouettes holding a glowing object (bulb, orb) | Do **not** use hands or people from AI. Take only the "object glowing in the dark" idea. |

Overall: a **cool blue, dark, glossy "premium tech" family**. It is more energetic than the kit's default "Clinical Calm" (off-white, no glow). Direction 2 below adapts it for a medical audience.
