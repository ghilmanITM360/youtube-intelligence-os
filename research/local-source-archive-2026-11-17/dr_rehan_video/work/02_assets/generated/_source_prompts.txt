Generate one image per prompt block below, in order (both a and b variants).

When done, write generated/_index.md listing each file and its prompt ID.
STYLE LOCK: CC (Clinical Calm)
Editorial photography look, natural soft window light, muted warm-neutral palette (off-white, warm grey, deep navy, one accent of clinical red #D64545 only where noted). Shallow depth of field, 50mm lens feel, subtle film grain, realistic textures, uncluttered composition with generous negative space for typography. NO text, NO letters, NO numbers on any screen, NO logos, NO watermarks, NO brand names, NO medical gore, NO glossy CGI, NO plastic skin, NO oversaturated colours, NO holograms. People: never a clear face. Hands, silhouettes, backs, over-the-shoulder, or faces cropped/out of focus. Pakistani / South-Asian everyday settings, modest clothing.

STYLE LOCK: MC (Midnight Clinic)
Same rules as CC, but low-key: pre-dawn blue hour, deep navy shadows (#0B1430), cool cobalt rim light, one small warm practical light source, quiet and intimate, not horror, not sci-fi. Leave the left 40% of the frame dark and empty for typography.

STYLE LOCK: NB (Notebook sketch)
Hand-drawn editorial illustration: fine graphite pencil and dark-grey ink line art (#3A3A3A) on warm cream paper (#FAF6EE), slightly wobbly human lines, light cross-hatching, at most one accent of red pencil (#D64545) where noted. Looks drawn by hand in a doctor's notebook, not vector, not 3D, not glossy. NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO labels, NO arrows with words. No faces drawn in detail. For "transparent" blocks: line art only on a transparent background (no paper).

STYLE LOCK: FX (overlay plates)
Pure photographic overlay plates for compositing in an editor. Shot on black (or mid-grey where noted), no objects, no text, no frame borders. Must loop or tile without visible seams where noted.
