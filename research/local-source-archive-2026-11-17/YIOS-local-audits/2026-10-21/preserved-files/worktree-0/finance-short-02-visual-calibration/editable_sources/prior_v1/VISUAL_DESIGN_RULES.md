# Visual design rules

Observation anchors resolve through REFERENCE_EVIDENCE.md. These are original production choices inferred from rendered output, not claims about the creator's authoring process.

1. **White space first — E01/E03.** 1080×1920 white canvas, one dominant explanatory relationship. Persistent layouts gain props and labels; diagrams sometimes occupy the canvas without a host. Environments and dark panels are documented exceptions, not mandatory decoration.
2. **Sparse ink — E01/E02/E07.** Slightly asymmetric silhouettes, black contours, restrained flat fills. Thin stick limbs and simple visible hand shapes. Current original woman has a side bun, oval tan face, dot eyes and four expressions; no spiky-haired reference-host recreation. No corporate cards, gradients, glossy shading or generic icon pack.
3. **Consistent contact.** Body/feet anchors stay fixed during expression/arm switches. Hold paper behind near thumb; paper and hand travel together. Coins/notes originate and terminate on a specified account/object path; arrows are conceptual, not loose objects falling through a character. No physics/IK implementation.
4. **Functional colour — E03/E04/E10.** Green for money/financial flows or inflation-fighting benefit; red for expense burden, loss or pause. Raising rates is not universally positive: green rate arrow denotes direction in the hook, while the red bill states its cost. Cream props are neutral. Yellow only highlights actual evidence. Retain labels so colour is not the only cue.
5. **Typography restraint — E04/E06.** One main phrase, one diagram relationship and one qualified number at a time. Editable terms and figures; short captions later follow audio. Current samples use macOS Chalkboard with Comic Sans MS/sans-serif fallback; no font files redistributed. These are preview fonts, not cross-PC pixel identity. Select/licence an open font or original lettering after approval, then recheck widths before export. Text remains live in SVG.
6. **Mobile composition.** Target essential content x80–920,y150–1470. Reserve rightmost150px and bottom350px, top120px for a deliberately conservative simulated Shorts obstruction. These are design test zones, not a verified universal YouTube UI template. Future captions aroundy1480 require a separate full-width check; this phase has no synchronized caption track. Important type44–102px; secondary source attribution30–40px. Tiny source text is corroboration, with a large original redraw carrying meaning.
7. **Progression — E03/E04/E05.** Banks/Fed persist while balances and the interest arrow appear. Local enlargement then exposes the annualized arithmetic. Mismatch replaces the mechanism, then becomes the deferred balance. Treasury flow pauses before the historical comparison is added. Do not invent graph samples or bar heights as measured expense/income values.
8. **Evidence — E06.** Use real dated document pixels with a separate highlight; only mention facts in the approved script. Never make an imitation official evidence panel. Hero03 shows a genuine H.4.1 row with original negative sign/units and a separate cumulative magnitude redraw.
9. **Motion menu, future only.** CUT, POP_IN, SLIDE_IN, POSITION_XY, SCALE, ROTATE, OPACITY, POSE_SWITCH, EXPRESSION_SWITCH, TRACE_REVEAL, SOURCE_HIGHLIGHT, ADDITIVE_BUILD, CAMERA_PUSH. A short local scale can guide attention; it is not evidence that the reference used a virtual camera. Default editor needs separate artwork layers, no new rig SDK. Mask/trace reveal may be appropriate for diagrams/highlights, but exact competitor methods remain unknown.
10. **Ending clarity — E10.** Pair the inflation benefit with a slower recovery burden. Use a labelled conceptual recovery path, not a forecast curve with an invented duration. Reuse the rate arrow/Fed motif for recognition. No claim that the quarter-point change caused≈$234B.

The current samples are a bounded style test. Source sequence fidelity is not animation-tested; no timing or artistic acceptance is certified. Easing, exact narration sync, caption pacing and any final tax/remittance nuance require the later editor/owner review.
