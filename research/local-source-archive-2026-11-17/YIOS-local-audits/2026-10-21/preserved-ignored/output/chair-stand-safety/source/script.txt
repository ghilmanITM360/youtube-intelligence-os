Don't Do This Chair Test If You Feel This
Before you try the 30-second chair stand test, there is one safety rule you shouldn't ignore.
So, start the counter, up to 30 seconds, try to count the number of times that you can stand up on the chair unassisted and then sit down. However, if you feel unsafe, if you got dizziness or ataxia, then don't do this test. Once you have your number, don't compare that to universal number.
