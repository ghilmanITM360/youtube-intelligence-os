Because for households worth between one and two million, U.S. Census Bureau data shows that 39% of their net worth sits in their primary residence and 33% sits in their retirement accounts, which means roughly 72% of the average millionaire's wealth is tied up in long-term illiquid assets, because paper wealth that's highly concentrated is real until it isn't. And if 72% of your net worth is in illiquid assets that are market sensitive, you're exposed.
