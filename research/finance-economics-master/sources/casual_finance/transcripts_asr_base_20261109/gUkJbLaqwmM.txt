In early June, a chipmaker called Broadcom beat its earnings, but guided its AI sales for the next quarter a few hundred million dollars light. For a company worth nearly two trillion dollars that should barely move the needle. But following that news, about three hundred billion dollars of its market value vanished in a single session. One of the biggest single-day losses in Wall Street history over what amounts to a rounding error for a company of Broadcom's size. That's the tell. When a few hundred million in soft guidance can erase three hundred billion dollars in a day, you're not looking at a healthy market. You're looking at one price for perfection, where being even slightly less than perfect costs you more than a quarter of a trillion dollars.
