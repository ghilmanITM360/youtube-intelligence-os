This year alone, big techs on track to spend more than $700 billion dollars building out AI. That's roughly two and a half cents of every dollar the entire United States economy produces. For most of the last decade, these companies spent roughly 40 to 50 cents of every dollar of cash their businesses produced on capital expenditures. The formal name for reinvesting cash back into your business. This year, PIMCO estimates that number hits 94 cents. That's tech companies running their entire cash engine to finance the AI build out. So it's not the vault is funding the build out anymore. It's the build out is draining the vault. And when these companies ran out of cash to reinvest, they hit the bond markets at a pace we've never seen before. From 2020 to 2024, the five biggest hyper scalers issued on average $28 billion a year of bonds. In 2025, the same five biggest hyper scalers issued more than $100 billion. More than four times their average pace over the previous five years. Then 2026 happened. In just the first five months of this year, they've already issued another $159 billion dollars. Meaning in five months, they borrowed more than they had in the entire previous five years combined.
