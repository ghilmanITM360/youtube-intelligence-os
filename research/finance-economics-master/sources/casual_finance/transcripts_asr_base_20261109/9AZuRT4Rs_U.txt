You've probably seen the memes. People financing a Chipotle burrito by splitting it into four payments. This new kind of debt is a form of financing that lets people buy something today and pay for it in smaller installments over time. It's called buy now pay later, and it used to just be for things like furniture and concert tickets. But now more than 90 million Americans use it. According to lending tree, more than a quarter of buy now pay later users say they've used these loans for groceries. And more than half say they couldn't make ends meet without using it. So a significant chunk of the country is now financing their trip to the grocery store just to get through the week.
