This year alone, the biggest tech companies in the world are on track to spend around $725 billion on artificial intelligence. And when most people hear a number like that, they usually just scroll right past it, without ever stopping to picture how much money that actually is. Because $725 billion isn't just a big number. It's an almost incomprehensible amount of money. To put it into perspective, if you spent $1 million every single day starting from the day Jesus was born, all the way through the fall of the Roman Empire, the Middle Ages, the Renaissance, both world wars, and all the way up to today, you still wouldn't have spent as much as big tech as projected to spend in AI in just this year. It breaks down to roughly $2 billion a day, $83 million an hour, or about $23,000 every second.
