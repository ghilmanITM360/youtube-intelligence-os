R1Tired on a Beta-Blocker? What Metoprolol Fatigue Looks Like and 3 Questions to Ask

Status: LAUNCH

Test evidence: YT test 2026-11-14: JJ Medicine metoprolol 1,013,965; Medicine with Dr. Moran 204,590; small channels 196-849. Demand proven, giants exist, no 'after 60' needed.

Thumbnail: Presenter slumped at a table with an untouched coffee, three pill-type icons above. Text: 'TIRED? THREE PILLS' (asli presenter ka chehra, cartoon nahi)

Script, hook (0-30s): “You sleep eight hours and still wake up flat. You tell your family, I'm just old. But there are three medicine types that can quietly pull your energy down, and a short checklist that tells your doctor exactly what to look at.”

Script, beech: Three groups: beta-blockers (may cause tiredness in some people), sedating antihistamines and sleep aids, benzodiazepine-type drugs. Also not-pill causes to ask about: low thyroid, low B12, anaemia, poor sleep, low mood. A 7-day energy score and pill-time sheet.

Script, akhir: Ask the clinician to review the list and consider blood tests; never stop a beta-blocker abruptly.

Sources (VERIFY, fetch nahi): Drug labels; AGS Beers Criteria; clinical guidance on fatigue (VERIFY)

R2Benadryl or 'PM' Pills Every Night? What the Dementia Studies Actually Say

Status: LAUNCH (test)

Test evidence: YT test: Leonid Kim MD 'This Pill is linked to DEMENTIA' 6,598,380; Doctor Mike 781,788; new small channels 127-301. Biggest demand, biggest competition.

Thumbnail: Closeup of an OTC 'PM' box, presenter pointing at the small print. Text: 'READ THIS WORD' (asli presenter ka chehra, cartoon nahi)

Script, hook (0-30s): “Look at the box you take at night. Find the small word in the ingredient list. That one word is why pharmacists get nervous when a person over sixty-five takes this every night. Let me show you where to find it and what to ask instead of guessing.”

Script, beech: Why one ingredient appears in many products (sleep, cold, 'PM' pain relief). Why older adults are more sensitive (dry mouth, urine retention, next-day drowsiness, confusion, falls). Non-drug sleep basics in 3 lines. How to ask: 'What are my safer options for sleep, for me?'

Script, akhir: Not 'stop tonight' but 'ask this week'. Pharmacist card in description.

Sources (VERIFY, fetch nahi): AGS Beers Criteria; FDA OTC labeling (VERIFY)

R3Medicines That Cause Constipation: Check Your List Before Buying Another Laxative

Status: LAUNCH

Test evidence: YT test: KenDBerryMD 72K (5 yr); Dr Nimra 815 (1 mo, exact angle exists); ~6 of 7 recent 'after 60' clones <1K. Modest, angle NOT new.

Thumbnail: Left half: presenter holding an open pill drawer, worried look. Right: big 'AGE?' crossed, 'PILLS?' circled. Text: 'AGE or PILLS?' (asli presenter ka chehra, cartoon nahi)

Script, hook (0-30s): “If you are over sixty and constipated for weeks, people tell you it is just age. Before you accept that, open your pill drawer. Some very common medicines are a known cause, and the fix is a conversation, not a guess. Today: five kinds of medicine to check, and three signs that mean call your doctor this week.”

Script, beech: 1) Opioid pain medicines. 2) Anticholinergics: some bladder pills, older allergy and sleep pills. 3) Some calcium-channel blockers for blood pressure (verapamil is the usual example). 4) Iron and calcium supplements or antacids. 5) Some antidepressants. For each: where to find it on the label, and the question to ask: 'Is there another option, or a timing change?' Red flags: blood in stool, unexplained weight loss, severe pain, a sudden change that lasts weeks.

Script, akhir: Do not stop any pill on your own. Write the medicine name and the date constipation began. The one-page Ask-Your-Pharmacist card is in the description.

Sources (VERIFY, fetch nahi): ACG/AGA constipation guidance; NIDDK constipation page; drug labels (VERIFY)

R4Dizzy When You Stand Up? Your Pills Could Be the Reason (3-Step Check)

Status: LAUNCH

Test evidence: YT test: Fauquier ENT 1,140,511; motivationaldoc Short 531,525; 7 recent 'after 60' clones 10-900. Trends 'dizziness' last 52w +42% vs prior.

Thumbnail: Presenter gripping a chair, motion lines, four small pill-class icons. Text: 'DIZZY? CHECK THESE 4' (asli presenter ka chehra, cartoon nahi)

Script, hook (0-30s): “You stand up and the room tilts for a second. You tell yourself it is nothing. For many people over sixty-five, that second is the start of a fall. And one of the most common causes is sitting in the medicine cabinet. Four kinds of medicine, and the three-step standing test you can do tonight.”

Script, beech: Explain blood-pressure drop on standing in plain words. Four classes to check: blood-pressure medicines (including combinations), alpha-blockers used for prostate or BP, some antidepressants and sedatives, water pills. Safe habit: sit on the bed edge for a minute before standing. Show how to take lying-sitting-standing BP readings and write them down for the clinician. Red flags: fainting, chest pain, new confusion.

Script, akhir: Falls are about the whole picture. Ask: 'Which of my medicines can lower BP on standing, and should any be reviewed?' Do not skip BP pills on your own.

Sources (VERIFY, fetch nahi): AGS Beers Criteria; CDC STEADI falls guidance; drug labels (VERIFY)

R5Waking to Pee at Night? Check WHEN You Take Your Water Pill

Status: LAUNCH (test)

Test evidence: YT test: Rena Malik 1,324,269 (5 yr); TopHealthTips 94,765 ('Do THIS before bed'); 6 recent clones 38-420. Pill-timing angle: none seen in top 9.

Thumbnail: Clock showing 2:47 AM, presenter yawning, a pill bottle with a small clock icon. Text: 'IS IT YOUR PILL?' (asli presenter ka chehra, cartoon nahi)

Script, hook (0-30s): “Two forty-seven in the morning. Third trip to the bathroom. You blame your bladder, your age, your tea. Here is a question almost nobody asks: what time did you take your water pill? Timing can change your nights. Let me show you what to check, and what to ask your prescriber.”

Script, beech: What a diuretic does and why timing matters (general principle; prescriber decides). Other contributors to check: evening fluids, caffeine, alcohol, swollen legs lying down, sleep apnea, prostate in men. A 3-day 'night log' the viewer can fill: time of pills, drinks, trips. Red flags: burning, blood in urine, sudden new urgency, fever.

Script, akhir: Never move a dose on your own: bring the night log to your pharmacist or doctor and ask 'Can this be taken earlier in the day?'

Sources (VERIFY, fetch nahi): Drug label for the specific diuretic; AUA/NIDDK nocturia information (VERIFY)

R6Medicines Linked to Memory Fog: 3 Shorts First, Long Video Later

Status: MONTH 2 (Shorts now)

Test evidence: YT test: Shorts 26K-65K (Dr. Ethan Melillo PharmD 64,636); long videos 15-1,278. Shorts carry demand, long form unproven.

Thumbnail: Presenter with a thought-bubble full of fog, one pill bottle clear in the foreground. Text: 'FOG: AGE OR PILL?' (asli presenter ka chehra, cartoon nahi)

Script, hook (0-30s): “Forgetting words. Losing your train of thought. Many families hear 'it's just aging' and move on. But some common medicines are known to cloud thinking in older adults, and sometimes the fog lifts when the plan is reviewed. This is not about scaring you. It is about asking the right question first.”

Script, beech: Medicine groups linked with confusion or memory complaints in older adults: strongly anticholinergic drugs (some bladder, allergy, sleep products), benzodiazepines and similar sleep drugs, some pain medicines. How to read an OTC label for 'PM' or 'diphenhydramine'. A one-line symptom diary. Red flags: sudden confusion, getting lost, personality change: urgent medical review, not a video.

Script, akhir: Bring your list to the pharmacist and ask: 'Is anything here on the Beers list for my age?' Never stop a sleep or anxiety pill suddenly: stopping can be harmful.

Sources (VERIFY, fetch nahi): AGS Beers Criteria; anticholinergic-burden research; FDA benzodiazepine labeling (VERIFY)

R7Tylenol, Advil or Aleve? Which Fits Which Senior

Status: LOW PRIORITY

Test evidence: YT test: Better Health While Aging 826,303; Talking With Docs 682,056; 5+ others 300K-1.7M. Saturated by giants.

Thumbnail: Three pain-reliever boxes as podium, presenter holding 'WHICH ONE FOR ME?' card. Text: 'ONE DRAWER, 3 RISKS' (asli presenter ka chehra, cartoon nahi)

Script, hook (0-30s): “Three boxes in your drawer. Everyone reaches for the nearest one. But if you are over sixty with blood pressure, stomach, or kidney concerns, the right choice may be different from your neighbour's. Let me give you the one-minute chart pharmacists use.”

Script, beech: Acetaminophen: liver limits and hidden duplicates in cold products. Ibuprofen and naproxen (NSAIDs): stomach, kidney and BP effects; special caution with blood thinners. A simple grid by condition. Duplicate-ingredient trap with combination products.

Script, akhir: Ask the pharmacist: 'Given my conditions and medicines, which is safest for me and what is my daily limit?'

Sources (VERIFY, fetch nahi): FDA/NIDDK OTC analgesic labeling; AGS pain guidance (VERIFY)

R8Swollen Ankles? Medicines That Cause Fluid Retention

Status: SPRING (Apr-Jun), not next 3 months

Test evidence: YT test: KenDBerryMD '11 Common Medications That Cause Edema' 1,752,304. Trends 'swollen ankles' Nov-Feb 25.2 vs 43.0 other months (-41%).

Thumbnail: Presenter pressing a sock-mark in a swollen ankle, sock-line visible. Text: 'SOCK MARKS? PILLS?' (asli presenter ka chehra, cartoon nahi)

Script, hook (0-30s): “Pull off your socks at night. Do you see deep lines around your ankles? Swelling is not always the heart, and it is not always age. Sometimes it is a medicine effect your clinician can adjust. Here are the medicine groups to check, and the swelling signs that need a same-day call.”

Script, beech: Medicine groups linked with ankle swelling: some blood-pressure drugs (for example amlodipine-type), NSAID pain relievers, some diabetes medicines. Other causes to rule out (heart, kidney, veins): why a clinician must check. Red flags: swelling on one leg only, breathlessness, chest pain: emergency.

Script, akhir: Ask: 'Could any of my medicines cause this, and is there another option?' Do not stop heart or BP medicines on your own.

Sources (VERIFY, fetch nahi): Drug labels; AHA/ACC edema guidance (VERIFY)
