But artificial intelligence right now is holding up the entire economy. A Harvard economist named Jason Furman ran the numbers. And in the first half of 2025, he found that investment in information processing equipment and software, which is the AI buildout, was only about 4% of GDP. But it accounted for roughly 92% of all the growth in the US economy. So if you strip the AI buildout from the equation, the US economy only grew at about one tenth of 1%.
