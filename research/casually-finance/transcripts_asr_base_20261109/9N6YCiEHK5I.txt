In JP Morgan's third quarter earnings call from 2025, when CEO Jamie Diamond was asked about the cracks in the credit market, he responded, when you see one cockroach, there are probably more. Which is hilarious for so many reasons, but it's also true. And it's also exactly what the data is showing us. Because earlier this year, the Fitch Private Credit Default Rate, which tracks more than 1,500 middle-market private credit borrowers, hits 6% on its trailing 12-month measure, which is the highest rate recorded since inception. This is where the consequences of rapidly rising rates begin to show up.
