You've probably seen this chart that looks like a circuit board. Every name on it is one of the biggest companies in AI, and every arrow is money moving between them. A purchase here, an investment there, a loan over there. And if you follow almost any arrow, it loops back to one place. Nvidia funds open AI. Open AI pays Oracle. Oracle buys Nvidia chips. The money makes its rounds throughout the industry, and eventually comes home to Nvidia, and every time it passes a hand, it gets counted again as somebody's brand new demand. So a lot of what looks like a booming market is really just the same money going around in a circle.
