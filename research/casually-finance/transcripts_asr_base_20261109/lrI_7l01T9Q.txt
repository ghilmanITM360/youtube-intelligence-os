The stock market just hit its 23rd record high this year. Home prices are higher than ever before, and net worth in America has never been larger. By the numbers, this is one of the richest moments in the history of the country. But at the same time, consumer sentiment toward the economy has fallen to its lowest level ever recorded. It's lower than the early 80s recession, lower than the 2008 global financial crisis, and lower than during the pandemic lockdowns. So there's a clear disconnect between what the data says about the economy, and what people say they're experiencing in their day-to-day lives.
