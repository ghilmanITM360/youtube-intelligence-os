Inflation in this country used to move like a pendulum. It would swing up, swing back down, and eventually come to rest around that 2% number. You never questioned it because you never had to. Economist even had a name for this period of American history, the Great Moderation. From 1985 to 2007, growth was steady, inflation was low, and the whole economy looked like it regulated itself. Except it didn't. There was no invisible hand. There was a real one. Someone standing beside the pendulum, giving it a firm push back toward the middle every single time it swung too far. Done so reliably for so long that everyone forgot the pendulum was being pushed at all. That's what the last 5 years are actually about. The thing doing the pushing is now the one in trouble.
