This year, the economy grew 2.1% in the first quarter, 1.5% in the second. Bessent's response to this is patience. He argues that America is on the cusp of a large productivity gain from AI investment, and his guess is, we start seeing the benefits in the next six months. But the reality is, the strategy of having patience and waiting for AI to save us makes zero sense. While productivity gains from new technologies genuinely do lag, patience only works if conditions are about to get better, and conditions right now for growth are about as good as they have ever been. Business spending on equipment and intangibles is up around 9% over four quarters, the fastest since 2021. Equipment alone rose 13.6% in the second quarter, and the largest pieces of that are computers and software, which leaves two possibilities. Either the money hasn't reached the output numbers yet, and Bessent is right to wait. Or 1.8 isn't the floor. It's the ceiling.
