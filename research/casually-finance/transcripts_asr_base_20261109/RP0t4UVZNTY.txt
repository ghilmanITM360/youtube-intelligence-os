Reserve currency status might sound boring, like it's something that only matters to Wall Street bankers who snack on nicotine pouches and live in $4,000 soho apartments, but in reality, it's actually the invisible engine behind the United States economy. And that's because being the reserve currency is like being the backbone for the global financial system. It means that every other country needs your currency to do business. Basically, if countries want to buy oil, settle trade, or pay international debts, they likely need dollars to do so. So countries are forced to buy US bonds and hold dollars in their reserves, which means America has access to the cheapest capital on earth, and its estimated the US saves more than $200 billion annually just from this arrangement. It's the greatest financial cheat code in human history.
