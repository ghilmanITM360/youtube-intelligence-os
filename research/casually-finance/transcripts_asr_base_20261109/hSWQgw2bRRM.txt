So what about using Bitcoin as a store of value then? And at first glance, the argument seems reasonable. It has a fixed supply and historically assets with limited supply, like gold, have often been used as a store of value. And to that, I say, no. And there's two reasons why. The first, stability. A store of value needs to reliably preserve purchasing power. And Bitcoin has historically been one of the most volatile assets in the world, which is exactly why you don't see anyone dumping their entire net worth into Bitcoin. Because an asset that can swing 20% in a single month isn't reliable. That's a level of volatility where businesses, households, and individuals start to treat it as a speculative investment, rather than a true store of value.
