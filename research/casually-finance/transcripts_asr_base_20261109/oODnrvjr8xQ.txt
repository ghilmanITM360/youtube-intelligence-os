The personal savings rate has dropped to about 2.6 percent near the lowest level on record, and down from 5.5 percent a year ago, and it gets much worse. Economists at the Bureau of Labor Statistics and the Bureau of Economic Analysis ran the numbers on savings across the income scale, and they found that for the bottom half of the country, more than 65 million households, the savings rate is actually negative, which means the bottom half of America is spending more than they bring in after taxes and running a loss every single month. So it's not just that they're missing a safety net. It's that they're sinking deeper and deeper every month. That's the divide. The rich are pulling away, while everyone else is borrowing to keep their heads above water. It's the same country, but completely different Americas.
