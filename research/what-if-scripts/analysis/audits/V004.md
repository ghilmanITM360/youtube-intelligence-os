## K. Strengths and weaknesses

- **Strengths.** (1) The inverted hook, which spends the climax and promises more. (2) Organisation by timescale. (3) Running character gags (Andrew to blame, the Moon as a friend).
- **Opportunities.** (1) ¶17 (spray heating) could be shortened. (2) The South Pole comic beat could be called back at the six-month-day reveal. (3) The question's detail (atmosphere retained) is stated only in ¶1 and is never re-flagged as the cause.
- **Distinctive.** "Death first, interesting second."
- **Risk.** Density at ¶17 (Speculative).
- **Lesson.** If the obvious answer is huge, give it away and promise something stranger.
