## K. Strengths and weaknesses

- **Strengths (Obs.).** (1) A false final answer followed by a dark-horse winner. (2) The early-eliminated category returns as the winner. (3) An elegiac final image.
- **Opportunities (Suggestion).** (1) The opening has no second-beat twist. A tease such as "the answer involves our most dangerous invention" would open the loop. (2) The middle rungs are formulaic. (3) Hydro (¶22–24) does not clearly beat wind, which slightly breaks the ascending order.
- **Most distinctive technique.** The false final answer.
- **Biggest risk.** A flat opening and a list-like middle (Speculative).
- **Lesson.** Rank candidates in ascending order, crown one, then overturn the crown.
