## B. Opening hook forensics

- **Obs. (High).** After the attributed question (Trevor, ¶1), ¶2 states a proverb (lightning never strikes the same place twice) and ¶3 flatly contradicts it ("'They' are wrong"), adding a dark joke about believers being filtered out.
- **Hook types.** Question Hook, plus a **Myth-Bust / Contradiction** hook, plus Dark Humour.
- **Construction.** It borrows a universally known saying and attacks it. That creates instant authority and comedy, and it plants a frame to be paid off at the end (Fn., Medium).
- **Withheld.** The strike-site answer, which arrives at ¶8–11. The script first detours to power harvesting (¶4–6).
- **Weakness (Obs.).** ¶4–6 (harvesting lightning power) delays Trevor's actual scenario by three paragraphs. This is the longest pre-question detour in batch 1.
