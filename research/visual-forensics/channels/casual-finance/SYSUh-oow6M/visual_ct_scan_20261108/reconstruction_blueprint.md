# Reconstruction blueprint - original asset system derived from SYSUh-oow6M
New ORIGINAL assets and motion only. No source screenshot, footage, logo or character drawing is copied; third-party items (`IMG_*`, `ICON_*`) are replaced with licensed or self-made equivalents and your own data. Timings at 30 fps, 1080x1920.

## Global system
- Canvas `#fcfcfc`; one small centred content cluster in the middle band of a 9:16 frame (content rows ~640-1250 px of 1920); a burned-in caption line below it (rows ~1250-1440); empty white above and below.
- Style: flat 2D hand-drawn stick figures and icons plus small embedded charts, infographics and report screenshots; red hand-drawn ellipses/arrows as annotation accent.
- Layers: canvas, character/icon cluster, chart or screenshot plate, annotations (ellipse, arrows), caption.

## Reusable modules
1. **Caption track:** separate text layer changing about every 0.37 s (238 measured changes), growing word by word; lag vs speech about zero.
2. **Stick-man reset:** the same plain stick-man plate returns as a neutral beat between evidence plates (cuts at f96, f588, f765, f1371, f1527, f2160, f2338; pointer variant at f857) with 1-3 arm/expression pose swaps while it holds.
3. **Pop-in build-up:** props appear one by one on single frames at speech beats (banknote piles, house, cigars, yacht; car, house, money bags; list lines 1-3; houses 1-3; factories 1-5; slope figures one by one) - 35 of 79 states.
4. **Evidence plate + ellipse:** small infographic/report screenshot cut in, then a red hand-drawn ellipse drawn on the key figure (f2093, f2568).
5. **Word split:** MILLIONAIRES splits into MILLIO / NAIRES moving apart in about 12 frames.
6. **Decade timeline scroller:** decade labels and red arrows scroll left to rest on 2020s.
7. **Black beat:** a 47-frame full-black cut (1.57 s) at a speech pause (f1480-f1526).
