## Modules (original rebuild; derived from measured patterns)
1. **Text-card beat**: small black rounded-handwriting text centred on white ('2025', 'The best part?', 'The Worst Trade Ever', chapter-style headings) for 1.2-4 s, hard cut in and out, no motion.
2. **Numbered question device**: three questions shown as a list, then each question returns as a heading ('1. WTF is going on?', '2. Why is this garbage working?', '3. How does this all come crashing down?') above a stick-man gag.
3. **Pop-in chain**: logo or subject, then arrow plus next element, then arrow plus next element, each appearing in one frame with zero tween and persisting until the next cut (MicroStrategy to bond card to photo to Bitcoin; label chain Convertible Bonds to Stock to Dilution).
4. **Icon trio with brace**: three fixed icons (pension fund, mutual funds, green bank) reused under a brace with a Bitcoin coin that is checked, crossed out or equated; reuse the same asset set across the video.
5. **Stick-figure bubble scene**: stick figures with one-line bubbles in a kneeling-versus-standing exchange; bubbles pop in one frame.
6. **Third-party insert slot**: photographs, article clippings, stock-quote screenshots, movie posters, logos and meme charts are replaced with licensed or self-made equivalents and your own numbers; never copy the source items.
7. **Dark screenshot card**: a full-frame dark data screenshot for 1-6 s, with a text or highlight overlay added by a single-frame pop-in.
8. **Flat grey interstitial**: a flat dark grey frame of 49-75 frames (1.6-2.5 s) is used three times; its purpose is unresolved, so treat it as optional.
9. **Cut cadence**: aim for median about 4.4 s, range 1.2-13.2 s; build long shots (up to 13 s) by pop-in stacking text lines.
10. **Codec note**: do not reproduce the MICRO states; they are keyframe refreshes of static content (INFERRED).
11. **End card**: 'Subscribe for more Casual Finance', platform logo with crossed-out alternatives, button, smiling character leaning on the button; hold about 5.4 s.
