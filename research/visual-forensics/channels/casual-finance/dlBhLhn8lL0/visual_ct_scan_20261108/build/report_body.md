## 8. Uncertainties and limits
- ASR is Whisper small.en; nobody listened. Of 72 proposed phrase pairs one found no matching word and 22 with a visual-word lag above 1.5 s were dropped as probable mismatches, leaving 49; this biases the reported lags toward small values. In this densely narrated video an unrelated time already falls within 0.1 s of some word start 47 % of the time (82 % within 0.3 s), so closeness alone is not evidence; only the semantic match and the consistent lead are.
- The purpose of the three flat grey frames (S033, S044, S077) is unresolved; all three runs are flat within +-1 and share mean luma 82.0, so they are the same grey; the two decoders disagree on its absolute value (82 vs 84) because of range conversion.
- Video ID attribution is the existing queue attribution (the filename carries no ID); live YouTube was not re-checked.
- Quiet frames inside holds are measured unchanged but not individually viewed; sub-threshold changes cannot be excluded. Small on-screen text (article bodies, stock tables, definition cards, holder rows) is not transcribed.
- Photographs of real people are not identified; on-screen and narrated claims (prices, premiums, percentages, headlines) are third-party or narrator content, not fact-checked.
