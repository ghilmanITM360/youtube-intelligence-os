## 7. Attention hypotheses (INFERRED, no retention or CTR data)
1. A new visual state appears every 1.27 s on average and a hard cut every 2.7 s, so there is rarely a long unchanged picture; narration of about 187 words per minute (1,585 ASR words) is echoed by on-screen text, so a viewer could follow with sound off (hypothesis).
2. In 75 of 88 selected phrase pairs the picture changes before the spoken word (median lead 0.32 s); a visual that is already waiting when the phrase lands may reinforce the claim, but the pair selection is mine and the lead may partly reflect ASR timing.
3. One indicator per visual vignette (a prop that changes) makes a dense statistical argument easier to follow; the later constellation scene (stars, dashboard) packages the earlier points as one image.
4. The only audience request appears at f11681-f11848 (SUBSCRIBE and COMMENT cards) after the argument closes, with the end card held for the final 6.0 s (f12195-f12338).
