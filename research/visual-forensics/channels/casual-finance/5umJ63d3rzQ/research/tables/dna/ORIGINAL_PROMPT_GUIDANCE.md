# Original prompt guidance for new YIOS videos — CREATIVE_INSTRUCTION (not source observation)
Derived from structural patterns of Video 003 (MEASURED/OBSERVED in PRODUCTION_DNA.md). Nothing here describes or reproduces source artwork, characters, captures, narration or sequence order. Use a new script and a new visual style.

## Composition (per beat)
- One composition per sentence or claim; new composition roughly every 2-3 s for claims, longer holds only for evidence you must read.
- One focal subject, supporting elements revealed in reading order. Keep text short and large.
## Editable layers
- Export every beat as: base, one layer per reveal element, text layer, pointer/progress layer (if used), licensed-capture slot (if using any third-party footage, and only with rights).
## Motion
- Default: hard cut in, additive pop-ins on a held base, in-place pose or text swaps. Use draw-on for charts and lines. Use zoom or fade rarely.
- Start a new composition on a narration pause when possible (measured tendency in the source: about half of group starts sit inside silences; correlation only).
## Prompt template (image generation)
"Original {style} illustration for beat {BEAT}. Concept: {CONCEPT from your own script}. One clear focal subject, supporting elements arranged for reading order, flat fills, plain background, separate editable elements. No existing characters, logos, or captures."
## Prompt template (animation)
"Hard cut in at beat start. Element N appears instantly at {frame offset} after the cut and holds. Text swaps in place. No tween unless the beat is a chart draw-on."
