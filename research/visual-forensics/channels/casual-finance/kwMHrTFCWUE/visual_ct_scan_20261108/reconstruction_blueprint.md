3. **Pop-in stacking**: elements appear in a single frame with zero tween and persist until the next cut; build a shot by adding 1-3 pop-ins over 1-4 s.
4. **Bubble rig**: bubble pops in with a curved tail line; text replaced by single-frame swaps; bubble removed together with a pose swap.
5. **Blue board scene**: pale-blue background, black bed-frame board with text lines or a chart; use for list beats (three lines at 1397, 1416, 1465 steps of 19-49 frames).
6. **Text-card punchline**: centred text on white for 0.8-3 s (23-90 frames) as a beat before the next gag.
7. **Third-party insert slot**: posters, photos, screenshots, packs and logos are replaced with licensed or self-made equivalents and your own numbers; never copy the source items.
8. **Cut cadence**: aim for median 4 s, range 0.8-10.7 s; hold the longest shots (7-10 s) for dialogue exchanges.
9. **Codec note**: do not reproduce the MICRO states; they are keyframe refreshes of static content (INFERRED).
10. **End card**: subscribe text, platform logo with crossed-out alternatives, button, smiling character leaning on the button; hold about 7 s.
