# Visual DNA

## Observed global rules

- **Characters:** black, loose hand-drawn stick figures; circles for heads; no filled skin or clothing. Character scale changes only with composition changes.
- **Environments/objects:** white paper-like field with thin black line drawings. Cave scenes add ice overhangs, snow ground, and sparse dots. Scientific inserts use crisp diagrams, whiteboards, black field, or light gray sun-edge gradient.
- **Palette/lighting:** near-white ground, black lines, dark charcoal backgrounds, and sparse signal red annotations. A soft gray-white edge glow appears only in Sun/heat inserts.
- **Camera/composition:** locked full-frame 16:9 compositions. Cuts or panel replacement create most changes; no evidence of decorative camera drift, parallax, or depth of field.
- **Typography/graphics:** hand-lettered all-caps display labels and thin technical text. Exact words should be added as editor text where legibility matters. Red arrows, red “BAD” labels, and red crossed-out values are semantic emphasis.
- **Motion language:** drawings appear through short holds, hard cuts, panel swaps, simple translation, and incremental write-on/reveal states. Static drawings stay static after arrival.
- **Transition language:** hard cuts predominate. S03/S13 use a three-panel or doorway composition; S08 deliberately alternates black and white panels. S02/S14 are red identity/end cards.
- **Asset reuse:** C01 lecturer and E01 board recur; E02 cave and C02 explorer recur at opening and close. The Sun-edge gradient and C03 silhouette recur in comparison/exposure inserts.

## Scene-specific exceptions

- S02 and S14 replace monochrome illustration with solid red branded card.
- S08 alternates high-contrast black/gray and white technical panels; preserve cuts rather than blending them.
- S11 adds anatomical X-ray-like line art and a red warning placard to otherwise whiteboard staging.
