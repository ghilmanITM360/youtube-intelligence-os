# WAY 2 — Source image prompts

These are reusable source-image specifications, not claims that images have already been generated. Generate at 9:16/1080×1920 or a higher matching ratio, then register to the evidence. Text and diagram labels are not baked; source-derived overlays preserve exact wording. Reuse generated end frames only where the preceding state actually changes; never restart later actions from an unmodified base.

## Continuity masters

- CHARACTER: C02 uses blank oval head and simple stick limbs; retain scene-specific pose. C03 always keeps the same ponytail and headset shape. C04 remains a held running pose. No invented eyes/clothes or breathing.
- ENVIRONMENT: off-white flat field; only the recorded gray/dark/outdoor exceptions. No parallax or environment movement.
- OBJECT: retain desk/chair geometry, laptop angles, and measured book counts. Cables and socket panels are separate matched variants.
- STYLE: 2D irregular black ink, white interiors, limited gray fill and muted-red annotations. Original font/software/model/source library UNKNOWN.

Exact overlay wording, alignment and timing are in the canonical TIMELINE.csv and A-ID map. Image prompts below independently contain their own appearance specification. Tiny source text that cannot be read confidently is preserved as an evidence crop rather than invented.
