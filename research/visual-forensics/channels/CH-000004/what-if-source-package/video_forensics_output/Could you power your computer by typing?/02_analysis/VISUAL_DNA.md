## Evidence and measurement conventions

Frame numbers are authoritative at 24 fps; millisecond times are rounded. Coordinates throughout are normalized top-left percentages unless expressly given in source pixels. A source-aligned 1080×1920 full-frame overlay at 100% scale is the simplest exact registration. In CapCut a center-origin position of (0,0) corresponds to full-frame center; do not mistake normalized source coordinates for inspector units.

A state can be a stable hold or a meaningful *reveal phase*. Continuous per-letter changes are grouped by word/line or strike, not inflated into hundreds of states. Motion endpoints may overlap the first complete-state frame. Instantaneous appearances occupy one frame in the motion map, followed by a hold in the timeline. Hard cuts are recorded in the timeline and are not counted again as object motion events.

No full-video dense image dump was needed. All 2,419 frames were decoded for change screening; sparse full-duration visual review plus adjacent change-pair review and targeted dense S07/text/fade sampling establish the sequence. Compression-only changes on held plates were excluded. Microvariation below this sampling/codec resolution is not treated as an intentional effect.
