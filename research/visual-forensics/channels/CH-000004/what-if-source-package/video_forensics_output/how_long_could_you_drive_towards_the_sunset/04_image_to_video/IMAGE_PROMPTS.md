## Reuse decisions
- S14 does not get its own source image: it reuses IMG03's exact plate and character silhouettes verbatim as a deliberate visual bookend to S03, differing only in caption overlay content (T04 dialogue instead of T01) and the added swirl motion graphic (A30) — regenerating a near-identical image would duplicate an asset the reference itself reuses.
- Every other shot (S01, S02, S04-S13) requires its own unique image because each has a substantially different background, composition, or environment from every other shot in this video — unlike more repetitive explainer videos, this one rarely revisits the same setting twice, so image reuse here is limited to the one genuine callback (S03/S14).
- IMG07 and IMG08 intentionally share the same sky-gradient illustration language (described identically in both prompts) to preserve visual continuity between the two "real-world location" shots, even though they are generated as two separate images due to their different foreground content (ice+seals+tractor vs. mountain-ridge+car).
