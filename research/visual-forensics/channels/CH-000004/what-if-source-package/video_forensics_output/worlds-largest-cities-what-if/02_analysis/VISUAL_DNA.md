# Visual DNA (global rules — see TIMELINE.csv for per-state detail)

## CHARACTER DNA
- One recurring stick-figure narrator (C01): circle head, no facial features, single-line limbs, holds a paper/clipboard. Appears only in S01 (0–4.42s), static pose throughout.
- Two decorative sticker vignettes (G04, G05) contain their own tiny line-art figures (mountain/landscape scene; two seated characters), each used exactly once, unrelated in style to C01 (simpler/rounder, no shared model).

## ENVIRONMENT DNA
- S01: a hand-drawn classroom blackboard on an off-white page background.
- S02: one continuous oversized hand-drawn world map (E01) — the entire back 79 seconds of the video is a camera move across this single asset, not a sequence of separate map graphics.

## OBJECT DNA
- Globes (O01/O02) are line-art, gray/white, appear only in S01.
- City markers (G02) are a single reused dot+label template applied 20 times.

## COLOR PALETTE (E01, the map)
- Teal — open ocean.
- Cyan — inland/flooded water or a shallow-sea band.
- Gray — exposed seabed / bare rock.
- Yellow (pale) — arid/desert climate band.
- Green — vegetated/temperate band.
- Magenta/purple — city marker dots (G02), the only saturated accent color against the muted map palette.
- Red — the one hand-drawn annotation circle (G03), the only other saturated accent color, used once for emphasis.
S01 (board scene) is grayscale/off-white only — no color appears until the cut into S02.

## LIGHTING
Flat, uniform, no shading/gradient beyond the hand-inked line work and flat color fills. No day/night or light-direction cues.

## CAMERA / FRAMING
- S01: locked-off, no camera movement.
- S02: a single moving crop window over one large map image. Movement alternates between FAST blurred pan/push bursts (the norm — 8 of 9 transitions) and one slow unblurred pan (A08, ~48–55s). No rotation, no parallax, no depth-of-field change anywhere in the video.

## COMPOSITION
9:16 vertical throughout. S01 stacks title card (top) / illustration (mid) / character (bottom). S02 has no persistent UI chrome — the map fills the frame edge-to-edge; city labels sit directly on the map near their dot.

## TYPOGRAPHY
- Title card (T01): black serif, two centered lines, on a rounded gray card — S01 only.
- Map labels (continent names, city names): hand-lettered look (uneven baseline, marker-style strokes), black, no card/background — S02 only, baked into or overlaid on the map.

## GRAPHICS / ILLUSTRATION STYLE
Uniform "hand-drawn doodle/whiteboard" style across both shots and both sticker vignettes: black ink outlines, minimal or flat color fill, no gradients, no 3D shading, no photographic elements anywhere in the video.

## MOTION LANGUAGE
Two motion types only, observed nowhere else:
1. STATE_REPLACEMENT (object pop-in/swap) — used for O01→O02 and every G02/G03/G04/G05 appearance.
2. PAN/PUSH with motion blur on the fast bursts, unblurred on the one slow pan (A08) — used only for the S02 camera moves.
No zoom-breathing, no parallax, no character motion (C01 is static throughout its whole appearance), no particle/glow effects anywhere.

## TRANSITION LANGUAGE
- S01 internal (F01.0→F01.1): straight content swap on the same locked composition, no visible wipe/fade.
- S01→S02: the only hard, blurred "whip" transition in the video, corroborated by a ~0.35s audio near-silence dip at the cut point.
- All S02 internal "cuts" (the scene-detector hits at 8.83/18.0/25.58/35.42/41.92/65.29/72.38s) are NOT shot changes — they are fast-pan motion-blur bursts across the same continuous map asset E01. Treat them as camera moves, not edits.

## ASSET REUSE PATTERN
E01 (the map) is the backbone: generated/drawn once, reused for the entire second half via camera movement only. G02 (city pin+label) is a single template reused 20 times. Everything else (T01, C01, O01, O02, G03, G04, G05) is single-shot/single-use.
