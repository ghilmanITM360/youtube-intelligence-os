# WAY 2 — Image Prompts

15 unique source images (IMG01–IMG15) cover all 9 shots. This video has a low asset-reuse profile (see `../02_analysis/VISUAL_DNA.md`) — nearly every shot introduces a bespoke diagram, so 13 of 14 possible reuse opportunities were not applicable; every image below is independently generated. TEXT BAKED defaults to NO throughout; exact wording is added as an editor overlay during final assembly unless noted otherwise.

## Master continuity references
- **C01 (narrator character):** minimalist black-outline stick figure — a single perfect circle for a head with two small dot eyes and a simple curved-line mouth, no hair, thin single-stroke arms and legs, no fill color, hand-drawn/slightly wavering line quality.
- **C02 (aside character):** identical stick-figure body language and line style to C01, but with a scribbly/wavy short-hair outline on top of the head as the one distinguishing feature.
- **Global style:** loose hand-drawn ink/vector line-art illustration, flat 2D, no photographic or 3D-rendered elements, no camera perspective/depth-of-field; black strokes on either a plain white or plain black background, with red reserved exclusively for annotation/rejection marks and aside captions.

## Reuse decisions
This video's fast, joke-per-beat structure produces almost no visual repetition between shots (see `../02_analysis/VISUAL_DNA.md`, Asset Reuse Pattern), so no image is reused verbatim across shots. Two internal first/last-frame pairs are used instead of extra intermediate images, maximizing reuse *within* a shot: IMG09→IMG10 (S06's zoom-to-black-hole payoff) and IMG14→IMG15 (S09's closing circle-growth payoff). IMG03→IMG04 and IMG05→IMG06 are similar within-shot pairs used where the composition changes too much for one image to cover the whole shot via animation alone.
