# Visual DNA

## Observed global rules

- Hand-drawn, thin black line illustration on near-white field; gray pencil-like hatching fills Earth, objects, and shadows.
- Stick-figure presenter/people recur. Geometry is flat, centered or side-by-side, with broad empty space.
- Time labels occupy a black strip at top on many explanatory scenes: `T = ...` in small white uppercase text.
- Important numerical change uses red handwritten-style text, red underline, or red arrow. Most explanatory text is black uppercase hand lettering.
- Camera is normally locked, full-frame 16:9. Major changes are hard cuts; whiteboard composition is reused. No observed decorative parallax, bloom, particles, or continuous camera drift.
- Motion language is sparse: draw-on/reveal, object/graphic replacement, simple translation, scale change, sequential collapse, and occasional diagram line growth. Static holds are common.

## Exceptions

- S02 and S39 use flat red branded title/end cards with black illustration and white text.
- S37–S38 use a black space field, grayscale Earth/Moon, thin orbit lines, and white satellite drawings.
- S03 briefly uses a dark cave/opening inside a whiteboard drawing.

## Reconstruction consequence

Keep backgrounds locked. Build exact text as editor overlays, not generated-image text. Treat source-specific artwork, fonts, music, editor, model, lens, and stock source as UNKNOWN.
