# Visual DNA

## Global observed rules

- **Character DNA — OBSERVED:** thin, black, hand-drawn stick figures; occasional red outline/speckled disintegration state. One presenter figure sometimes carries a paper or speaks via curved callout line.
- **Environment DNA — OBSERVED:** sparse diagrammatic spaces: white paper-like fields, charcoal night fields, black silhouette ground, grey midtones. Buildings, machinery and props are ink outlines with deliberately uneven strokes.
- **Object DNA — OBSERVED:** simple, high-contrast technical icons; many have no shading beyond grey fill. Nuclear/cesium items add cyan emission; failures use red detail.
- **Palette — OBSERVED:** near-white, black, cool greys; red is alert/disappearance/accent; cyan is radioactive/energy glow; end card is flat red with black and white.
- **Lighting — OBSERVED:** localized soft white pools for lamps and night scenes. Industrial daytime frames are flat white. Cyan objects have a soft outer glow. No photorealistic lighting.
- **Camera/framing — OBSERVED:** locked orthographic-like 2D view. No evidence of camera pan, dolly, perspective parallax, or lens behavior. Reframing occurs by hard cut or a graphic wipe/slide.
- **Composition — OBSERVED:** left-to-right explanatory staging; large negative space around diagrams; panels divide comparisons. Text is placed in clear unused space, usually top or center.
- **Typography — OBSERVED:** hand-lettered, black, mostly all caps. Exact font unknown. Exact readable labels should be editor text, not image-generated text.
- **Graphics — OBSERVED:** black callout lines, speech lines, warning symbols, panel borders, simple graphs and white/grey cards.
- **Depth/style — OBSERVED:** flat 2D line illustration with silhouette layers; no observable 3D material or depth-of-field.
- **Motion language — OBSERVED:** sparse sequential substitutions: lights extinguish, people convert to red particles, icons/cards appear, panels reveal, decay texture grows, rain overlays, and a final object splits. Static holds dominate.
- **Transition language — OBSERVED:** hard cuts dominate. Some darken/fade-to-black, lateral reveal/slide, and simple element pop-ins occur. No evidence of decorative motion between every state.
- **Reuse — LIKELY:** recurring visual vocabulary (same stick figures, pylon, wind turbine, lamp glow, warning signs, handwritten text treatment) is reused, but source files are UNKNOWN.

## Reconstruction constraints

Use 24 fps, 16:9, locked framing, hand-drawn black outlines and off-white/grey fields. Red and cyan only where timeline assigns them. No global film grain, camera shake, parallax, bounce, or ambient particles. Any live texture in rain/disintegration must be limited to its assigned A-ID.
