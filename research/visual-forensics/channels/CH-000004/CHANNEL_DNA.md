# xkcd's What If? visual research entry point

**Channel:** `CH-000004`, xkcd's What If?
**Scope:** 14-source historical Production Intelligence Brain, with a reviewed YIOS identity contract. This is a navigation and production-adaptation summary, not a new forensic observation set.

## What the measured corpus supports

- The Brain records 200 shots, 581 visual states and 438 motions across 14 source videos. See its [release notes](what-if-source-package/production_intelligence_brain/RELEASE_NOTES.md) and [canonical paths](what-if-source-package/production_intelligence_brain/CANONICAL_PATHS.md).
- `VISUAL_DNA.md` reports 522/581 states (89.8%) in LOCKED or STATIC camera-state families. Illustrated content often changes within a fixed frame. This is a corpus observation, not a rule for every future video.
- `EDITING_DNA.md` reports 146/200 shot entries in a cut-family category; transition fields also include free-text motion descriptions. The measured median shot duration is 7.77 seconds, with a 1.54–78.91 second range. Do not convert this distribution into a mandatory shot duration.
- `MOTION_DNA.md` records 438 motions with varied durations. No single duration band dominates.
- `PACING_DNA.md` reports 604 meaningful sentences. It does not establish a link between sentence complexity and edit timing.

These points come from [Visual DNA](what-if-source-package/production_intelligence_brain/02_production_dna/VISUAL_DNA.md), [Editing DNA](what-if-source-package/production_intelligence_brain/02_production_dna/EDITING_DNA.md), [Motion DNA](what-if-source-package/production_intelligence_brain/02_production_dna/MOTION_DNA.md), and [Pacing DNA](what-if-source-package/production_intelligence_brain/02_production_dna/PACING_DNA.md). For a specific video, cite an actual table row from `01_master_data/` and its source identity; summary statistics alone are not video-level evidence.

## Use for original production

For a new script, consider a consistent coordinate frame, staged visual changes, and a clear cut when the explanatory model changes. These are **creative adaptations** to test, not source observations about the new script. Define original illustrations, symbols, layouts, voice and motion. Keep competitor frames, characters, scripts, music and exact scene sequences out of produced assets.

Start with the [source-to-forensic map](what-if-source-package/production_intelligence_brain/SOURCE_TO_FORENSIC_MAP.csv), the [source index](what-if-source-package/production_intelligence_brain/SOURCE_VIDEO_INDEX.csv) and the [YIOS source contract](../../../../docs/WHAT_IF_FORENSIC_SOURCE_CONTRACT.md). For catalog-linked queries, use the [integration workflow](../../../../docs/WHAT_IF_INTEGRATION_WORKFLOW.md). The two SRC01/SRC02 source-to-YouTube-ID joins remain unresolved; two other verified identities remain held from catalog promotion.

## What this entry point cannot prove

Brain validator checks structure and referential integrity, not every free-text semantic claim. No corpus-wide palette or typography measurement exists in its schema. These 14 videos do not establish causal performance effects or a universal production rule. Original media remains external and requires its own byte and recovery checks. The frozen v1.0.0 release must remain unchanged.
