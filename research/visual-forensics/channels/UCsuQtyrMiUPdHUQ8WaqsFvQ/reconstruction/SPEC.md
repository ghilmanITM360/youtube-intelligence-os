# Visual grammar spec (distilled; source facts are measured medians, your art must be ORIGINAL)
Canvas: flat near-white #FDFDFD, 50 fps CFR. Black line art: thick, even outline with slight hand wobble and rounded caps (landscape ~12 px, portrait ~14-18 px at native size); flat fills only, no gradient, no cast shadow. One signature flat accent: warm yellow (e.g. #F2D42A) on the mascot, present in roughly half of all held states; red (#E8321E) only for emphasis/negation.
Mascot (ORIGINAL, required): a round-bodied snail-like or bean-like creature of your own design with dot eyes, a bulbous nose, mitt hands with finger lines; yellow scarf or shell. NOT a caveman, no beard, no spotted tunic, no headdress, no feathers.
Ink coverage: drawings are big and heavy - black ink should cover about 7-10% of the frame in an average held state (landscape) / 8-12% (portrait).
Rhythm: one idea per drawing, held ~1.0-1.3 s (median). States change by SINGLE-FRAME replacement (frame N old, frame N+1 new) in >=90% of changes. The only multi-frame transition allowed: one dip-to-white (~8 frames out, ~8 frames in) at a scene/era break.
Devices to use: scene swap to a new drawing; pop-on of an object; pose/expression swap of the mascot; word-by-word text build in rounded hand-lettered style on white, with blank space reserved for the next word (each word = one single-frame pop); a thick flat red X popped over a subject for negation; picture the key word literally (e.g. "time is money" -> clock next to coins). Optional: one blank white beat between ideas.
Ending: final joke state, held, then the video ends. No CTA, no end card, no subscribe prompt.
Layout: portrait is recomposed vertically (stack text above/below the picture), not a cropped landscape frame; keep everything inside the frame with margins.
