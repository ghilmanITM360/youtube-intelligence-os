## Channel grammar with evidence limits

The 16-source [visual DNA](https://github.com/ghilmanITM360/youtube-intelligence-os/blob/043b85bf58f84fb6a1c8981d54e3b233faa6a928/data/extracted/forensic-analysis/casually-finance/2026-10-12/04_PRODUCTION_DNA/VISUAL_DNA.md) describes sparse outlined characters and financial props alternating with denser charts or source excerpts. [Motion DNA](https://github.com/ghilmanITM360/youtube-intelligence-os/blob/043b85bf58f84fb6a1c8981d54e3b233faa6a928/data/extracted/forensic-analysis/casually-finance/2026-10-12/04_PRODUCTION_DNA/MOTION_DNA.md) and [editing DNA](https://github.com/ghilmanITM360/youtube-intelligence-os/blob/043b85bf58f84fb6a1c8981d54e3b233faa6a928/data/extracted/forensic-analysis/casually-finance/2026-10-12/04_PRODUCTION_DNA/EDITING_DNA.md) describe observed reveals, cuts, highlights and state changes. Their pooled rates describe this selected corpus; they are not production targets. Audio signal and ASR alignment do not establish music, voice quality, precise sync or human listening. Financial claims need independent primary-source verification.

For an original video, map each narration claim to an original diagram and dated source. Make original characters, captions, charts and motion. Link each borrowed *principle* to a source video, timestamp or evidence row; do not reuse source artwork, dialogue, specific shot layouts or footage. Keep observed evidence, reviewer interpretation and new creative choice in separate columns. Use the [storyboard test](../../../../docs/intelligence/CASUALLY_FINANCE_STORYBOARD_TEST.md) as a small example.
