## Do not omit these owner requirements

- Not a topic summarizer; physical visual construction above literary interpretation.
- Scene/frame chronology and PTS/native-frame evidence; source hash and true sampling coverage.
- Characters/pose/facial/gaze/camera; typography/color/layers/iconography/animation timing/motion curves; actual source visibility vs guesses.

## Two necessary scientific/engineering resolutions

1. Owner's uploaded 100ms prompt requests synthetic per-2–3-second numerical retention values, while later supplied prompts prohibit fake retention/CTR. The **higher-integrity rule** wins: never invent metrics; supply source-measured retention only or nonnumeric predicted risk map.
2. 'Every frame, describe every change' on hours of footage would create impossible gigabytes of prose, and 100ms is not frame-accurate for normal frame rates. Preserve exact relevant events at original PTS/frame precision and compress unchanged intervals with an explicit temporal coverage log. Declare unverified intervals rather than implying omniscience.

## Additional limits

Exact animation project, source layers and editor software cannot be uniquely inferred from final compressed pixels. AI detection is appearance hypothesis. Lens number/style influence and color-emotion causal claims require actual source or evidence. An image-generation brief is an original creative transfer, not permission to reproduce copyright-protected source frames/characters. No file in this kit retroactively changes `VISUAL_ANALYSIS_PARTIAL` records.
