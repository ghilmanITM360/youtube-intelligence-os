# 01 — Evidence authority, provenance, safe scope and scientific honesty

## The priority stack

1. Source MP4 decoded video frame and source PTS/frame index.
2. Adjacent source frames / continuous source playback.
3. Directly heard source audio and its time positions.
4. Readable on-screen text and graphics.
5. Verified source transcription with confidence/source date.
6. Original thumbnail/title/description with acquisition date; cannot certify upload-era artwork.
7. Existing validated YIOS audited observations and frame receipts.
8. User/project descriptions.
9. Analyst hypotheses / style analogies / creative improvement ideas.

Record conflicts rather than silently replacing one with another. A model ASR transcript does not establish that someone *listened*. A screenshot cannot establish audio or motion. A still of a 3D style does not prove native 3D assets, exact software, or any AI model was used. Video evidence does not measure viewer gaze, emotion, neural reward or Studio metrics.

## Every analytical statement has two coordinates

**Source provenance:** `Video_ID`, source path/identity, source hash, audio/video track, timestamp (precision), relevant frame(s), extraction settings, author/reviewer, verification date.

**Inference label:**
- `MEASURED`: tool-derived quantity, with method and tolerance/precision.
- `OBSERVED`: visible/audible quality with exact location; no claim of numeric measurement.
- `ESTIMATED`: approximate visually inferred quantity with stated bounds.
- `INFERRED`: interpretation of possible effect, creator intent or influence, with alternative explanation and confidence.
- `UNAVAILABLE`: inaccessible, unreadable or unverified.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`: genuinely absent in medium (e.g., lip sync without lips).

Separate `supported`, `contradicted`, `undetermined` verdict from `high`, `medium`, `low`, `speculative` confidence.

## No fake performance psychology

- Do not assert exact CTR, retention percentages, scroll-stop probability, dopamine loops, measured emotions, eye-tracking fixation order, or causal algorithm outcomes without source data.
- Can describe **salience predictions** based on motion/contrast/size/face/text placement; these are hypotheses, not gaze measurements.
- If prompt asks for “retention graph”, give a **risk/opportunity map** without invented numeric percentages, or actual owner-supplied retention graph with provenance. No fabricated simulated percentages masquerading as observation.
- If user gives an arbitrary dominance weighting (30/25/20/15/10), label it a user-defined heuristic, not validated perceptual science. Prefer element-by-element explanations and explicit weights only where defined.
- Symbolism, artist influence, intention and psych effects are interpretive; use cautious analogy, never certainty from a single still.

## Visual medium classification

Evidence tags: native footage, photographed live action, likely vector/raster illustration, 2D limited animation, 3D-style render, composited UI/chart, stock-like footage, synthetic/AI-looking image. AI versus human origin cannot be reliably determined solely from appearance. Do not impersonate independent provenance verification.

## Authorization and privacy

Competitor source material is read-only; do not publish competitor video or raw derived frames into Git. Make original reusable design principles rather than artist-identity duplication. Separate source evidence from suggested transformation. No secret credentials, private analytics or personal information leakage. Project Writer ownership must be verified independently of Git authentication.
