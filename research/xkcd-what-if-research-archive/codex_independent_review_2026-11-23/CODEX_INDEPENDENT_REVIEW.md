## Science

SC03 OPEN is defensible: flash total, energy delivered to ground, and recoverable electricity differ; household usage and original spoken wording remain unverified here. 899 kWh/month implies 106.3 MJ/day and two days 212.6 MJ, conditional on US average. SC02: 5.95337 g at constant average density, 1 cm/s, 100 Julian years, mean radius 6371 km; rounded ~6 is not literally >6, but the original article explicitly anticipates self-compression, so its wording cannot be disproven using constant density alone. SC05: both relations and two binding-energy model calculations reproduce, but seismic magnitude definitions differ and M25 is extreme extrapolation; no reliable outcome for Sun follows without a physical model. E09g: original article explicitly asserts 5 PW and double global electricity; 2012 IEA chart gives about 19,550 TWh/year or 2.23 TW average, versus 5,000 TW, roughly 2,250 times average. The discrepancy is clear for article wording, not proof of the intended correction or video audio. See reproduction log for sources and calculation. No expert review was performed.
