## Material corrections and limitations

1. **SC03 lightning — unresolved, not established error.** The audit compares 0.2 GJ (two days at its assumed consumption) with 1–5 GJ total flash energy, treating the latter as available electricity. Flash energy, energy at ground and recoverable electricity are different quantities; input assumptions and underlying original claim must be matched before declaring a contradiction. A peer-reviewed flash-energy study gives 2×10^8–7×10^9 J depending on flash type/storm stage: https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2008JD011506 . A lightning-physics review notes most energy is dissipated in the atmosphere and a small fraction reaches the strike point: https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6633/ad7bc8 . Reclassify as OPEN / DEFINITIONS AND SOURCE REQUIRED, not a proven channel mistake.
2. **SC02 constant-density Earth — numerical caveat.** The stated computation yields 5.95 g, below a literal “over 6 g”; keep the discrepancy in rounding and assumptions explicit, not simply an unqualified consistency claim.
3. **Coverage.** The report itself records 1 deep visual-only video, 108 of 719 audit shots viewed, 125 selected quantity frames, and no human-listened audio alignment or independent reviewer. The 125-frame style sample is purpose-selected; red frequency, stroke and figure measurements must not be generalized to all channel frames. Counts of machine-differenced frames are not watched frames.
4. **Holdouts.** H1–H9 contain distinct test samples, but several reuse detector families or same-model coding; H6 is expressly a stability split rather than a preregistered holdout. H9's 83/108 measures sampled detector precision, not recall or the error rate across all shots. Treat generalization as provisional.
5. **Identity and catalog.** The archive reports 92 public tab entries and 88 media matches on the original Mac; neither the live recount nor the local files can be independently reproduced from this ZIP. A public listing is not an inventory of private or unlisted items. Catalog transcript and ASR are separate evidence grades.
6. **Draft rules.** 23 ledger entries and 14 draft documents require rule-by-rule evidence tracing, sampling limits, review and original asset tests before being described as a validated production system. Preserve provenance, source rights, and independent-art requirements.
