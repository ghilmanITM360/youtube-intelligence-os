# Physics visualization playbook

DRAFT knowledge-base document, prepared inside the audit folder (not a repository branch). Every rule below carries an ID in `RULE_EVIDENCE_LINKS`/`RULE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.csv`; coverage and limits are in `WHAT_IF_RESEARCH_COVERAGE_AND_LIMITATIONS.md`. Review state: self-reviewed unless stated; no independent review exists. Rule classes: OBSERVED, MEASURED, INFERRED, HYPOTHESIS, OWNER.

Process proven on 10 claims (`07_SCIENCE_CLAIM_CHECKS.csv`): quote the line with script locator and ASR time, state assumptions, recompute with stated constants, compare with an authoritative source, record a verdict (consistent, consistent with caveat, qualified, open, discrepant, not verified; use "open" while the quantity's definition or the original source is unresolved), then mark the drawing to-scale or schematic. Results (v3, 2026-11-23, after the Codex independent review): 4 consistent, 2 with a caveat, 2 qualified (SC02: constant-density 5.953 g supports 'about 6 g', not a literal 'over 6 g', and self-compression is not modelled; SC05: relation-dependent toy extrapolation), 1 open (lightning, definitions and original source required), 1 not verified, 0 discrepant (K19). v1 had 1 "discrepant" (lightning); it was withdrawn because per-flash total energy, energy delivered to ground and recoverable electricity are different quantities (`07_SC03_LIGHTNING_REWORK.md`). Lessons: rounding on screen can disagree with narration (470 m/s vs 1,040 mph); an extrapolated law (magnitude 25) is arithmetic, not geophysics; household and lightning figures need a stated basis and a stated quantity (total, delivered, recoverable); a comparison of different quantities must not be called an error. Constants not fetched in this study are listed in `07_SCIENCE_CLAIM_CHECKS.md` and must be confirmed. This is not expert review. Ten-episode pass (2026-11-23, `07e_TEN_EPISODE_PHYSICS_STUDIES.md`): 51 recomputed rows over the directive's ten topics (19 consistent, 13 with caveat, 8 qualified, 7 not verified, 2 supplementary, 1 open, 1 discrepant as stated). Lessons: (1) compare spoken, captioned and drawn values separately, because they differed (Boston 330 m/s drawn vs 344 recomputed; molecules ~1,000 mph drawn vs "a few hundred" spoken; 6.45e15 W on the board vs "about 6 petawatts" spoken); (2) auto-caption unit errors ("paws", "matt", "terowatt") and ASR word errors ("soil" for "oil") change a physics check, so check both texts and the drawing; (3) a derived multiplier that is exactly 0.5000 is a different finding from a 45% band; (4) an original-article figure can be repeated by the video and still disagree with a contemporary benchmark (E09g: 5 PW is about 1,900 to 2,250 times 2012 average world electricity, not 2x; record the discrepancy and the units question, do not guess the intended figure); (5) a result can depend on which published relation is used (energy-magnitude 1.5/4.8 vs 1.44/5.24); (6) Episode 6 (rotated world) has no stated quantitative model, so only qualitative notes are possible.
