# Scientific animation grammar

DRAFT knowledge-base document, prepared inside the audit folder (not a repository branch). Every rule below carries an ID in `RULE_EVIDENCE_LINKS`/`RULE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.csv`; coverage and limits are in `WHAT_IF_RESEARCH_COVERAGE_AND_LIMITATIONS.md`. Review state: self-reviewed unless stated; no independent review exists. Rule classes: OBSERVED, MEASURED, INFERRED, HYPOTHESIS, OWNER.

Devices that carry a quantity (counts over 125 frames): numeric or unit label 46, mechanism diagram 30, scaled axis 13, map 11, side-by-side 8, object 8, scale-reference figure 8 (K15). A comparison rides on layout; a tiny person or a labelled bracket gives size; huge numbers are often drawn as context, not as the number. Observed changes are mostly pop-ons, scene swaps and write-ons (K16). Choose the method by idea (see the table in `08_ORIGINAL_ASSET_RECIPES.md`): static or label for ratios, split panel for before/after, procedural instancing for crowds, parametric path for geometric solutions, simulation only when the film claims numerical accuracy; add 2.5D, 3D or parallax only when the idea needs it (K20, K21). Mark every frame to-scale, schematic or partly.
