# Physics visualization playbook

DRAFT knowledge-base document, prepared inside the audit folder (not a repository branch). Every rule below carries an ID in `RULE_EVIDENCE_LINKS`/`RULE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.csv`; coverage and limits are in `WHAT_IF_RESEARCH_COVERAGE_AND_LIMITATIONS.md`. Review state: self-reviewed unless stated; no independent review exists. Rule classes: OBSERVED, MEASURED, INFERRED, HYPOTHESIS, OWNER.

Process proven on 10 claims (`07_SCIENCE_CLAIM_CHECKS.csv`): quote the line with script locator and ASR time, state assumptions, recompute with stated constants, compare with an authoritative source, record a verdict (consistent, consistent with caveat, qualified, open, discrepant, not verified; use "open" while the quantity's definition or the original source is unresolved), then mark the drawing to-scale or schematic. Results (v2, 2026-11-23): 4 consistent, 3 with a caveat, 1 qualified (relation-dependent), 1 open (lightning, definitions and original source required), 1 not verified, 0 discrepant (K19). v1 had 1 "discrepant" (lightning); it was withdrawn because per-flash total energy, energy delivered to ground and recoverable electricity are different quantities (`07_SC03_LIGHTNING_REWORK.md`). Lessons: rounding on screen can disagree with narration (470 m/s vs 1,040 mph); an extrapolated law (magnitude 25) is arithmetic, not geophysics; household and lightning figures need a stated basis and a stated quantity (total, delivered, recoverable); a comparison of different quantities must not be called an error. Constants not fetched in this study are listed in `07_SCIENCE_CLAIM_CHECKS.md` and must be confirmed. This is not expert review.
