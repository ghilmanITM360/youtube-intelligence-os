# Master visual DNA (measured style)

DRAFT knowledge-base document, prepared inside the audit folder (not a repository branch). Every rule below carries an ID in `RULE_EVIDENCE_LINKS`/`RULE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.csv`; coverage and limits are in `WHAT_IF_RESEARCH_COVERAGE_AND_LIMITATIONS.md`. Review state: self-reviewed unless stated; no independent review exists. Rule classes: OBSERVED, MEASURED, INFERRED, HYPOTHESIS, OWNER.

Observed series look, from 125 viewed science frames (quantity moments, not random): dark line on near-white (88 of 125 frames have the 248-grey ground) or white line on near-black (about 21 of 125), grey for maps, ground and bodies of water, one repeated red accent in about a third of frames (K10); thin stroke about 6 px at a 1080 px short side (K11); large negative space, 0.66 long and 0.77 Short (K12); flat, side-on staging; mostly capital hand-lettered labels with mixed-case handwriting for equations; ring-head stick figures (K13). Silhouettes and glow gradients appear in some frames, so the look is not purely line art. **Not established:** whether the video artwork matches the static blog or book illustrations (no book image was measured); line irregularity; expressions. For an original channel choose and document your own palette and stroke parameters; use these as comparison values only.
