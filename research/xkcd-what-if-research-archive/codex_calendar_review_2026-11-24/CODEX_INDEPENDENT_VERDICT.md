## Decision rule
Science plausibility and truthful packaging are gates. Among eligible ideas, prefer one clear thought experiment that pays off visually, an angle distinguishable from the closest competitor, and a realistic production dependency. Timeliness breaks ties when verified. Audience confidence is separate from science confidence. Competitor titles and lifetime views support plausible interest; they cannot isolate topic effect, CTR, retention, or forecast our views.
