<science_review>
Before recommending a title or thumbnail claim, check:
- Higher fall: comparison unit, altitude, drag, posture, atmosphere and the
  difference between peak speed and speed just before landing.
- Tiny planet and Phobos: horizontal launch, orbit vs escape, radius,
  density and terrain/surface clearance.
- Voyager: one-way signal delay, round-trip delay, current NASA mission
  status and whether a reply is actually possible at the stated time.
- Moon disappearance: location-specific tides, remaining solar effects,
  model assumptions and limits.
- Neutron-star matter: assumed density, sample volume and the explicitly
  hypothetical confinement/held-together premise.
- Lunar laser: Earth-Moon distance, round trip and reflection assumptions.
- Earth-stops-spinning backup: precisely define what stops and for how long.

Use authoritative sources and reproduce central arithmetic where possible.
No expert sign-off unless an actual qualified reviewer performs it.
Mark material unresolved issues HOLD FOR SCIENCE.
</science_review>
