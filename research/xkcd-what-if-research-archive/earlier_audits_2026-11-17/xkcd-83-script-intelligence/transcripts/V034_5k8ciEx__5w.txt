Here’s a puzzle I once heard: Imagine you tied a rope tightly around the Earth, so it was hugging the surface all the way around. Now imagine you wanted to raise the rope 1 meter off the ground. How much extra length would you need to add to the rope? Miles? Hundreds of miles? The answer is about 6 meters. Circumference is proportional to radius, so if you increase radius by 1 unit, you increase circumference by 2π units.
