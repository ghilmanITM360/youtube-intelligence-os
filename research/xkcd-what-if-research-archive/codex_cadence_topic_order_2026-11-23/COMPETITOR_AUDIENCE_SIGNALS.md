The channel universe was seeded by xkcd and public searches for animated “What If” science, hypothetical physics, space scale explanations, and animation and experiment-led science. Core comparisons need a recurring bounded hypothetical. Close comparisons share the premise or production language but have material differences. Adjacent experiments, mission explainers and science news offer mechanism or cadence contrasts. Related names that resolved to the wrong channel or language were excluded. Visible English-language, active and legacy channels are overrepresented and the sample is purposive.

Streams, explicit premieres, unknown RSS-only format and compilation-title flags are in the [cadence table](CHANNEL_UPLOAD_CADENCE.csv). YouTube Videos, Shorts and Streams tabs determine format, with public feed and watch metadata used for dates. A short runtime or tall artwork is insufficient. YouTube's current rule applies to eligible square or vertical videos up to three minutes uploaded from October 15, 2024, subject to documented exceptions. Recognizable public premieres can lose their marker and compilation flags are based on title clue. Gaps and same-day bursts are calculated only on reliably timestamped uploads within each window and can overstate gaps when intervening dates are unknown. Release times and recurring posting days cannot be inferred across an incomplete history. [YouTube Shorts classification](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877)

## Interpretation

- xkcd alternates periods of more Shorts with fewer longs. Underknown is recently Short-heavy while maintaining roughly weekly long output over 90 days. What If Animations recently shifted from publishing some Shorts to frequent longs. Lightbound's recent burst contrasts with Epic Spaceman's quiet period and a high-view older long. These are observed distribution choices across distinct audience sizes and production models.
- Questions under a shared family such as everyday physical objects, destruction scenarios, or mission communication can perform very differently. Separate recording of formats matters because every Shorts start/replay can count as a public view from March 31, 2025. A long’s registered impressions and CTR, and engaged Shorts metrics, require first-party analytics. [YouTube Shorts counting change](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/333869549) · [YouTube impressions and CTR](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154)
- Potential explanations include a pre-existing subscriber or book audience, repeat viewers, title/thumbnail, topic, series, publication age, external coverage, seasonality, promotion and craft. A quiet channel with a strong older video offers no causal frequency test. No inferred subscriber conversion, retention, unique reach, or outcome projection.
- Treat a theme-level pairing such as xkcd's digging, floating or DNA as a reason to test standalone Shorts with possible parent discovery. Sequence, intent and conversion require channel data or direct confirmation. These observations use public title/date and format metadata. Comments, transcript annotation, auditory or full-sequence inspection were outside the captured sample.
