# Unreasonably Exact — deep cadence and editorial audit

## Publishing decision

Start with the balanced pilot if there are two independently reviewable Longs in the pipeline. The data demonstrate several sustainable-looking public schedules for established properties and very different emerging schedules. At launch, production evidence should set the cap. The first 60 days are a test of questions, formats and throughput. Established-channel popularity is context only.

| Scenario | Longs per week / 30 days | Shorts per week / 30 days | Workload estimate | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative fallback | 0.5 / about 2 | 0–0.5 / 0–2 | ~10–23 hours/week | Best if one person owns science and editing. Fewer comparable first-party observations. |
| Balanced recommendation | ~0.7 / about 3 | ~0.9 / about 4 | ~17–32 hours/week | Six longs, four independent Shorts and four excerpts. Distinct family tests with a real readiness buffer. |
| Aggressive | 1 / about 4 | 2–3 / 8–12 | ~26–56 hours/week | Requires parallel review/design, reusable original systems and measured throughput. More test opportunities, less time per science and packaging decision. |

Assumptions are approximately 18–32 hours per Long, 3–7 hours per Short and 1–3 hours weekly overhead. Range combines mix and process variation. These are editorial workload estimates. Count original illustrations, source review, accessibility and verification when measuring actual hours. The initial plan uses 42 rest/production days, three designated review days and one unscheduled reserve. If a science review slips, remove the optional excerpt first, then widen the Long interval. ## Ranking and missing data

Define seven 0–3 editorial inputs before comparison. 0 is unusable or actively weak on that criterion, 1 substantial risk, 2 workable with conditions, 3 strong. Weights are originality/saturation 20, packaging 15, science 20, production ease 15, visual explanation 10, evergreen 10 and channel fit 10. Divide the weighted result by three and round to five-point bands. Independent evidence about interest, freshness, and classification dates is recorded in separate columns. Unavailable demand does not score as zero. Untested reserve candidates remain unscored. Keep confidence separate and limit it for single-source metrics, book overlap or unanswered science. Give distinct family transitions and operational readiness priority over a small editorial-band difference. A priority is a reasoned launch test. No band predicts public performance. ## Chronological priorities and strongest counterargument

| Day / date | Format and question | Opportunity and challenge |
|---|---|---|
| 1 / Dec 7 | Long. Would a Higher Fall Make You Hit the Ground Faster? | Familiar intuition, existing approved script history and a visual answer. Generic terminal-velocity coverage is crowded. Use one test body and explicitly model speed just before contact. Prior fixed-posture estimate around 200 km/h is illustrative. The prior model's roughly 186–229 km/h sensitivity under a ±20% drag-coefficient scenario does not cover all morphology, atmosphere and Mach uncertainties. Release only after a second model and qualified review. If unresolved use an independently ready kettle, otherwise slip. [NASA Glenn](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/falling-object-with-air-resistance/) · [Stratos data analysis](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0187798) |
| 8 / Dec 14 | Long. Why Can It Take Voyager Two Days to Reply? | Verified mission milestone and competitive attention. Novelty can cool before the planning start. Light-Day overlaps the same premise, so separate propagation from operations in an original timeline. Use the verified November crossing condition. Fall back to evergreen DSN engineering or kettle. [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasas-voyager-1-makes-history-reaching-one-light-day-from-earth/) · [Light-Day](https://xkcd.com/3325/) · [AstroKobi](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/SS4pQOnQ3BM) |
| 11 / Dec 17 | Short. Can You See Earth’s Shadow After Sunset? | Early proof of everyday observational scope. Eastward viewing and sky conditions need careful wording. Explicitly label the shadow and Belt of Venus. [Observing guide](https://earthsky.org/earth/when-can-you-see-earths-shadow/) |
| 18 / Dec 24 | Long. Can the Same Kettle Boil at Two Temperatures? | Distinct setting, one analyzable variable, excellent comparison visual. Appetite unmeasured and similar boiling demonstrations exist. Compare two pressure readings and a valid temperature fit. December holiday effects are unknown. [NIST](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C7732185&Plot=&Type=ANTOINE) · [Museum of Science](https://www.mos.org/video/vacuum-chamber-science-watch-water-boil-room-temp) |
| 24 / Dec 30 | Short. What Does ‘Farthest From Another Human’ Actually Mean? | Short, clear metric contrast just before orbit. There are at least three definitions across recordkeepers and missions. [Recordkeeper](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/784773-farthest-separated-humans) · [CSA](https://www.canada.ca/en/space-agency/news/2026/04/beyond-the-moon-artemis-ii-crew-reached-the-farthest-distance-humans-have-travelled-from-earth.html) |
| 29 / Jan 4 | Long. Could You Jump Into Orbit Around a Tiny World? | Strong visual distinction between straight up and tangential launch. Close small-world prior art and terrain/collision risk. Use an idealized body and ejecta solely as an analogy. [Orbits](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/13-4-satellite-orbits-and-energy) · [Bennu](https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/where-rocks-come-alive-nasas-osiris-rex-observes-an-asteroid-in-action/) |
| 40 / Jan 15 | Long. If the Moon Disappeared, Would the Ocean Still Have Tides? | Broad recognized premise. Generic title space is crowded. Use one station and a harmonic-component thought experiment. Exact transient response and uniform amplitude are unresolved. [NOAA methods](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/about_harmonic_constituents) · [Met Office example](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3sofzVzpt0) |
| 44 / Jan 19 | Short. Can Earth’s Rotation Add a Second to Your Clock? | Everyday object and single surprise. UTC vs UT1 can confuse. Use one historical second and avoid promising a future insertion. [NIST](https://www.nist.gov/pml/time-and-frequency-division/time-realization/leap-seconds) |
| 51 / Jan 26 | Long. Could a Mars Helicopter Fly on Earth? | Bounded engineering story and time for review. Test conditions support specific rotor behaviour. ## Changes to the old plan

Keep higher fall with a rewritten promise around speed and move Voyager into the first two weeks with an operations distinction. Spread out space. Delay tiny-world orbit, make the astronaut metric a Short, and introduce kettle and Earth shadow before the longer orbital/planetary run. Put Moon tides and Mars rotor after their review lead times. Park alien Earth observation until the detectable signature and instrument are specified. A known light-travel delay alone does not establish observability. Hold slow light until a specific medium/geometry separates group velocity from vacuum propagation. Hold the neutron-star mass implication and confinement premise. Retire or replace exact/near-exact broad collision variants around bulk-rain impact, steak and last human light. Leave the approved original untouched and use the workbook's side-by-side comparison and candidate rows for slot-level statuses, score, dependencies and alternatives.

Original Day 29 was conditional. At original Day 14, days 1, 3, 5 and 8 can nominally have 72 hours, with Day 11 clock-dependent. Days 1, 3 and 5 can have seven days. The latest Long then has only six days. Comparing by family at that point mixes ages and formats. The new Day 29 is an explicit science-gated slot. At proposed Day 14 compare D1 and D8 at 72 hours, only D1 at seven days. At D30 compare D1, D8 and D18 at both ages. D29 is immature. D60 has both ages for all six Longs, while the D56 Short nominally has 72 hours and needs more time for seven days. Check exact release time and analytics lag.

## First-party after launch

Log actual dates and hours at completion, duration, traffic source, registered Long impressions, CTR, watch duration and first-30-second retention. Log Short engaged views, stayed-to-watch and source separately. Save age-aligned snapshots at 72h and seven days. With tiny samples, compare packaging and promise diagnostics qualitatively and avoid declaring a winning family. Re-rank after multiple comparable tests and only increase frequency when review and production remain reliable. A strong competitor lifetime count can prioritize a question for testing.
