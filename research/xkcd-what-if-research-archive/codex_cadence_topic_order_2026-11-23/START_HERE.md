# Unreasonably Exact — cadence and topic order
Audit date 23 November 2026, Asia/Karachi.

**Recommendation.** Use the balanced pilot only when two independent long videos can clear science and packaging review. Across 60 days that is six longs and eight Shorts, four standalone and four parent-derived. Plan approximately one long every 7–11 days and one Short per week. With unknown or limited capacity, start with one long every 14 days and up to one Short per long.

**Observed cadence.** In the latest 30-day window xkcd posted two longs and one Short, Underknown’s What If three longs and 21 Shorts, What If Animations 20 longs and zero Shorts, and Lightbound seven-day uploads included seven longs. In 90 days those channels had 4/6, 12/34, 24/8 and 23/2 longs/Shorts respectively. The full tables include 22 channels, of which three are core and four are close comparisons. The rest test adjacent production or packaging models. Table values refer to public uploads visible in the audit.

**Day 1.** Retain higher fall if a scoped drag simulation and packaging claim clear review. Use the everyday kettle question if that review is late and the kettle science is ready. Move orbit to Day 29, compress Apollo record definitions into a standalone Short, retain Voyager near launch with an original operations angle, and defer alien visibility pending an instrument-specific premise. Insert Earth shadow, kettle, clock rotation and lunar laser ranging.
