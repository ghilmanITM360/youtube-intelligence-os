5. **Science and feasibility.** Verified source paths and separated reference conditions from hypothetical ranges. Each priority needs the explicit production science check in the workbook. Statements about source fit and project capacity carry different confidence.
6. **Challenge.** Searched for deliberately similar treatments and scientific failure modes. Generic competitor packaging and an urgent news rush did not survive this pass.

No expert and no independent human reviewer participated. Numeric science follows the cited model or actual source where scoped. No final production fact-check has been performed.
