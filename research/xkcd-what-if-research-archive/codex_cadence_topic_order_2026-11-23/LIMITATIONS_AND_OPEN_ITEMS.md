No reliable four-hour publication timestamps retained. No demand census for 24 hours. US Google Trends comparison, 90 days, YouTube Search, All categories, Voyager 1 / terminal velocity / boiling point / Earth shadow, returned no usable normalized readings. Lifetimes can be censored by collection time and are rounded when displayed in K/M. Age-bin medians are descriptive. Geography and audience language for the proposed channel are planning assumptions.

Tiny body orbit needs terrain and launch geometry. Moon disappearance needs a specified stationary harmonic model vs immediate ocean response. Alien observation needs a specified telescope or receiver and signal. Slow light needs regime, geometry and group-delay boundaries. Neutron-star material confinement and any real thumbnail-mass interpretation are unverified. Clock needs timing standard and historical insertion check. Kettle needs fixed pressures, valid vapor-pressure fit and clear units. Mars rotor needs NASA chamber conditions and assumptions about full vehicle. Eclipse/shadow and lunar ranging each need explicit viewing/measurement geometry. Qualified independent science review remains UNAVAILABLE.
