Exceptions: current audience demand is not numerically scored because comparable same-age evidence is incomplete. Science is documented against topic-level methods and sources separately. Assets, editing and scripts are outside this task. First-party export and analytics review are future checkpoints because the channel metrics are unavailable. This audit did not invoke the channel orchestrator with a substitute channel or register a new channel. This is an editorial backlog and publishing recommendation, and no production content plan or intelligence pipeline output was certified.

The locator and rule record passed the standalone framework checker for structure. It does not establish semantic review or channel orchestrator completion.
