No source or channel list is given in the snapshot for any of the numbers.
  - Effect: the "less than weekly" claim fits cadence B's 6 longs in 60 days, but it is an unsourced creator statistic, so it is **not** used as support. B still rests on the observed competitor cadence.

- **Related back-catalog boost after a hit** (tab 59: related older videos get a boost). Consistent with the existing idea of ordering follow-ups around one proven topic, but it is a single creator's observation.

## DS-000031: creator-strategy, audience-psychology and experimentation corpus (txt, sha256 ab555773…)
- 1,426 lines, mixed Urdu script and Roman Urdu, several separate posts and one auto-caption stub. Examples are Hollywood, boxing and Islamic stories, not science.
- **Read depth**: first ~330 lines and the competitor-analysis section (lines ~1195-1426) read in full; the middle (lines ~330-1195) read by headings and keyword search only.
- Rule class: creative heuristics and creator opinion.

### Usable ideas (heuristics, not evidence)
- **Compare to the channel's own normal ("views vs normal").** Same logic as the audit's use of lifetime views per channel baseline; it also repeats that one viral video is not a pattern.
- **Topic vs angle**: an outlier is a demand signal, not a video to copy; the next question the viewer asks gives follow-up angles. Already how the follow-up candidates were derived.
- **Story density / reveal inventory** (can you find 7-10 real turns?). A useful pre-script check for the physics candidates; applied as a check only, not a score.
- **Hit, follow-up, cluster, series.** Competitor upload sequences after a hit are worth recording. The audit's cadence data does not yet reconstruct this, so no change.
- **Diagnosis ladder** (no traffic: distribution; traffic but no click: packaging; click but no watch: content; both fine: scale) and "change one thing at a time". Consistent with the 60-day plan's evaluation slots. Method only.
- **Honest limit repeated**: you cannot see a competitor's retention graph, only infer. Same as the audit's rule.

### Not used
- 70-80% proven / 20-30% experiments, "3-5 pilot videos", "30-50 topics", "wait 48-72 hours", "20-30 topics in 3-5 days". Unsourced rules of thumb.
- Islamic-content examples and the Hollywood "downfall" angles: other niches.
- Anything presented as "YouTube's official framework" inside the posts: not checked against official docs here.
