# Slot 08 · Long · Title and packaging
*Self-review draft. No A/B tests, CTR data or audience data exist for any title below; none is predicted.*

**Original calendar title:** What Would Happen to the Ocean If the Moon Vanished?
**Recommended working title:** What Would Happen to the Ocean If the Moon Vanished?

**Three alternative titles**
1. If the Moon Disappeared, Would the Tides Disappear Too?
2. What Happens to the Tides When the Moon Vanishes?
3. The Ocean Without the Moon: What Changes and What We Do Not Know

**Viewer's expected payoff (one sentence):** The viewer learns how tides really work (a difference in pull), what the Sun would still do, what changes in timing and monthly rhythm, and what is honestly unknown.

**Thumbnail concept (text description only; no asset created):** Earth at centre with a blue ocean ring that has two large bulges; a faded outline of the Moon dissolving at one side; a small Sun glow on the other with a smaller bulge. Text (max 3 words): "WITHOUT THE MOON". Original line art; no claim written about the size of the tide.

**Why title and thumbnail work together:** Title and thumbnail both ask what happens to the ocean when the Moon is gone; the thumbnail avoids promising "no tides" so the script's answer (tides shrink and change rhythm) is not a betrayal.

**Science, ambiguity, overlap and overpromise risks**
- Ocean response, transient decay and local timing are not quantified: the script says so (paras 4, 6, 7).
- Do not narrate the S2/(M2+S2) percentages (finding D) or "12 h on every coast" (finding C).
- Moonless-Earth tilt/day-length claims are excluded (V08-C12/C13).
- Crowded topic: other creators likely cover "Moon disappears".
