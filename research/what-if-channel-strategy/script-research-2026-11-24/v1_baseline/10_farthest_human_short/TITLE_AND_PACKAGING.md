# Slot 10 · Short · Title and packaging
*Self-review draft. No A/B tests, CTR data or audience data exist for any title below; none is predicted.*

**Original calendar title:** How Far Has a Human Been From Every Other Person?
**Recommended working title:** How Far Has a Human Been From Every Other Person?

**Three alternative titles**
1. Who Has Been Farthest From Every Other Human?
2. The Most Isolated Human Ever: How Far Was the Nearest Person?
3. Farthest From Earth Is Not the Same as Most Alone

**Viewer's expected payoff (one sentence):** The viewer learns that "most isolated" is about the nearest neighbour, why the record is about the Moon's width plus an orbit height, and why the 2026 Artemis II records are different quantities.

**Thumbnail concept (text description only; no asset created):** Cross-section circle of the Moon: a small capsule icon above the top edge (far side) and a tiny lander icon on the bottom (near side), joined by a dotted line through the Moon with the label "NEAREST HUMAN?". Original line art; no portrait of any real person.

**Why title and thumbnail work together:** Title and thumbnail both ask for a distance to the nearest person; the thumbnail's through-the-Moon line is the geometry the script explains in paragraph 3.

**Science, ambiguity, overlap and overpromise risks**
- EXACT question overlap with Munroe's "Loneliest Human" (disclosed; owner editorial decision).
- Guinness does not state the altitude or method; the script labels the 2R+h reading as our estimate.
- Time-sensitive: records change.
- Three different "farthest" records could be confused (separated in paragraph 4).

**Competitor / prior-art findings (with coverage limits)**
- Munroe, What If? "Loneliest Human" (book pp.270-272, read via OCR in an earlier session) and what-if.xkcd.com/72 (extraction): the same question. No xkcd video title on it (registry titles only). Ours is an original framing: define isolation by nearest neighbour, separate three record types, explain the 2R+h geometry, and disambiguate the 2026 Artemis II records. We deliberately do not use Munroe's examples, jokes or sequence.
- Coverage limit: other creators' "most isolated human" videos not surveyed.
