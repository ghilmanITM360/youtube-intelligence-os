## Voice-over

Who has been farthest from every other human being? Not from Earth, but from the nearest person.

In 1971, Apollo 15 astronaut Al Worden orbited the Moon alone while his two crewmates were on the surface. Guinness World Records lists the distance to his nearest fellow human at three thousand, five hundred and ninety-six kilometres.

The Moon is about three and a half thousand kilometres across. Worden was probably on the far side, roughly a hundred and twenty kilometres up, with his crewmates on the near side. Add the Moon's width to his height, and you land close to the record. That is our estimate, not Guinness's stated method. Earth did not matter: everyone else was hundreds of thousands of kilometres away.

In April 2026, the Artemis II crew went farther from Earth than anyone before. But there were four of them, together. Isolation counts your nearest neighbour, not your distance from home.
