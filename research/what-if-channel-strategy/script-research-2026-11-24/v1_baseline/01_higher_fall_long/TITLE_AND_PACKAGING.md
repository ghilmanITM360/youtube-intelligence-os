# Slot 01 · Long · Title and packaging
*Self-review draft. No A/B tests, CTR data or audience data exist for any title below; none is predicted.*

**Original calendar title:** Would a Fall From Space Make You Hit the Ground Faster?
**Recommended working title:** Falling From 100 km vs 1 km: Which One Hits the Ground Faster?

**Three alternative titles**
1. 1 km vs 100 km: Which Fall Lands Faster?
2. Does Falling From Space Make You Land Faster? (We Simulated It)
3. Would a Fall From Space Make You Hit the Ground Faster?

**Viewer's expected payoff (one sentence):** The viewer learns that in a simple model both fallers land at nearly the same speed because the air sets the limit, while the 100 km fall takes about six minutes and passes through far higher speeds on the way.

**Thumbnail concept (text description only; no asset created):** Split tall frame. Left: a small suited figure at the lower third of a ruler marked "1 km". Right: a figure near the top of the same ruler marked "100 km". Between them, two identical speed dials with needles side by side and a large "?" Overlay text (max 4 words): "1 km vs 100 km". Original line art, no photograph of a real person, no printed speed numbers.

**Why title and thumbnail work together:** The title poses the two-heights comparison; the thumbnail shows the same two heights and two dials. The ambiguity in "faster" (time vs landing speed) is resolved by 0:40 in the script (paragraphs 4-5), so the click is not betrayed.

**Science, ambiguity, overlap and overpromise risks**
- "Faster" is ambiguous (arrival time vs landing speed): handled aloud in paragraphs 4-5.
- Every speed is a model output (CdA/m 0.0050-0.0078; USSA densities above 86 km recalled); script gives rounded ranges and says "simple model".
- Could be misread as a safety claim: script says physics experiment, no survival claim, heating not modelled.
- Same topic family as the owner-approved Day 1 v3 (ladder 1 m to 400 km) and Day 2 draft: this script is an alternative focused on the calendar question (100 km vs 1 km); the owner must pick one for slot 01 (see STATUS).
