- **Central question:** A radio signal travels at the speed of light — so why does it take about a day to reach Voyager 1?
- **One-sentence answer (qualified):** Light is fast, but Voyager 1 is about 25.9 billion km away — NASA says it passed one light-day on 18 Nov 2026 — so each trip takes ~24 hours; the Short makes no claim that Voyager is replying.
- **Viewer expects:** a slow signal or a weak one.
- **Viewer actually learns:** the delay is distance, not speed; a scale ladder of light-times; the delay isn't a constant.
