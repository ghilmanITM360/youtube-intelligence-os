## A. IDENTITY
- **Calendar:** Day 28, Short (50–60 s).
- **Current working title:** "How Far Has a Human Been From Every Other Person?"
- **Issue:** fine, but "farthest from every person" can be mistaken for "farthest from Earth" or "farthest apart from another person"; the Short must define the quantity first.
- **Proposed alternatives:**

- **Central question:** What is the greatest distance a human has ever been from their nearest other human?
- **One-sentence answer (qualified):** Guinness lists Alfred Worden, Apollo 15 command module pilot, at 3,596.4 km from his nearest living fellow human (30 Jul–1 Aug 1971) — about the Moon's diameter plus his orbit height — and the 2026 Artemis II records are different quantities (farthest from Earth; farthest between two crews), so they don't change it.
- **Viewer expects:** a far-from-Earth number (hundreds of thousands of km) or an Antarctic/ocean story.
- **Viewer actually learns:** the definition matters; the answer comes from geometry (2R + h); the three "farthest" records are different.

## B. PRECISE SCENARIO
- **Changes:** none hypothetical — a documented record.
- **Definition (must be on screen):** distance from one person to the *nearest* other living human (V10-C01).
- **Geometry:** the command module pilot orbits the far side of the Moon while the crewmates are on the surface; the nearest humans are the two on the surface, not 380,000 km away on Earth. At the highest point, opposite the lander: distance = 2R + h with R = 1,737.4 km (V10-C02).
- **Known vs inference:** record and definition are the record keeper's; the 2R + h reading is our geometric inference, with an altitude uncertainty (110–123.5 km across sources → 3,585–3,598 km).

## E. NUMBERS INTO IMAGINABLE SCALE
- 3,596.4 km: "about the Moon's diameter (3,475 km) plus a bit" — exact arithmetic: 2 × 1,737.4 = 3,474.8 km, plus ~121.6 km.
- 406,771 km: "about 1.06 Earth–Moon distances" (406,771 / 384,400 = 1.058).
- 419,656 km: the straight-line separation between two crews on opposite sides of Earth and Moon (Guinness) — label it a separation, not a distance from Earth.
Qualification: record keeper's values; altitude inputs vary.
