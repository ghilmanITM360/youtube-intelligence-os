## 2. Answers to the Phase 5 questions
1. **Are Day 1 and Day 4 meaningfully different?** Yes, if kept disciplined. The Long compares release heights and separates *peak* speed from *impact* speed; the Short explains one mechanism (weight vs drag, why speed can fall) with one curve. They share a model and a reviewer hold (drag), so a drag correction changes both. Risk: the Short is derivative of the Long (Claude review flagged this); it is justified as a mechanism explainer that works for a viewer who never saw the Long.
2. **Voyager Short vs Long — different enough to justify both?** The Short, yes. The Long only if beats 3–6 of blueprint 06 (cadence, a sourced real precedent, current status, autonomy) hold. As researched it risks being a stretched Short: the 2024 precedent is snippet-level and the current status is unknown. See Issue A below.
3. **Does the Phobos Short distinguish escape from the orbital Long?** Yes. The Long is a horizontal launch into orbit on a uniform toy sphere; the Short is a vertical escape on a real, named, two-body moon, with "leaving Mars" as the second step. One risk: both mention escape speed; the Long should keep escape as one rung of the ladder.
4. **Does the Moon Short have an independent payoff?** Yes: "why does the Sun still make tides" (difference of pull, 179× vs 0.46, measured S2 data) is not the Long's conclusion, and each carries its own rules.
5. **Does the Day 28 record Short still feel like this channel?** Partly. It delivers a precise number from geometry (on brand for "Unreasonably Exact"), but it is the least hypothetical item and has an exact-question overlap with Munroe (SRC-08 pp.270-272; SRC-22). Editorial decision needed (Issue B).
6. **Are several space topics in a row too repetitive?** Hypothesis only. After the two atmosphere videos (Days 1, 4), Days 7–19 are five consecutive space-themed uploads (Voyager, orbit, Phobos, Voyager, laser/Moon) and Days 22–28 are Moon/ocean/Moon-geometry. The month is almost entirely "sky + Moon". Mitigation within the locked order: keep each video's *hook* in a different domain (a number, a ground-level scene, an ocean scene), and watch first-party analytics after Day 14 and Day 28 if available. No measurement supports or refutes the concern.
7. **Are titles clear out of sequence?** Mostly. Weak: slot 5 ("a Moon" reads as Earth's Moon), slot 6 ("a reply" implies a working exchange), slot 10 (can be read as "farthest from Earth"). Clearer alternatives are in each blueprint's section A; the calendar was not edited.
8. **Is any Short misleading without a Long?** Slot 2 is misleading if it omits "from high up" (not every fall slows); slot 9 is misleading if it omits that the Moon is assumed gone; slot 5 is misleading if the gated Mars-pull beat is shown as a result. Each blueprint repeats the needed context.
9. **Can each Long support the planned length?** Slot 1 yes (7 beats). Slot 4 yes (7 beats; overlap with "Little Planet" needs a distinct opening). Slot 6 doubtful. Slot 8 conditional on keeping to the ocean-only scope (tilt/climate beats are gated).
10. **What if a parent video or a science check is delayed?** See §3.
