## Voice-over

Who has been farthest from the nearest other human being? Not from Earth: from the nearest person.

Guinness World Records lists the answer as three thousand, five hundred and ninety-six kilometres. The holder is Apollo 15 command module pilot Al Worden, who orbited the Moon alone from the thirtieth of July to the first of August, 1971, while his two crewmates worked on the surface.

Guinness does not show how it measured that. Here is our own arithmetic, not Guinness's method. The Moon is about three thousand, four hundred and seventy-five kilometres across, and Worden orbited roughly a hundred to a hundred and twenty kilometres up. If he was directly opposite his crewmates, the distance is the Moon's width plus his height, about thirty-six hundred kilometres. That is close to the record, and it means everyone else was farther away.

In April 2026, NASA said the Artemis II crew had travelled farther from Earth than any humans before. That is a record of distance from Earth. Isolation is measured from your nearest neighbour, and the four of them were together.
