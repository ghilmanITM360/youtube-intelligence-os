**Recommended title:** What Would Happen to the Ocean If the Moon Vanished?
**Alternatives (same question, different wording):**
- If the Moon Disappeared, What Would the Oceans Do?
- No Moon, Still Tides? What Would Change in the Ocean

## Five-part title check (Playbook title engine, used as a checklist)
| Part | This video |
|---|---|
| Subject | Earth's oceans |
| Situation | The Moon is instantly removed; Earth otherwise unchanged |
| Trigger | Tides depend on a difference in pull, and the Sun still provides one |
| Tension | Which parts of tides vanish at once, which fade, which stay? |
| Promise | Separate the immediate loss, the transient motion, the solar push and the local response, without inventing a number |

## Promise check
Title asks what happens to the ocean. The final paragraph answers in four parts (sea stays; Moon's stretching stops; existing motion fades; solar push continues; local height unknown). No flood/no-flood claim and no tide-height claim is made. PASS (self-check).

## Title rules applied
- One dominant question; no unqualified absolutes ("every", "only", "first", "best", "last", "real reason").
- No manufactured mystery, no fear framing, no invented statistic.
- Title and thumbnail text must not claim more than the script answers. Scorecard numbers are not used: they are not a CTR predictor.

## On-screen honesty labels (proposal, owner to confirm)
- REAL DATA: NOAA and NASA statements
- NOT KNOWN: transient decay time; local tide heights

## Description, first two lines (draft)
- What an instant disappearance of the Moon would and would not change in the ocean.
- We found no ocean model of a Moonless Earth, so no tide height is claimed.
