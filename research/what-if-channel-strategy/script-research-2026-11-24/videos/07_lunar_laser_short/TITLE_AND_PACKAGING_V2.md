## Promise check
Title asks how long: ~2.5 s (range 2.4–2.7 s) in sentence 1 and paragraph 3. The rest explains why a measurement still works. PASS (self-check).

## Title rules applied
- One dominant question; no unqualified absolutes ("every", "only", "first", "best", "last", "real reason").
- No manufactured mystery, no fear framing, no invented statistic.
- Title and thumbnail text must not claim more than the script answers. Scorecard numbers are not used: they are not a CTR predictor.

## On-screen honesty labels (proposal, owner to confirm)

- PRECISION, not accuracy

- The round trip of a laser pulse to the Moon takes about 2.5 seconds; mirror arrays make measurement possible.
- Figures are for the APOLLO station; other stations differ.
