## Promise check
Title asks 'could you jump off and never come back'. The script gives the ball-model answer (no, falls back) and says the real answer varies by location and is unsettled; it ends with the Mars-orbit point. It no longer claims a categorical 'no'. PASS (self-check).

## Title rules applied
- One dominant question; no unqualified absolutes ("every", "only", "first", "best", "last", "real reason").
- No manufactured mystery, no fear framing, no invented statistic.
- Title and thumbnail text must not claim more than the script answers. Scorecard numbers are not used: they are not a CTR predictor.

## On-screen honesty labels (proposal, owner to confirm)
- MODEL: perfect ball
- UNCERTAIN: real shape, spin, Mars's pull

## Description, first two lines (draft)
- Escape speed from Phobos in a ball model, and why the real, lumpy moon is harder to model.
- Local values reported in secondary sources disagree; see sources.
