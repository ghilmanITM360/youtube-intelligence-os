## Promise check
Title asks 'which hits faster'. The script answers both meanings (first / faster at arrival) in paragraphs 3–12 and the last paragraph says: in this model, neither is faster at arrival; the tall fall is longer. PASS (self-check).

## Title rules applied
- One dominant question; no unqualified absolutes ("every", "only", "first", "best", "last", "real reason").
- No manufactured mystery, no fear framing, no invented statistic.
- Title and thumbnail text must not claim more than the script answers. Scorecard numbers are not used: they are not a CTR predictor.

## On-screen honesty labels (proposal, owner to confirm)
- MODEL (not measured) while we are above 41 km
- MEASURED for Baumgartner 2012 and Eustace 2014 heights
- ASSUMED: constant drag shape

## Description, first two lines (draft)
- A thought experiment: two falls from rest, 1 km and 100 km up. Which arrives sooner, and which arrives faster?
- Model limits, sources and the numbers we could not check are listed below; this is not survival advice.
