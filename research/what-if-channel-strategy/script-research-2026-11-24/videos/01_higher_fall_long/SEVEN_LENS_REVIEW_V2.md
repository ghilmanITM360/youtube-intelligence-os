## Analyzer notes
- **Title analyzer:** Question + honest limit; no clickbait word; title says 100 km vs 1 km exactly as the script compares them.
- **Intro analyzer:** Intro states premise, rules and the question within 60 words; no greeting, no secret-promise.
- **Script analyzer:** Cause-effect chain present (weight, drag, thinner air, speed, thicker air, slowing); every opened question is closed.
