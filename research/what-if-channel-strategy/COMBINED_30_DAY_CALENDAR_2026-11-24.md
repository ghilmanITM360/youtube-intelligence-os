## Editorial and science gates

- Higher fall: distinguish peak speed from ground-impact speed; do not lock exact 204-vs-204 km/h numbers without independent review of drag, posture and atmosphere.
- Tiny planet: define sideways launch, orbital clearance and orbit versus escape. The Phobos Short addresses escape; account for shape and Mars's gravity.
- Voyager: the Short answers one-way delay; the Long must have a genuinely different command/reply payoff. Check current mission status and distinguish signal travel time from actual reply. If the Long cannot sustain a distinct explanation, reconsider the slot.
- Moon: focus on ocean/tidal effects. The Short independently explains solar tides and must not simply repeat the Long.
- Neutron-star Long remains a future candidate pending scientific review of the hypothetical confinement premise; it may replace the Voyager Long if cleared and the latter lacks substance.
- Farthest-human Short: verify the exact record definition and whether newer records exist before stating a name or number.
- Every Short needs a complete standalone question and payoff. The 4 Long / 6 Short cadence depends on actual production capacity. First-video completion and weekly capacity remain unconfirmed.
