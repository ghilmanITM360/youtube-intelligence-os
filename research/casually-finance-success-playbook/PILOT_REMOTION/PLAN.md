# Original mortgage pilot: design and execution record

Goal: render and evaluate a 30–45-second original finance mechanism in Remotion, with reusable SVG artwork and articulated motion. User's explicit build brief authorizes this feasibility project; one agent, no paid generators, no new catalog audit. This extends the playbook proof of concept in PR #28. Other worktrees and raw evidence remain untouched.

Design: a 42-second, 1920×1080, 30-fps composition. Nori is an original coral-clothed host with fixed warm-tan face, asymmetrical navy hair, round glasses and yellow shoes. Three body poses (shrug, point, hold) share a jointed rig and five expressions. Fixed foot anchors and two-bone leg solving avoid foot sliding. A living-room/studio kit, house, contract, lock, rate arrow, coins, chart and process diagram recur. World-camera transforms exclude captions. All transforms depend on absolute frame, with no image-to-video or accumulated mutable animation state.

Narrative: rates down / payment unchanged; U.S. standard fixed-rate contract; policy rate does not reset that contract; illustrative rate/payment chart; refinancing replaces the loan; costs/approval/term; escrow qualification; contract-lock payoff. Values are schematic, not current rates or a borrower quote. Official CFPB and Federal Reserve Consumer Help sources support claims.

Audio: local open-source Kokoro narration, original synthesized bed and effects, retained stems. No impersonation or paid generation. Segment durations are measured, then scheduled; clauses receive captions during their actual voice segments. Full human listening remains pending unless actually performed.

Implementation plan:
- [x] Verify source-description attribution and hash a bounded set of reference sources; inspect three short sequences, not the full catalog.
- [x] Build tested frame/pose/leg math, one SVG rig and modular objects; export a pose/expression board.
- [x] Generate measured narration, mix a restrained original bed/effects, wire eight scenes and narration-aligned captions.
- [x] Type-check, render sample frames, correct visible anatomy/composition/text defects, render H.264/AAC pilot.
- [x] Decode and scan final video, inspect contact sheet and sampled motion sequences, score ten craft dimensions with confidence and limitations.
- [x] Save timing, inventory, hashes, reproduction instructions and exact quality gaps; commit/push only PR #28 changes and verify remote head/CI.

Verification priorities: fixed identity across all poses; two-bone lengths/feet; full caption and voice coverage; absolute-time determinism; text safety; illustration geometry; frame-by-frame black/freeze scan; audio duration/peak/loudness; sampled temporal evidence. Automated checks do not certify human-perceived parity or publication readiness.

Result: rendered and reviewed with quality gaps. Final human listening and independent art direction remain pending; no overall parity/publication claim. Three existing source sequences total 10.2 seconds. No paid generators, subagents, watchers or raw evidence changes.
