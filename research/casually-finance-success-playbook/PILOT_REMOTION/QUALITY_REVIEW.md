# Rendered quality comparison — original mortgage pilot

**Decision: PILOT_RENDERED_QUALITY_GAPS.** The 42-second pilot proves an editable SVG character system and real frame-based animation, including articulated poses, progressive chart strokes, moving diagram elements and reused props. It does not prove equal human-perceived production quality to Casually Finance. It is a technical and creative prototype, not approved for publication.

Watch [original pilot](render/fixed-mortgage-pilot.mp4) and [10.2-second moving comparison](evidence/motion-comparison.mp4). The comparison is an actual encoded video, not an animated contact sheet. ## Comparison basis

One agent reviewed native rendered samples, 18 closely spaced pilot motion frames, 12 source frames, browser playback samples and full-frame technical scans. No independent human reviewer or full human listening pass participated. Numbers below are subjective, provisional craft judgments, not automated quality grades or population estimates. Scale: 0 absent/failed; 1 major breakdown; 2 functional with substantial defects; 3 credible with visible limitations; 4 strong with smaller corrections; 5 polished with completed critical review. Half points express judgment, not measurement precision. Do not average these into a parity claim.

Reference footage was selected from existing verified media, not downloaded again: [roe3SgezmmU 0.000–3.400s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=roe3SgezmmU&t=0) (host/chart reveal), [5umJ63d3rzQ 5.067–8.467s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5umJ63d3rzQ&t=5) (bespoke character acting), [rSa2aziEsAI 38.600–42.000s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rSa2aziEsAI&t=38) (banknote transfer/reveal). Their actual local hashes match the existing source registry. Reference pixels appear only in internal criticism artifacts; the original pilot contains none. The Short is pillarboxed, preserving its aspect ratio. Scores concern these selected sequences; they cannot establish a universal channel score. Different topic complexity, framing and formats limit direct comparison.

## Defects corrected in this build

Widened the header pill so its white text stays on dark fill; removed a shelf crossing headlines; shortened leg bones and raised hips to reduce the constant crouch; added a real contract to the hold pose; repaired a malformed SVG sweater path detected in browser diagnostics; delayed the held contract to local frames 20–26 so it does not float during the preceding arm movement; enlarged captions from 48px to 58px; fixed overlapping rows in the comparison sheet. See [inventory](evidence/asset-and-motion-inventory.json) for the record. Final comparison and QA use the corrected render.

## Human refinement needed next

1. **Acting and hands, highest priority:** redraw palm/thumb silhouettes for a credible grip; add mirrored grasp, palm-up, pointer and resting hands; add eye direction and head anticipation; direct a hold/settle after each gesture. The current left hand is partly hidden by the contract, and the character lacks torso turns. Start with pilot 4.33–5.20s and 21.27–22.13s.
2. **Custom staging:** replace at least two repeated host/card setups with an original scene-specific metaphor or close-up. The chart scene at 15.40–21.27s could use the host as an active pointer, and the refinancing scene could show an intentional hand-off of the contract. Do not import competitor drawings.
3. **Precise audiovisual timing:** listen to all 42 seconds; correct pronunciation, breath spacing and emphasis; word-align “does not reset,” “new loan,” and “taxes and insurance” before tuning reveals/SFX. Current full-text subtitle chunks use proportional timing, not ASR-certified word alignment.
4. **Small-preview editing:** shorten card subtitles, enlarge essential labels, reduce duplicate headline/caption information and move detailed citations to a description/review appendix while retaining useful on-screen attribution. Validate again at 320px and 160px.
5. **Publication gate:** independent illustrator/animator review, full listening on headphones and phone, financial wording sign-off, caption proofread and export review. Automated decode, identity-by-component and clipping checks cannot substitute for these.

## Explicit answers

- **Credible Casually Finance-level explanatory visuals?** Credible diagrams, typography, progressive reveals and reusable character identity are demonstrated. Overall Casually Finance-level illustration/acting/polish is not demonstrated by this pilot.
- **Consistency over many shots?** The same rig can maintain identity; this render proves eight layouts and fifteen exported character states. Complex side views, prop grips and long-form scene variety remain untested.
- **Smooth keyframes without text-to-video?** Yes. All artwork is SVG/React geometry; absolute-frame functions drive transforms, reveals and camera movement. The encoded motion and backward-seek check support this.
- **Human refinement?** Custom illustration, hands/anatomy judgment, acting, gaze, scene metaphors and fine timing; also listened voice/music/SFX decisions.
- **Ready for publication?** No. A working technical/creative prototype with documented quality gaps and pending human listening/art direction.
- **Scale time/cost?** See [production report](PRODUCTION_REPORT.md): estimated 40–90 person-hours for a 5–8-minute episode after the kit is refined, with explicit assumed budgeting rates rather than a price guarantee.
