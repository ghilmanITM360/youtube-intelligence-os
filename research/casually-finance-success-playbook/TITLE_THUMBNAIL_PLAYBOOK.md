## Title evaluation rubric

Score each candidate 0–2 on: identifiable subject; concrete tension; personal or societal consequence; answer delivered by the script; credible specificity; distinct wording from recent uploads. Maximum 12. Proposed gate: >=9 and no zero for truthfulness or payoff. This is our editorial gate, not a learned popularity predictor. Read it aloud, inspect a two-line mobile truncation, remove clauses that do not change the promise, and prohibit unconditional financial forecasts unsupported by evidence. A question should not imply a premise the source disproves.

Generate three paths per idea: (1) mechanism: “Why [observed outcome] persists despite [expected cause]”; (2) consequence: “The [dated change] that affects [specific decision]”; (3) story: “How [actor] created [observable outcome]”. Pick one; do not combine every curiosity device. Rewrite emotional language into a falsifiable proposition before researching it.

## Mobile-first construction and validation

Our 0–2 rubric: one focal relationship; silhouette at 160px; short readable text; contrast and hierarchy; title complement; truthful implication. Gate >=9/12 with truth >=2. This is an original QA policy. Export 1280×720, view actual 320×180 and 160×90, then test the expected feed crop. Ask three people what question the image promises within two seconds; log responses rather than declaring success. Keep words approximately 0–5 as a starting constraint, then test legibility, not an immutable limit. Remove labels that cannot be read; retain a full cited chart inside the video. Distinguish a designed Short cover from first-frame/swipe-through testing. After publishing, measure impressions/CTR or viewed-versus-swiped-away by format when Studio permits.
