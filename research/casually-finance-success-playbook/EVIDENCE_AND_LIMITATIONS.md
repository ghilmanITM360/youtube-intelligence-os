## Evidence vocabulary

- OBSERVED: saved metadata or already inspected source image, with exact reference.
- MEASURED_ASSOCIATION: calculated descriptive relation in this snapshot; no causal inference.
- INTERPRETATION: analyst reading of narration, scene or title structure.
- HYPOTHESIS: possible audience mechanism requiring an experiment or Studio data.
- RECOMMENDATION: our original production design, not the competitor's known workflow.
- UNAVAILABLE / UNVERIFIED: missing evidence or an assertion beyond the verified scope.
