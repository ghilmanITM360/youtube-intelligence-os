## 17. Strengths and weaknesses
Honest AI disclosure, real citations, freedom framing, worked examples, future pacing. Weaknesses: trap-framed SS/Medicare topics, late news, failed Shorts, shrinking length.

## 18. Replicable working model
**"You've Earned the Right"**: an age-based rule (55, 59½, 73) → permission promise → consequence-chain example → step-by-step math → next-video tease. Packaging: friendly elder mascot, relaxed, 2-3 words.

## 19. What to copy
Permission framing; age-rule topics; disclosure of AI character; official citations; next-video chains.

## 20. What not to copy
"TRAP/LOSE" packaging, Social Security claiming basics, Medicare enrolment manuals, late legislative news, Shorts that just repeat long-form facts.

## 21. Missing evidence and limitations
Title formats may have been edited after publish (neutral titles); Jan–Nov 2026 only; Shorts sample tiny.

- Not available from this dataset for any channel: CTR, impressions, retention curves, traffic sources, subscriber growth history, comments text. Hook timings are word-position estimates (untimed transcripts).
