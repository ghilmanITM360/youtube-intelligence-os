Photoreal presenter (serious, centre/right), US flag, bank/SSA documents, yellow+white headline, red bottom banner; "BORN BEFORE 1966?" qualifiers. Winners and losers indistinguishable in style → thumbnail style is a channel constant, not the success driver.

Audience qualifiers ("SENIORS:", "Born Before 1966?"), "These 3 Rules/Things", "Most … Only Know 1", "Could Freeze/Could Affect". Repetitive formula.

"Right now…" urgency + institution + mechanism in sentence one ("Right now, the Social Security Administration is holding…", "Right now, your family cannot touch a single dollar…"), then negation list ("A will would not help them. A notary would not…"), then the one fix. Strong PDF Zone 1-2 compliance.

## 13. Script and storytelling strategy
14 → 27 min; procedural (form names, steps, documents), legalistic tone; ends with next-topic teases ("…who actually pays when a scammer gets your money") in some; loop phrases absent.

## 14. Audience engagement model
Click: fear of a frozen/withheld account + birth-year identity. Stay: concrete forms and steps. Share: relevance to spouses/families. Trust: disclaimers, institution names (no links).

~17 uploads/month, ≤4-day gaps; runtime doubled mid-window. 2 near-duplicate scripts.
