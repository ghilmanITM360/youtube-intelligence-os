Long (24-34 min), dense, analytical, modelling-style ("portfolio-adjusted crossover"); calm, technical vocabulary; endings invite viewers to "run your three numbers". Few open loops, high authority.

Click: "am I enough/ahead?". Stay: precise data and personal calculation frameworks. Trust: named statistics. Risk: technical density may lose casual viewers (retention UNKNOWN).

## 17. Strengths and weaknesses
Statistical authority; clear adequacy promise; persona of someone who already retired at 55 (identity-aspirational). Weaknesses: technical rule topics, generic trap lists, philosophical titles, extreme volume.

## 18. Replicable working model
**"Enough-to-Walk-Away Data Check"**: one adequacy question → national statistic or two-option comparison in sentence one → ladder of levels/percentiles → "run your three numbers" action. Packaging: goal-state metaphor + one amount.

## 19. What to copy
Statistic-first hooks naming the source; adequacy/percentile framing; "walk-away" ladders; 62-vs-70 style numeric comparisons.

## 20. What not to copy
IRMAA-type niche rules as standalone videos; vague "This Number" titles; generic retirement-traps lists; philosophical "can it make you rich?" questions; publishing 30+ long scripts a month.

## 21. Missing evidence and limitations
Aug–Nov 2026 only; persona's real retirement status UNVERIFIED; SCF figures checked for plausibility, not re-computed; ASR errors in some numbers.
