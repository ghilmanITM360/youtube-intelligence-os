## 17. Strengths and weaknesses
Original concepts first; vivid prose; small-habit framing. Weaknesses: later imitation of others' formats, sensitive off-niche subjects, no CTAs/sourcing.

## 18. Replicable working model
**"Your Life If… (small habit)"**: one small, physical daily choice → second-person life across years → peer comparison → quiet payoff. Publish concepts first; don't chase the cluster's latest hit.

## 19. What to copy
Small-habit premises ($5/day), physical-object writing, peer-reversal POVs.

## 20. What not to copy
Late copies of other channels' hits; health/dating "every level" subjects; inherited-wealth plots.

Thumbnails not montage-inspected; fiction; first-mover inference relies on DB coverage (other channels' videos outside the DB could exist).

## 22. Final channel diagnosis
1) Wins with original small-habit and secret-success POVs; loses with late imports. 2) Advantage: prose + originality. 3) Model: small-habit life simulation. 4) Topic: everyday money choices. 5) Packaging: wordless scenes. 6) Script: literary. 7) Interest: "what if my $5 mattered?". 8) Failures: copies, sensitive subjects. 9) Learn: be first with a concept. 10) Don't copy: the cluster's latest hit. 11) Repeatable: needs writing skill. 12) Reputation: none. 13) Lesson: **in a crowded format, the first version of a concept takes the views.**
