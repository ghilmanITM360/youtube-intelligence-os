## 11. Title strategy
List titles dominate (51%): "[Number] [Habits/Signs/Things] [Wealthy/Old-School] …" often with a parenthetical qualifier "(Not Just a Big Salary)", "(Even on One Income)". Long titles (~70-90 chars). Winners include a $ amount or a felt outcome; losers lean on "Why"/"Truth"/"Mistake".

## 12. Hook and introduction strategy
Openings are aphorism or second-person scene + statistic by sentence 2-3 (Fed, Edmunds, Fidelity). Very good objection-handling in the best hook (ylv5PYLrMVM). Promise ("12 of them…", "So today I'm…") within ~60-75 words (~25-30 s) — meets PDF Zone 3. Few explicit open loops.

## 13. Script and storytelling strategy
~25 min lists; each item = mini-scene + statistic + practical move; calm tone; low but-density (0.12 — linear AND-THEN lists, weak BUT/THEREFORE causality). Endings: long comment invitations ("tell me one lesson…") and frequent next-video pacing ("the next video on the money lessons school never taught picks up right where…") — one of the few channels doing PDF future-pacing.

## 14. Audience engagement model
Click: aspiration/identity ("rich aura", "costs nothing"). Stay: countdown list completion + steady stats. Return: explicit next-video links and confessional comments. Trust: data citations + calm persona.

## 15. Upload and publishing strategy
~30 uploads/month (several per day at times) for 3.4 months, ~25 min each — industrial output from a 105k channel; near-duplicate flag on only 1 video. No gap/length effect.

## 17. Strengths and weaknesses
Strong persona consistency; reliable list template; data-anchored hooks; future pacing; aspirational packaging. Weaknesses: extreme volume of 25-min lists (inauthentic/repetitive-content risk, PDF p.555), clichéd topics, linear scripts, persona-age inconsistency.

## 18. Replicable working model
**"Calm Money Lifestyle List"**: promise a desirable feeling at zero or low cost → aphorism + objection-handling + one survey stat → 10-20 items each with a scene and a move → future-pace to the next list. Packaging: one aspirational persona scene, 2-3 words, one red emphasis word.

## 19. What to copy
Aspirational framing (calm, luxury, control) over fear; objection-handling hooks; a stat from a named survey in the first 15 s; ending next-video bridges.

## 20. What not to copy
Behavioural-science mechanism explainers without viewer action; "hidden fees" and "wealthy drive old cars" clichés; persona backstory claims that contradict the visual; publishing 1-2 25-minute lists daily.

## 21. Missing evidence and limitations
Only 3.4 months in DB; persona first-person claims UNVERIFIED; no evidence on whether volume is sustainable; subscribers is a point-in-time value.
