## 10. Thumbnail strategy
Cartoon host (black T-shirt, glasses) on white with 2-4 words, one red highlight bar. Winners: aspirational single symbol; losers: negative emotion or cluttered multi-chart. Face detector found the host in only 16-30% — cartoon misses again.

## 11. Title strategy
"[N] Signs/Milestones/Rules…" + parenthetical reassurance "(Even If You Started Late)", "(Even If You Don't Feel It)". CAPS emphasis on one word (SIGNS, MORE, THESE, LOSE).

## 12. Hook and introduction strategy
Thesis hooks ("There is a specific age where…"), matched-pair scenes (checkout line), feeling hooks ("Nobody warns you about the strange feeling…"). Weak: self-introduction openers; generic bank-app scene reused across topics.

## 13. Script and storytelling strategy
24-25 min lists; each item explained with a scenario; ends with "pick three… this week" micro-actions. Loop density 0; but-density low; long runtime padded by list length (17 rules, 16 habits).

## 14. Audience engagement model
Click: age comparison + reassurance. Stay: list completion. Return: series/sequels. Funnel: tip jar + store.

## 15. Upload and publishing strategy
~25 uploads/month, ≤3-day gaps, fixed ~24 min. 3 near-duplicate-script flags (the highest so far) — repetitive-content risk.

## 17. Strengths and weaknesses
Clear milestone/reassurance angle; recognisable whiteboard host; sequels of winners. Weaknesses: industrial volume, padded 24-min lists, generic openings reused, culture-trivia and crash-asset detours.

## 18. Replicable working model
**"Am I Ahead? Milestone Check"**: one age or net-worth threshold → state why that age/amount is a turning point → 7-8 checkable milestones/signs → reassure + 3 actions this week. Packaging: host + one rising symbol + "READY AT 45?".

## 19. What to copy
Age-anchored milestone checklists with a forgiving qualifier; "secretly ahead" reassurance framing; sequels of proven list titles.

## 20. What not to copy
Scold titles (STOP overspending), "dangerous net-worth level" fear for tiny audiences, culture-trivia lists, self-introduction openers, 24-minute padding.
