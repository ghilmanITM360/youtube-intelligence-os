## 17. Strengths and weaknesses
Originated a viral format; strong first-rung indignity hooks; escalation structure. Weaknesses: off-format fraud/trade stories; feared-institution variants.

## 18. Replicable working model
**"Every Level of Wealth (Institution Ladder)"**: an institution everyone uses → rung 1 humiliation → rungs where treatment changes → top-rung deference → reflection. Choose institutions viewers want to rise within (banks, airlines, hotels), not ones they fear.

## 19. What to copy
Institution ladders; first-rung indignity; dollar ranges in titles.

## 20. What not to copy
Feared-institution ladders (IRS), quick self-remakes, off-format crime stories.

## 21. Missing evidence and limitations
Thumbnails not inspected; fiction; originator status limited to DB coverage.

- Not available from this dataset for any channel: CTR, impressions, retention curves, traffic sources, subscriber growth history, comments text. Hook timings are word-position estimates (untimed transcripts).

## 22. Final channel diagnosis
1) Exploded by originating the institution-ladder format; later variants and copies decayed. 2) Advantage: format invention. 3) Model: institution ladders. 4) Topic: banks/salary/accounts. 5) Packaging: wordless scenes. 6) Script: escalating levels. 7) Interest: status and dignity. 8) Failures: feared institutions, remakes. 9) Learn: invent the format, pick aspirational institutions. 10) Don't copy: fast institution swaps. 11) Repeatable: yes, if early. 12) Reputation: none. 13) Lesson: **the ladder works when climbing it is desirable.**
