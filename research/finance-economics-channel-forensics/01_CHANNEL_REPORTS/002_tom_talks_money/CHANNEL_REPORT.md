## 17. Strengths and weaknesses
Clear brand promise and persona; consistent illustrated identity; strong thesis-first openings; genuine contrarian argument (debt-free vs net worth). Weaknesses: generic consumer topics with small stakes; formulaic "Explained/Math" titles; no open loops; very high volume for a ~2k-view baseline; promotional endings.

## 18. Replicable working model
**"Contrarian Money Math"**: pick a rule everyone repeats → state it, then the BUT in sentence 2 → model it with one numeric scenario → show the behavioural trap → give a rule of thumb. Prioritise **big-ticket, loss-framed** subjects (market crashes, lost decades, mortgage vs invest).

## 19. What to copy
Thesis-first one-line openings; BUT-turn in the first 30 s; historical "imagine it's 2000" story drops with real data; physical-metaphor thumbnails with one illustrated host.

## 20. What not to copy
Low-stakes consumer annoyances (free shipping, subscriptions) as main topics; question titles; repeated "Explained"/"Math" suffix packaging; thumbnail promises that the title doesn't make.
