## 17. Strengths and weaknesses
Strengths: instantly recognisable thumbnail mascot; thresholds/levels give infinite, searchable topic inventory; concrete-scene and contradiction hooks; real data in hooks (VERIFIED NAR example). Weaknesses: slow promise (~75 s), sparse open loops, no cited sources, format drift into reactions, template sequels, and a severe 2026 reach collapse.

## 18. Replicable working model
**Model = "Money Level Ladder" explainer:** pick one threshold or life-stage the viewer can measure themselves against → open with an impossible stat or the viewer's 2-a.m. money scene → climb 5-7 levels with one character and exact numbers → end with "pick one move this month". Packaging: one mascot, one number or one fear phrase, one object, arrow.

## 19. What to copy
Threshold/level topics; the paradox hook built on a verifiable statistic; recurring illustrated presenter; one-number thumbnails; second-person scene openings.

## 20. What not to copy
Reaction compilations of other creators' clips (lowest PI + reused-content risk, PDF p.555 §7); "rich people formula" generic titles; sequelising a winner by swapping the age; self-introductions before the promise; claiming studies without naming them.

## 21. Missing evidence and limitations
Newest 100 uploads only; subscribers missing; one undated winner (15Gr4BAJi00) has a channel-median baseline so its PI is overstated; reasons for the 2026 reach collapse are UNKNOWN; claim verification done for 1 video only.

- Not available from this dataset for any channel: CTR, impressions, retention curves, traffic sources, subscriber growth history, comments text. Hook timings are word-position estimates (untimed transcripts).

## 22. Final channel diagnosis
1) Success came from evergreen, measurable money-level topics packaged with one mascot and one number; 2026 underperformance is channel-wide, not video-specific. 2) Advantage: recognisable mascot + threshold topic system. 3) Model: long, calm, numeric milestone explainers. 4) Topic strategy: evergreen life-stage thresholds + occasional housing/data paradox. 5) Packaging: single mascot, ≤4 words, one object. 6) Script: ladder/escalation with characters and numbers, weak loops. 7) Interest: "where do I stand?" 8) Failures: format drift, generic topics, template sequels. 9) Learn: concrete thresholds beat abstract wealth talk. 10) Don't copy: reaction compilations, 20+ min padding without loops. 11) Repeatable: ladder template, mascot, paradox hooks. 12) Reputation-dependent: little — topics carried videos even on a small channel in 2025. 13) Lesson: **sell a measurable threshold, not "wealth".**
