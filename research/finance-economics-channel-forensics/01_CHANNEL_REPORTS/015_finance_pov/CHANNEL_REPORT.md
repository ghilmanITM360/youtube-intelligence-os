Clear fantasy promise; dramatic-irony hooks; escalation structure; cinematic consistent art. Weaknesses: drifts into generic frugality; borrowing other channels' saving concepts; little factual grounding.

**"Silent Empire POV"**: a bystander underestimates "you" → reveal of hidden scale → step-by-step build of boring assets → final scale reveal. Packaging: calm protagonist before a fleet/warehouse + "NOBODY KNOWS".

Dramatic-irony bystander openings; big-number secrecy titles; boring-business escalation; wordless cinematic scenes.

Thrift and bank-explainer topics on a fantasy channel; importing concepts that don't fit the channel promise.
