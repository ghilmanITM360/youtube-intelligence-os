## Evaluation per sub-niche (12 criteria, evidence-based)

| Criterion | Senior benefits | Retirement adequacy | Wealth-psych explainers | Money POV / drama | Business-ownership econ | Macro/markets | ELI5 history/allegory |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Audience problem | protect/claim money before a date | "am I enough / ahead?" | "am I behind?" | vindication, fantasy | "could I run this / why does it work?" | "is the system breaking?" | "explain the famous thing simply" |
| Topic availability | high (rules, calendar) but concentrated | high | high but clichéd | unlimited (fiction) | very high (any business) | news-dependent | finite canon + concepts |
| Trend sensitivity | calendar/policy | low | low | format waves | low | high | low-medium |
| Evergreen potential | medium (rules change yearly) | high | high | medium (format fatigue) | **high** | low-medium | **high** |
| Competition | **saturated network** (shared scripts) | crowded mascot clones | most crowded | crowded, fast copying | moderate | dominated by big brands | moderate |
| Research difficulty | high (YMYL accuracy) | medium | low | low | medium | high | medium |
| Production difficulty | low-medium (presenter templates) | low | low | medium (writing) | medium-high (visuals) | high (expertise) | high (animation/comedy) |
| Policy risk | **high** (misleading claims, impersonation, duplicates; one channel terminated) | low | low | low-medium | low | medium | low-medium |
| Hit concentration | very bimodal | moderate | low | bimodal | strong subject effects | flat (brand floor) | strong |
| Repeatable opportunity | yes, *if* original & verified | yes | weak unless specific | yes, premise-led | **yes** | needs expertise | yes, needs craft |

## Where a new creator has the best evidence

1. **Business-ownership / everyday-business economics** — highest replicable ceiling with low policy risk: 10 channels, template-proven subject effects (Mr. Finance 401→266k on one template; Business Decoded 1.46M buffet; Millionaire Problems 0→10k subs in weeks; Built On You 431k in week 1). Requires decent visuals.
2. **Retirement adequacy & compounding with reassurance/permission framing** — steady demand, low risk, clear winning concepts (thresholds, "secretly ahead", "earned the right"), evidence on 7+ channels.
3. **Senior benefits — only as an original, accuracy-first channel** — strongest breakout share (24%) but the most saturated and policy-exposed group; copying the network is a lottery.
4. **Money POV storytelling** — viable for skilled writers with original premises; copies fail; format fatigue visible.
5. **Avoid as a launch niche:** generic wealth-psychology explainers (lowest breakout share, most clichés); macro commentary without expertise/brand.
