"Big Grievance, Surprising Verdict": a widely felt economic problem → a country/policy case → sardonic analysis → a verdict.

Grievance topics with a hopeful or justice twist; witty metaphor openers.

Distant war/sanction geopolitics; abstract essays.

22. Final channel diagnosis

  1. Large brand; wins most with relatable structural grievances. 2) Advantage: authority + voice. 3) Model: macro verdict explainers. 4) Topic: housing, inequality, youth, country cases. 5) Packaging: verdict titles. 6) Script: sardonic data. 7) Interest: "is the economy broken?". 8) Failures: geopolitics. 9) Learn: domestic grievance > foreign conflict. 10) Don't copy: reliance on established brand. 11) Repeatable: topic selection yes; reach no. 12) Reputation: high dependence. 13) Lesson: even a big brand earns most on problems the viewer lives with.
