4. Teen lens ka nichor: script, title, thumbnail

Lens 1: 9 channels ke winning models (NINE_CHANNEL_LIVE_COMPARISON_AND_LAUNCH_MODEL)

  • Opening pehle 15 s: document ka naam/tareekh + ek bara number + viewer ka stake. 181 transcripts mein association (Casual Finance, Crayon, Capital Case p<=0.03), Economics Explained mein asar nahi; causal nahi (INFERRED, MEDIUM-LOW).
  • Length 11-14 minute, kam se kam ek asli source chart/document screen par.
  • Repeat topic chhota hota hai (Monkey Epstein 838K phir 137K; PENNY $40T 1.15M, 13.6K, 3.3K). Isliye ek hit ke "2 alag angle" 14 din baad, copy nahi.
  • Hit concentration (Nutshell: 2 videos = 90.7% views; PENNY: 1 video = ~74%) = pehle 10 videos ke baad "model sahi" na maanein.
  • Pehle 10 videos tak Shorts aur sponsor nahi. vidIQ engagement % selection ka metric nahi.
  • Har title/thumbnail change ka timestamp likhein.

Lens 2: baqi finance channels (69-channel FINANCE_ECONOMICS_WINNING_MODELS)

  • Audience self-placement sentence pehli lines mein ("Agar aap ghar kharid rahe hain...").
  • Gain / permission / protection, loss aur outrage se behtar chala. Isliye stake ko protection ke taur par likhein ("kaun si fees aap lad sakte hain"), "aap loot rahe hain" nahi.
  • Chhupa hua number general advice se behtar. Document ki ek line = chhupa hua number.
  • Title = specific subject + situation + stake. Thumbnail = ek binary choice ya ek amount + 4 lafz se kam, ek jaisa character/style.
  • Avoid: crowded infographic, CAPS, siyasatdan ke chehre, "SHOCK", windfall ke wade.
  • Payoff = ek action. Parameter series (same format, document badalta hai): "Line Item" series.
  • Sources screen par; synthetic presenter ho to disclose karein.
  • Takraao: hamare thumbnail text mein CAPS hain ("WHO GETS PAID?"). Har video ke liye ek lowercase/sentence-case variant bhi test karein. Lens 2 aur Lens 1 mein dar vs protection ka farq hai; maine protection chuna (HYPOTHESIS).
