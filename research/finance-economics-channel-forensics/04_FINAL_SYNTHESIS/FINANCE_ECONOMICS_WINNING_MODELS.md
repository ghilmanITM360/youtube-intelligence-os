## B. The winning content pipeline (each stage: what winners do → evidence → PDF principle → do → avoid)

1. **TOPIC DISCOVERY** — Winners mine (a) perennial decisions and age/amount thresholds, (b) places and products viewers use (Costco, buffets, gyms), (c) the benefits/tax calendar, (d) concepts that already won on 2-3 channels *only if* perennial-search (rent vs buy) or re-angled; late copies of browse concepts flop. *Evidence:* concept ledger; Logical Money vs Finance POV $5/day. *PDF:* P01 Outlier, Method 1/4, P21. **Do:** keep an outlier database across 15+ channels (PDF p.7) and record first-publication dates. **Avoid:** copying a concept 2+ months after it peaked.
2. **AUDIENCE PROBLEM** — Name the viewer's self-placement: age, balance, birth year, store, decision. *Evidence:* Matty pivot, Ivan "Ready at 45?", Lana "Born Before 1966?". *PDF:* P02. **Do:** one sentence "this is for people who…" (PDF p.65 step 1).
3. **CONTENT ANGLE** — Gain/permission/protection beats loss/outrage; a hidden number beats general advice. *Evidence:* F1, F2, W5. *PDF:* P08, P22. **Avoid:** windfall promises, conspiracy, "SHOCK".
4. **TITLE + THUMBNAIL** — Title: specific subject + situation + stake (PDF five-part engine); thumbnail: one binary choice, one amount or one calm-vs-showy contrast, ≤4 words, consistent character. *Evidence:* Finance ANALYST 367→368k pair; montages. *PDF:* P08-P11. **Avoid:** crowded infographics, CAPS, politicians' faces, look-alikes.
5. **HOOK (0-5 s)** — Matched pair, exact-time story drop with an object and a price, or number-first with a named source. *PDF:* P12. **Note:** hooks are near-universal; they enable, not differentiate.
6. **INTRODUCTION (5-30 s)** — Confirm the title's promise with the key number or the ironic situation; no self-introduction before it (Nick/Ivan/Max losers). *PDF:* P13, P14, P22.
7. **STORY STRUCTURE** — Ladders/levels (escalation), 20-year matched race, list with mini-scenes, or dream → numbers → verdict. *PDF:* Engagement §5 escalation; Masterclass explainer structure (p.329).
8. **VALUE DELIVERY** — Real numbers, named datasets (SCF, SSA, Redfin), exact forms/steps for protection topics. *PDF:* P16. Accuracy is non-negotiable in YMYL (two verified misleading winners exist; one channel terminated).
9. **PAYOFF** — A clear verdict or the one action ("pick three this week", "run your three numbers", "send this to your parent"). *PDF:* P18, P22.
10. **NEXT VIDEO / SERIES** — Parameter series (new amount, new store, new age), explicit next-video links (Alicia, Carl, Uncle John). **Avoid:** remaking the same promise within weeks (P01 contradictions).

## C. What a new creator should launch with (practical blueprint)
- **Niche choice:** Model 1 (business-ownership/everyday economics) if you can produce clean visuals; Model 2+3 (retirement adequacy + decision duels) if you prefer low-cost animation and data. Treat senior benefits as a later, accuracy-staffed option.
- **First 10 videos:** 5 subjects from a cross-validated list (≥2 channels with PI ≥5 in the last 6 months), 3 parameter variants of whichever performs best, 2 tests of a second format (format rotation, PDF P03).
- **Packaging test:** for every flop with a good subject, retitle with a specific scene/stake (Finance ANALYST precedent) rather than abandoning it.
- **Compliance:** original scripts (no cross-channel paragraphs), disclose synthetic presenters/characters (Uncle John model), cite sources in descriptions (Nia/Wise Future model), no look-alikes or invented quotes.
- **Measure:** Studio CTR, 30-second retention and traffic sources — none of which this public dataset can show (PDF Part D §6, §10).

## D. Live-pass reconciliation (2026-11-13)
See `01_CHANNEL_REPORTS/*/LIVE_EVIDENCE_2026-11-13.md` and `04_FINAL_SYNTHESIS/NINE_CHANNEL_LIVE_COMPARISON_AND_LAUNCH_MODEL_2026-11-13.md`.
- **Scope:** the 9 live-checked channels are all explainer/animation channels, so they test the "satire/allegory ELI5" craft models and parts of Model 3, not Models 1, 2, 4, 5.
- **Launch-model difference (owner decision):** section C above recommends a first niche of everyday-business economics or retirement adequacy + decision duels. The 9-channel launch model is a topic/opening rule (real, dated event tied to the viewer's money; claim-first opening; 11-14 min). They are not contradictory: the 9-channel rule can be applied inside Model 1 or 3, but they were derived from different channels and have not been tested against each other.
- **Consistent across both:** one topic per video, no sponsors or Shorts in the first videos, parameter angles instead of remakes, change packaging with timestamps, judge against the channel's own median.
- **Softer than stated above:** "late copies flop" and "hooks are near-universal" hold; but the claim that topic choice explains success is MEDIUM-LOW where checked live (age/size confound, hit concentration).
