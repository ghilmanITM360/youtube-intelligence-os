## 1. Niche (HYPOTHESIS)
**"Fine-print economics": jab aap ko kisi cheez ka paisa dena parta hai (ghar ki closing, car dealer ka finance office, warranty, subscription), us waqt asli document mein har dollar kis ke paas jata hai.**

Har video ek asli document leti hai (Closing Disclosure, warranty contract, F&I menu, HOA budget) aur usay line-by-line kholti hai: kitna, kis ko, kyun. Ye pehle ki research ke Option C ("document parhna") ko ek pakka format banata hai, jis mein har video ka topic pehle se screen ho.

Ye naya kyun hai (INFERRED): 9 studied channels mein koi bhi har video ko "ek document, line-by-line, paisa kis ke paas" ke format mein nahi chalata. Jo topics hain wo sab ke dekhe hue hain, magar angle (document ka har line) naya hai. Poori tarah naya hone ka dawa main nahi karta; YouTube par kisi ne kahin aisa kiya ho sakta hai.

## 6. Har video ka rule (purani research se, VERIFIED jahan PDF 11/14 mein hai)
- Pehle 15 second: **asli document ka naam ya tareekh + ek bara number + viewer ka stake.** Joke, skit, "Meet X", ya sawal se shuru nahi.
- 30 second se pehle ek open loop ("aakhir mein bataunga ke kaun si line aap negotiate kar sakte hain").
- Har video mein ek asli primary document ya source screen par (Closing Disclosure, state rate filing, CFPB, FTC, company ka terms page).
- 11 se 14 minute. Koi sponsor nahi, Shorts nahi pehli 10 mein.
- Har number ka source description mein. Jahan source na mile wahan number nahi bolna.
