# Owner ke liye saada guide: ab kya karna hai (2026-11-14)

Labels: VERIFIED / INFERRED / HYPOTHESIS / UNKNOWN. Jo "HYPOTHESIS" hai wo guess hai jo test hona baqi hai. Studio CTR, retention aur traffic mere paas UNKNOWN hain.

## 1. Channel ka ek-line idea (HYPOTHESIS)
"Asli, dated money event (default, Fed ya NY Fed ki release, court ka faisla) jo viewer ke paison ko chhoota hai, 11 se 14 minute mein ek kahani ki shakal mein."
Source: PDF 14 sections 1 se 3. 9 channels mein mazboot videos aksar asli named event + viewer-money link wali thin (INFERRED, MEDIUM-LOW). Weak videos: abstract sawal, idare, poori company history.

## 2. Sach: abhi koi topic approved nahi hai
- 16 topics check hue, strict rule par 0 pass (PDF 13).
- Wajah: jin topics ki demand hai unhe bade channels pehle kar chuke hain; news wale topics ki demand kuch dinon mein khatam.
- Isliye "pehli 10 videos" ki list khali hai. Main ne jhoote topics se bharna munasib nahi samjha.

## 3. Pehli video ke liye 3 options

**Option A: C1 reframed (sabse mazboot, lekin conditional)**
- Sawal: pandemic ke dauran (2020-22) diye gaye credit cards ka asar kam ho raha hai, lekin kya naye cards kam mayaar par diye ja rahe hain?
- Kyun mazboot: do primary documents (NY Fed ki Liberty Street post 2026-11-10, TransUnion Q3 2026 report) ko koi closest video jodta nahi (tin closest videos par check, do ki transcript parhi).
- Kyun abhi approved nahi: (1) TransUnion ke charts bina written permission ke video mein nahi laga sakte (VERIFIED, unki terms). (2) Is exact sawal ki demand UNKNOWN. (3) Second Start Money aur Mike the Credit Guy ki transcript abhi parhi nahi (HTTP 429). (4) Dono datasets ke score models alag hain, isliye "do datasets side by side" kehna hoga, ek hi nateeja nahi.
- NY Fed ka data attribution ke saath use ho sakta hai (VERIFIED, terms parhi).

**Option B: student loan delinquency, umar ke hisaab se (C2)**
- Status: NEEDS MORE RESEARCH. Umar ke numbers ek chart ki tasveer se liye gaye (INFERRED), reuse terms UNKNOWN.

**Option C: "document parhna" series**
- Har taaza release par "release asal mein kya kehti hai". Har video ko pehle wahi screen karna hoga. Abhi koi tayyar topic nahi.

**Meri salah:** Option A ko pehli video ke liye tayyar karein, magar sirf us waqt jab neeche ke 4 kaam band ho jayen.

## 4. Option A ke liye title / thumbnail / script ka dhancha (mere DRAFT, test nahi hue, HYPOTHESIS)

**Title ke draft:**
1. "Pandemic Credit Cards: The Wave Is Fading. What Comes Next?"
2. "Credit Card Defaults Are Easing. The New Cards Tell a Different Story."
Rule (PDF 14 section 5): asli naam ya event + ek idea. Sirf bada number kaafi nahi.

**Thumbnail:** "NY Fed" ka naam + ek idea (jaise "2020-22 cards" bamuqabil "naye cards"). Apna chart banayen, TransUnion ka chart nahi. Chart par "modified" likhna hoga agar NY Fed ka data badla ho.

**Script ka dhancha (pehle 15 second ka rule):**
- Asli naam ya tareekh + ek bada number + viewer ka stake. Joke, skit, "Meet X" ya sawal se shuru nahi.
- 30 second se pehle ek open loop.
- Viewer se seedha baat ("aap ka card").
- Is mein 9 channels sab se zyada kamzor the (P13, P14, P17), isliye yahan aasan jeet hai.
- Har video mein kam az kam ek asli source chart/document screen par.
- Pehli 10 videos mein: Shorts nahi, sponsor nahi.

## 5. Aap ke abhi ke kaam (tarteeb se)
1. Channel ka naam aur ek apna visual character system tay karein (kisi dusre channel ka character copy nahi).
2. TransUnion se written permission ka sawal, ya kisi qanooni mashware se ye poochna ke sirf unke numbers apne chart mein dikhana theek hai. Main legal salah nahi de sakta (INFERRED).
3. YouTube Studio mein mere liye ye data dein jab channel ban jaye: CTR, 30 second retention, traffic. Bina iske test ka nateeja nahi nikalta.
4. Video banane ke din hi coverage dobara check karein: kya kisi ne is dauran wahi sawal pehle kar diya?

## 6. Kab rukna hai (kill rule, PDF 14 section 10)
10 videos ke baad: agar koi bhi pehli 3 videos ke median se 3x nahi gayi, to model kamzor hai. Agar ek 10x gayi, to uski framing ek baar dobara test karein; hit ek video mein concentrate ho sakta hai (do studied channels mein 74% se 91%).

## 7. Jo UNKNOWN hai
- Naya channel bina subscribers ke chalega ya nahi.
- Demand ka exact numbers (search volume): vidIQ ka paid plan ya Studio chahiye.
- PENNY aur Paper Billions ka 14/28 din ka re-pull (2026-11-20, 2026-12-04).
