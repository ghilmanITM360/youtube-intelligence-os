# Content Opportunity Map

Built from cross-channel evidence (concept ledger, channel reports). "Saturation" = number of channels already publishing the concept in this dataset; "Win rate" = share of those videos at ≥2x their channel baseline. Opportunities are **hypotheses to test**, not guaranteed winners (PDF Part D §9).

## A. Evergreen concepts with strong multi-channel evidence (test with an original angle)
| Concept family | Evidence | Saturation | Suggested original angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economics of owning an everyday/dream business | 109 videos / 6 channels, 28 ≥2x; gym, café, casino, McDonald's, oil rig, tavern | medium | under-covered everyday businesses (car wash, laundromat, storage unit, vending route, dentist office) with real unit economics |
| "Why does [place] do [irrational thing]?" | Business Decoded buffets (1.46M), mattress stores, free bread; Brimm airline layout; Neu containers | low-medium | subscription gyms, hotel minibars, airport prices, cinema popcorn, outlet malls (UK/AU equivalents) |
| Compounding thresholds / monthly amounts | 13 channels, 23/52 ≥2x, 4 flops | high | country-specific (UK ISA, Canada TFSA, Australia super) amount series; honest "first $100k" timelines |
| Perennial decision duels | rent vs buy (10 ch), real estate vs stocks, growth vs dividends, Roth vs 401k | high but search-tolerant | under-served duels (lease vs buy car with real data, pay off student loan vs invest, annuity vs bonds) with matched characters |
| Retiree adequacy & permission | Matty, Uncle John, Tom Lazy, Wise Future | medium | "earned the right" age-rule videos (55, 59½, 62, 67, 73) per country |

| Famous money stories ELI5 | Crayon (4.2M Wolf), Capital Case Rockefeller | medium | canonical stories not yet done in a distinctive visual voice |

## B. Calendar/trend opportunities (time-sensitive; publish 1-3 weeks before)
- Benefit-calendar dates: COLA announcement (Oct), Medicare ANOC (by 30 Sep), SNAP income-limit reset (1 Oct), tax-season deadlines, year-start "good news" — **gain-framed** (Nia, Robin, Mark Calloway winners). Avoid same-day announcement videos (Nia's 15-Oct COLA flop).
- Mega-events with a money angle: IPOs (SpaceX: 10 channels; first mover Casual Finance won), housing-market data releases (Redfin, NAR), AI-capex stories — only with genuine analysis.

## C. Market-localisation gaps (format arbitrage by country)
Walter Canada, Arthur Penrose (UK), Frank Law (UK, 768k), Wealth Logic Australia show demand for localised senior/banking topics. Opportunity: **original, verified** localisation (CRA/CPP/OAS; DWP/HMRC/ISA; ATO/super) of the strongest concepts — not translated US scripts (mislocalisation flops: Marcus "UK Pensioners" with US script; Arthur's variant flooding).

## E. Data gaps that limit this map
No search-volume or Google Trends data, no CTR/retention, comments not collected; the DB holds only the newest ≤100 uploads for most channels. Before committing, run PDF Method 4 (demand/supply scan) and the Topic Validation Prompt (pp.72-80) on each candidate.
