## titles
"[A] Vs [B] — The Real Math" is the signature (comparison format median PI 20.8 on n=1 tag, explainer 1.32). "Why Everything Changes After…" borrowed concept. Losers: "Rules You MUST Follow", "Japanese Habits", "Psychology of Money in Just 10 Minutes".

## hooks
Dominant hook: matched-pair story ("Two guys, same age, same city, same $X…") — a controlled experiment in 15 seconds; second: dated anticlimax scene (Dana). Promise of a 20-year result arrives within ~25 s in winners. Losers more often start with a tangent or a famous anecdote.

## script
14-19 min, number-dense (≈4 numbers/100 words), two-character comparison with year-by-year milestones and a reveal; endings ask viewers for their own number and plug "weekly videos… real math, no hype". Almost no open-loop phrases.

## engagement
Click: an unresolved big decision the viewer faces. Stay: two characters racing over 20 years — the viewer waits to learn who wins (built-in curiosity gap). Return: series of decisions. Trust: real numbers, no sales link.

## publishing
~14 uploads/month, median gap 1 day, one 21-day pause; 13-19 min. No cadence/length effect.

## strengths
Decision-comparison concept with near-infinite inventory; matched-pair story device; binary thumbnails; high data density. Weaknesses: borrows other channels' concepts; lifestyle clichés; cluttered infographics; small baseline audience between hits.

## model
**"Decision Duel"**: one high-stakes A-vs-B money decision → two matched characters, same inputs, 20-year race → year-by-year numbers → twist on who actually wins → "what's your number?". Packaging: host between two labelled options with 2-3 key numbers.

## copy
Matched-pair controlled-experiment hooks; A-vs-B titles with "The Real Math"; binary thumbnails that add numbers.

## avoid
Re-telling famous book anecdotes; life-path topics with no concrete decision; wealth pyramids/multi-tier infographics; copying another channel's exact title.

## limitations
Apr–Nov 2026 only; competitor-copy direction inferred from DB dates only (videos outside the DB could predate both); outlier views may include promotion (UNKNOWN).

## diagnosis
1) Success is concentrated in comparison videos about life's biggest money decisions. 2) Advantage: matched-pair math storytelling. 3) Model: decision-duel explainer. 4) Topic strategy: perennial comparison searches + borrowed proven concepts. 5) Packaging: binary illustration + numbers. 6) Script: two-character 20-year race, number-dense. 7) Interest: "which choice makes me richer?". 8) Failures: non-decision lifestyle/psychology topics. 9) Learn: a story device only works when the topic is a real decision. 10) Don't copy: exact titles of other channels. 11) Repeatable: fully — no face, public math. 12) Reputation: none needed; 19.8k-sub channel hit 495k. 13) Lesson: **turn a comparison search into a two-person race.**
