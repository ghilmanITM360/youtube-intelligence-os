## failures
- **Why Retirees Who Flaunt Their Money Run Out of It Faster** (afrSPW5LupQ, 694 views, PI 0.06): the negative mirror of the winning concept — about other people failing (schadenfreude) rather than the viewer's quiet success.
- **The Biggest Wealth Lessons from The Intelligent Investor** (vY3Bw1BU1BY, 886): book summary (as with Mark Invests' "Psychology of Money") — oversupplied.
- **Why Some Retirees With $2M Still Use Coupons** (ksw-lTsVFC8, PI 0.11): thin variant of the quiet-wealth idea (habit trivia).
- **The Average Retirement Account Balance by Age** (MCmTtjBzB3A, PI 0.12) vs winner "How Rich Is The Average Retiree?": similar data, but table-style title ("balance by age") vs status question ("how rich"). Also heavily supplied search term.
- **Why Turning 73 Costs More Than Any Birthday Before It** (CEel9XTB7c0, PI 0.15): RMD tax mechanics — fails across the cluster.

## diagnosis
1) Wins with retiree stealth-wealth identity and status questions; loses with summaries, mechanics and negative mirrors. 2) Advantage: credibility + vignettes. 3) Model: quiet-millionaire retiree stories. 4) Topic: retiree wealth psychology. 5) Packaging: calm mascot + $ reveal. 6) Script: vignette + stats. 7) Interest: "am I a quiet millionaire too?". 8) Failures: mechanics, summaries. 9) Learn: the same cliché fits retirees, not 30-somethings. 10) Don't copy: book summaries. 11) Repeatable: yes. 12) Reputation: citations build trust. 13) Lesson: **match the stealth-wealth story to the audience that already lives it.**
