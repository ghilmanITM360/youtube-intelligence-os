## comparison

Substantive difference (INFERRED from reading): winners promise **a felt state the viewer wants (calm, luxury, stability)** with a concrete count/amount; losers explain **mechanisms or risks** (brain biases, fees, inheritance law) or recycle clichés. ## model
**"Calm Money Lifestyle List"**: promise a desirable feeling at zero or low cost → aphorism + objection-handling + one survey stat → 10-20 items each with a scene and a move → future-pace to the next list. Packaging: one aspirational persona scene, 2-3 words, one red emphasis word.

## copy
Aspirational framing (calm, luxury, control) over fear; objection-handling hooks; a stat from a named survey in the first 15 s; ending next-video bridges.

## avoid
Behavioural-science mechanism explainers without viewer action; "hidden fees" and "wealthy drive old cars" clichés; persona backstory claims that contradict the visual; publishing 1-2 25-minute lists daily.

## limitations
Only 3.4 months in DB; persona first-person claims UNVERIFIED; no evidence on whether volume is sustainable; subscribers is a point-in-time value.
