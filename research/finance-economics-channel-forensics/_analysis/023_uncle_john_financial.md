## diagnosis
1) Wins by giving near-retirees permission through age rules; loses on trap-framed mechanics. 2) Advantage: trustworthy, transparent grandfather persona. 3) Model: permission explainers. 4) Topic: age-based IRS/withdrawal rules. 5) Packaging: relaxed mascot + age/rule. 6) Script: consequence chains + math. 7) Interest: "can I stop working sooner?" 8) Failures: traps, late news. 9) Learn: freedom framing beats fear for the same audience. 10) Don't copy: profanity-style thumbnails (brand risk). 11) Repeatable: yes. 12) Reputation: helps trust; not required. 13) Lesson: **tell near-retirees what they're now allowed to do.**
