## model
**"Contrarian Money Math"**: pick a rule everyone repeats → state it, then the BUT in sentence 2 → model it with one numeric scenario → show the behavioural trap → give a rule of thumb. Prioritise **big-ticket, loss-framed** subjects (market crashes, lost decades, mortgage vs invest).

## copy
Thesis-first one-line openings; BUT-turn in the first 30 s; historical "imagine it's 2000" story drops with real data; physical-metaphor thumbnails with one illustrated host.

## avoid
Low-stakes consumer annoyances (free shipping, subscriptions) as main topics; question titles; repeated "Explained"/"Math" suffix packaging; thumbnail promises that the title doesn't make.

## limitations
Library begins Mar 2026 (no earlier history in DB); comments not available to verify the comment-to-topic loop; thumbnails fetched in Nov 2026 may differ from launch thumbnails.

## diagnosis
1) Modest success from a credible contrarian persona; investing-loss topics overperform, consumer-cost topics drag. 2) Advantage: "real math, no hype" positioning. 3) Model: thesis→flaw→math→action explainer. 4) Topic strategy: evergreen debates + consumer traps. 5) Packaging: illustrated host + metaphor prop + 3-5 words. 6) Script: argumentative BUT-driven, analogy heavy, no loops. 7) Interest: protecting invested money. 8) Failures: small stakes, abstract thumbnails, question titles. 9) Learn: loss-framed big-ticket topics win. 10) Don't copy: suffix-template titles, app plugs in every ending. 11) Repeatable: thesis+BUT hook, metaphor thumbnails. 12) Reputation-dependent: membership loop needs an audience. 13) Lesson: **contrarian maths only travels when the money at stake is big.**
