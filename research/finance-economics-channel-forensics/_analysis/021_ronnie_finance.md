Audience (INFERRED): viewers of Reddit-style family-revenge stories, adults 25-55. Job: emotional vindication + wealth fantasy.

Losers have *more* numbers (65% vs 42%) and $ in titles (42% vs 35%) — the dollar figure alone is not the hook. Winners: self-made reversal against a parent figure; losers: passive windfall or no conflict. Thumbnails: winners freeze the humiliation moment (being thrown out, given $1); losers show signing papers or neutral scenes.

## research
Fictional narratives — no factual sourcing required; occasional Fed/BLS numbers in explainers. Idea source (INFERRED): Reddit "revenge/entitled family" story genre and YouTube story-narration channels (format arbitrage into finance); old-money aesthetics trend.

## trend
Genre-driven; spike in Oct 2026 after the stepdad video; rapid cloning within days.

## thumbnails
Wordless comic scenes of family confrontation; one with text ("THEY GET PAID BEFORE YOU NEED THEM") — an explainer that flopped. Consistent dark-haired protagonist.

## titles
Two-sentence drama: "[Family member] [humiliated you]. [Then reversal with $X]." Long (100+ chars).

## hooks
Exact time and place ("5:10 a Monday morning… Ironbark Depot"), emotional backstory compressed in one line ("16 years expecting to hear from one man").

## script
10-16 min novelistic second person, detailed workplace textures (depots, fabrication shops), redemption endings.

## engagement
Click: injustice + reversal; stay: waiting for the confrontation payoff (built-in curiosity gap); share: emotional catharsis.

## publishing
~10 uploads/month; four revenge videos in one week (6-13 Oct).

## strengths
Found a high-emotion format; strong scene-setting; wordless conflict thumbnails. Weaknesses: tiny audience base, inconsistent identity, lottery-like genre, fictional content presented in a finance channel.

## model
**"Self-Made Reversal POV"**: a parent figure dismisses you → you quietly build wealth through unglamorous work → the reveal moment → restrained resolution. Title: humiliation sentence + reversal sentence with $X.

## copy
Self-made (not inherited) reversal arcs; precise time/place openings; wordless confrontation thumbnails.

## avoid
Passive-windfall twists (trusts, inheritances); flooding the same plot within days; mismatched channel description.

## limitations
Small sample per sub-genre; fictional content; Oct 2026 spike might include external traffic (UNKNOWN).

## diagnosis
1) A late genre pivot produced one big hit and mixed clones; explainers fail. 2) Advantage: emotional reversal storytelling. 3) Model: self-made revenge POV. 4) Topic: family injustice + hidden wealth. 5) Packaging: wordless humiliation scene. 6) Script: novelistic second person. 7) Interest: vindication. 8) Failures: windfalls, sibling plots, explainers. 9) Learn: earned wealth > discovered wealth in reversal stories. 10) Don't copy: identity confusion, rapid cloning. 11) Repeatable: format yes, hit rate uncertain. 12) Reputation: none. 13) Lesson: **viewers reward the reversal they can believe someone earned.**
