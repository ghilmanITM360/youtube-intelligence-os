## identity

## strengths
Honest fiction disclosure, strong irony hooks, real rules inside stories, consistent protagonist. Weaknesses: cloning specific plots, frugality POVs, very high volume.

## model
**"Secret Windfall POV"**: an ordinary worker secretly holds big money → someone at work/home underestimates them → the story teaches one real rule (tax, mortgage, credit) → quiet resolution → next-story CTA.

## copy
Broad secret-wealth premises with varied jobs/settings; exact-time irony openings; fiction disclosure; real rules embedded.

## avoid
Cloning another channel's specific revenge plot; small private-frugality stories; 1 video/day of 18-minute fiction.

## limitations
2 months of data; fiction (no factual verification needed beyond embedded rules); production method unknown.

## diagnosis
1) Wins with secret-windfall irony; loses with clones and small-stakes frugality. 2) Advantage: irony-rich openings + honest fiction framing. 3) Model: secret-windfall POV. 4) Topic: lottery/quiet-wealth premises. 5) Packaging: protagonist vs antagonist scene. 6) Script: novelistic with one rule. 7) Interest: fantasy + vindication. 8) Failures: clones, low stakes. 9) Learn: share premises, not plots. 10) Don't copy: other channels' specific stories. 11) Repeatable: yes. 12) Reputation: none (5.8k subs → 682k). 13) Lesson: **a broad premise can be re-told; a specific plot can't.**
"If I wanted to build a channel like this, I would study evtp6soM62Y and 0VqARlMwRiw and replicate: a secret-windfall or quiet-wealth premise, an exact-time workplace scene with a dollar contrast in the first sentence, one real money rule inside the story, a wordless confrontation thumbnail and a fiction disclosure — never cloning another channel's plot."
