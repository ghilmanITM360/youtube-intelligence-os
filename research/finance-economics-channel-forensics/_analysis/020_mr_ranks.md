## key
"Your Life as Every [X] Rank" level-by-level POV channel (31.6k subs) that sits at the edge of the finance niche: **money-ladder POVs (airport owner, crypto miner from one GPU, esports pro) and the dark-web rank video break out (168-400k)**, while crime-hierarchy, survival and addiction POVs (mafia cleaner, cartel smuggler, lost at sea, sobriety, Foreign Legion) flop at ~1-5k — money stakes, not drama, carry the format.

## identity
Faceless illustrated POV channel, minimal description ("Contact me for more work"). Format: second-person "Level one… level two…" rank ladders inside a profession or underworld. Primary sub-niche (INFERRED): **career/profession money ladders and secret-world tiers** — only partly finance. Audience: 18-35 male-skewing curiosity viewers (esports, crypto, dark web, CIA) — INFERRED from subjects. Problem/job: entertainment + "what does each level earn/cost?".

## comparison
Identical title template ("Your Life as Every X Rank") and identical thumbnail grammar (one illustrated figure + arrow + 2-3 hand-lettered words) in both halves. The variable is the **subject's money relevance** (and possibly policy-sensitivity of crime/addiction subjects). Classic P21 evidence.

## strengths
Strong escalation format, immersive second-person writing, distinctive minimalist thumbnails. Weaknesses: off-niche and sensitive subjects; no sourcing; unclear audience.

## model
**"Money Ladder POV"**: profession or dream asset → Level 1 at the viewer's real starting point ($47 in the bank) → each level adds income/cost numbers → top of the ladder reveal. Packaging: one character, arrow, 2-3 words, visible money.

## copy
Level-by-level ladders with money at each rung; rock-bottom first rung that mirrors the viewer.

## avoid
Crime-hierarchy, violent and addiction subjects (policy/advertiser risk, low PI); drifting outside the money promise.

## limitations
Rule topic tags unreliable for this channel (analyst regrouped); limited finance relevance; reasons for crime-topic suppression are hypotheses.

## diagnosis
1) Wins when the ladder is about money or secret access; loses on crime/survival/addiction. 2) Advantage: escalation format. 3) Model: profession money ladders. 4) Topic: dream careers and assets. 5) Packaging: lone character + arrow. 6) Script: level chapters. 7) Interest: "what's it like at each level?". 8) Failures: sensitive, non-money subjects. 9) Learn: keep the ladder financial. 10) Don't copy: crime ladders. 11) Repeatable: yes. 12) Reputation: none. 13) Lesson: **the rank ladder works when every rung has a price tag.**
"If I wanted to build a channel like this, I would study PmEsq-6eGEk and oFKjAJrLims and replicate: a ladder from the viewer's own bank balance to owning an asset, money numbers at every level, and a single-character arrow thumbnail — staying away from crime and addiction subjects."
