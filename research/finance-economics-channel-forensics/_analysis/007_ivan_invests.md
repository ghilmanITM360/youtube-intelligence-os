## diagnosis
1) Wins on reassurance milestone lists for 40-45-year-olds; flops on scolding, fear and trivia. 2) Advantage: reassurance positioning. 3) Model: milestone-check lists. 4) Topic strategy: age/threshold self-assessment + sequels. 5) Packaging: cartoon host + single aspirational symbol. 6) Script: long list, scenario per item, small actions. 7) Interest: "am I behind for my age?". 8) Failures: scolding, fear, off-topic trivia, negative affordability. 9) Learn: reassure people about the milestone they fear. 10) Don't copy: padding and repeated openings. 11) Repeatable: fully. 12) Reputation: low. 13) Lesson: **tell middle-aged viewers they may be ahead — and show the checklist.**
