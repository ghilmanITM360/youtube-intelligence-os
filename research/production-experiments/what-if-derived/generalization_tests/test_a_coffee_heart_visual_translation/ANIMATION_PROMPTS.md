# Animation Prompts

One short prompt per motion. Each describes only what changes over time. None
repeats the frame description — that lives in `VISUAL_STATES.csv`.

All 17 are executed in CapCut. None is an image-to-video generation, so these
are editor instructions, not I2V prompts. See `I2V_DECISIONS.md`.

**Camera: locked for the entire video. No pan, no zoom, no push-in, no drift,
no parallax, in any of the 17 motions.**

---

### A01 — Fork and first label appear
The fork lines, the left label plate and the GOOD? text become visible together in a single frame.
Everything else — coffee mug, heart, background, camera — stays completely static.

### A02 — Second label appears
The right label plate and the BAD? text become visible together in a single frame.
The mug, heart, fork lines, left plate and its text stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A03 — Dr. Rehan appears
The doctor figure becomes visible in the lower-left field in a single frame, already in final position — no walk-in, no slide, no scale-up.
The entire binary diagram above him stays completely static. No camera movement.

### A04 — Strike stroke draws across the fork
The red stroke draws itself on progressively from its lower-left end to its upper-right end, revealed by a wipe along its own path, over roughly 0.5 seconds.
The mug, heart, fork, both plates, both text labels and Dr. Rehan stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A05 — Energy-drink can appears
The can becomes visible standing on the right of the baseline in a single frame, already in final position and scale.
The mug, its two strokes and the baseline stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A06 — Can's stimulation strokes appear
All four strokes above the can become visible together in a single frame — they do not appear one by one and they do not grow.
The can, the mug and the mug's two strokes stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A07 — Caution mark appears
The red caution triangle becomes visible above the can in a single frame. It does not pulse, flash, shake or scale.
The can, mug and both stroke groups stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A08 — Patient appears
The patient figure becomes visible in the right field in a single frame, already in final position — no entrance movement.
Dr. Rehan and the binary diagram stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A09 — Strike stroke draws across the consultation diagram
The red stroke draws itself on progressively from its lower-left end to its upper-right end, revealed by a wipe along its own path, over roughly 1.0 second.
Both figures, the diagram, the plates and their text stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A10 — The binary model is deleted
The mug, both branch lines, both plates, both text labels and the red stroke all disappear together in a single frame, leaving empty off-white. No fade, no shrink, no slide-off.
Dr. Rehan and the patient stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A11 — Left arrow draws
The arrow draws itself on progressively along its curve, from the mug down and to the left, arriving at the left figure's chest, over roughly 0.4 seconds.
The mug, both figures and the baseline stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A12 — First response arc appears
The single red arc becomes visible at the left figure's chest in a single frame. It does not pulse or repeat.
The mug, both figures and the left arrow stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A13 — Right arrow draws, then three arcs appear
The right arrow draws itself on progressively along its curve from the mug down and to the right over roughly 0.4 seconds; the moment it lands, all three red arcs become visible together at the right figure's chest in a single frame.
The mug, both figures, the left arrow and the left figure's single arc stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A14 — First factor fills
The gauge icon and the BLOOD PRESSURE text become visible together inside the top plate in a single frame.
All three plates, the connector line and Dr. Rehan stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A15 — Second factor fills
The heart icon and the HEART RESPONSE text become visible together inside the middle plate in a single frame.
All three plates, the connector line, the top plate's contents and Dr. Rehan stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A16 — Third factor fills
The three-cup icon and the QUANTITY text become visible together inside the bottom plate in a single frame.
All three plates, the connector line, the top and middle plate contents and Dr. Rehan stay completely static. No camera movement.

### A17 — Factor list swaps to video card
The three plates, all their icons and text, and the connector line all disappear in the same single frame that the video card, its play mark and the WATCH THE FULL VIDEO text become visible. One instantaneous swap, no fade, no crossfade, no slide.
Dr. Rehan and the background stay completely static. No camera movement.
