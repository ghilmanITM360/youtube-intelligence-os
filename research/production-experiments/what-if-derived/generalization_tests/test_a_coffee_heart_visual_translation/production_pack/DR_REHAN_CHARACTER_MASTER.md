# Dr. Rehan — Character Master

One reusable design. Draw it once, keep the file, reuse it in every shot he
appears in. This is a recognition cue, not a likeness. Do not attempt a
photorealistic portrait or the real person's face.

**Appears in:** VISUAL 01 (SH01), VISUAL 03 (SH03), VISUAL 05 (SH05).

## The design

| Feature | Specification |
|---|---|
| Overall | Minimal stick figure, flat 2D, thin black hand-drawn linework, slightly uneven natural strokes |
| Height in frame | Roughly 34% of frame height (y58–92%), same in all three shots |
| Head | Plain circle outline, roughly 1/5 of total figure height |
| Glasses | Two small rectangular lenses joined by a short bridge, across the middle of the head. Rectangular, not round — this is the primary identity cue |
| Beard | Short solid black filled arc under the chin. Small. Not a full beard shape |
| Hair | One short curved side-swept stroke across the top of the head |
| Face | Nothing else. No eyes, no mouth, no nose, no ears |
| Body | Single straight stroke |
| Arms | Two simple straight strokes |
| Legs | Two simple straight strokes |
| Line weight | Identical to every other line in the project — one uniform fine-marker weight |
| Colour | None. Pure black line |
| Doctor cue | The glasses + beard combination only. **No coat, no stethoscope, no clipboard, no badge** |

Pose A is a standalone transparent PNG (asset C01). Poses B and C are drawn
inside base plates IMG03 and IMG05.

**Consistency method:** draw pose A once as a transparent PNG. Generate IMG03
and IMG05 with the figure description copied word for word from the table
above. Then compare the three side by side before building — head size,
glasses width and beard size must match. If one drifts, regenerate that plate.

## Why he is not a doctor cliché

The corpus has no medical video and no doctor asset. A white coat and
stethoscope would be an import from stock medical illustration, not a transfer
from the learned grammar. The narration names him; the glasses and beard make
him the one distinguishable person on screen. That is all the identity the
plan needs, and it is why the patient (C02) and the two figures in VISUAL 04
(C03) are deliberately featureless.

C03's rule is not a preference. The whole argument of VISUAL 04 is *same
coffee, same arrow, different response* — if the two figures differ in any
visible way, the viewer reads the difference as the explanation. Duplication
guarantees they are identical. Generation does not.
