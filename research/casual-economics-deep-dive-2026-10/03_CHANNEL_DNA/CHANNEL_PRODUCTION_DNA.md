# Casual Economics — Channel Production DNA (11 videos)

Research-only synthesis, DS-000036. Built from 11 structurally validated packages: six historical packages in `data/extracted/forensic-analysis/casual-economics/2026-10-12/02_VIDEO_FORENSICS/` (Switzerland `3tVMjRRXQIg`, Norway `xVhMZPiIIGs`, Rome `rl6ggpS13ao`, Europe Depression `VTx478EUybo`, Singapore `kbPauzdHhcI`, Dubai `N9GUfsfiM4M`) and five research packages in `02_VIDEO_FORENSICS/` (Big Short `Iwz0fhJWN9Q`, Greece `nr5dcBPACH0`, Japan `x_MZu-QF-6o`, 2008 Crisis `APlGPpM-nBo`, Great Depression `qF3ekm9Gx_E`). Timestamps are seconds in the cited video. Abbreviations below: SWI, NOR, ROM, EUR, SGP, DXB, BSH, GRC, JPN, C08, GDP.

**Evidence rules.** Every visual observation is from sampled frames reviewed at contact-sheet or targeted native scale (point samples ≤4 s apart, plus temporal sequences); none is continuous playback. Motion "mechanisms" are inferred, keyframe-compatible reproductions — the creator's software, layers, easing and keyframe values are UNKNOWN. Audio is signal-measured plus ASR captions; nothing was directly heard. Asset appearance counts are design-family observations, never authored-file counts. Labels: OBSERVED (seen in frames), INFERRED (interpretation), MEASURED (computed).

## 3. Asset DNA

**Recurring design families across videos (OBSERVED):** money bags with $ or ¥ (JPN 97/144, C08 24/41, GDP 28–29, SWI, BSH); stock/debt/MBS/CDO certificates with ornate borders (JPN 102/117/160, C08 32–48/124, GDP 39/46/74); bank building icon (JPN 97/121/186, C08 65/74, GDP 89/103, EUR); tower/skyscraper icons (C08 27/67, JPN 120/185); seals of institutions (C08 Fed/SEC/CFPB, GDP Fed/SEC); FOR SALE sign (JPN 25/116/137, C08 3/9/113); red zigzag trend arrow (JPN 59/64, C08 137, GDP 75, both 2008 & GDP thumbnails, EUR thumbnail); green up arrow (JPN 148, GDP 16/46); chalkboard on easel (JPN 31–33, C08 22/29/151, NOR green chalkboard); country/region maps; flags; brand logos; cracked/repaired house variants (C08 77/135/164–165).

**Within-video reuse is heavy.** Japan revisits ~10 environment plates many times (governor office ×9 samples, trading floor ×9, palace/moat ×6, old street ×10 — see `x_MZu-QF-6o/ASSET_OBSERVATIONS.csv`). C08 repeats the opening Dow chart at 103 and the rating-monitor scene at 126–128. GDP ends with callbacks to its opening jazz club, factory and bank lobby (164–176).

**Cross-video reuse (OBSERVED similarity, not proven file identity):** the cream Dow Jones 1920–1940 crash chart appears in JPN 168, GDP 1/133/158, EUR. The "DOW JONES CRASHES" grid appears in C08 1 (past 60 years, no 1929) and GDP 132 (with 1929 -90%). The 1929 breadline storefront "FREE COFFEE & DOUGHNUTS" appears in C08 2 (split) and GDP 3/116/144. The 2008 and GDP thumbnails share one template. This supports a shared asset library across videos (INFERRED).

**Layering hierarchy (INFERRED from occlusion):** background plate → mid props (tables, desks, counters that hide character legs) → characters → foreground props (money bags row, certificates) → overlay text/number → transition overlays (REWIND, PAUSE). Examples: C08 24 bags in front of desk; BSH table hides entering bodies; JPN 144 bags on floor in front of executives.

**Scene construction formula (OBSERVED, repeated):** plate + 1–3 characters + one prop that carries the concept + optional caption. Concept cards: neutral field + 1–5 icons + one headline + one number.

## 5. Editing DNA

- **Information density:** a new visual state roughly every 1–4 s. MEASURED historical medians: stable state 0.8–1.3 s, candidate shot 4.8–5.4 s (SWI 5.37, NOR 5.19, ROM 4.77, EUR 4.82). Research packages did not certify shot counts.
- **Sentence-to-visual mapping:** most caption sentences get one new visual proposition (a number, an icon, a pose). Example JPN 84–100: "unionization 3.2%" → label 85; "53%" → label 88; "land reforms" → title; "38%" → number; "resold" → FOR SALE sign 98. Caption overlap only; not word-aligned.
- **Pattern "statement → proof card":** a scene states a claim, then a cream chart proves it (JPN 296 property +5000%, C08 242 Case-Shiller, GDP 140 Dow 6X).
- **Year cards on black** punctuate chronology (JPN 7, 38, 55, 70; C08 58, 152; GDP 7, 47, 148).
- **Reading holds:** dense list or quote cards stay up longer (GDP quote 248–256; JPN reform list 56–60; C08 Basel/Dodd-Frank lists). Durations are sample-bracketed.
- **Callbacks** reuse earlier plates in the conclusion (C08 488–560 and 667–692; GDP 164–176; NOR success recap; SGP broken rulebook).
- **Sponsor reads** where present are visually distinct segments: SGP ~250–298 (6.5% of runtime), NOR sponsor with live-action/screenshots, BSH sponsor panel 215. Not observed in JPN, C08, GDP, GRC samples.

## 7. Audio DNA (limited evidence)

- **DIRECTLY_HEARD: none.** No listening in any video.
- **SIGNAL_MEASURED:** continuous non-silent soundtracks. Overall RMS ≈ −24.6 to −28.0 dB in JPN/C08/GDP (ffmpeg astats on decoded stereo); historical per-second mono medians −18.8 (NOR) to −36.9 (SWI) dBFS, with very few windows below −45 dBFS (SWI 18, NOR 5, ROM 0, EUR 11). This is consistent with continuous narration over a music bed, but it does not prove it.
- **ASR_INFERRED:** single narrator voice style; second-person hooks ("Picture this", "What if I told you"); dramatized dialogue lines; caption density ~2,600 words in 16.7 min (JPN), i.e. ~155 words/min including dialogue (MEASURED from captions, not speech).
- **SOURCE_DECLARED music:** Kevin MacLeod tracks dominate (ROM, DXB, C08, GDP); JPN credits Jason Shaw, Audionautix, Alex Productions, Keys of Moon, Silent Partner, StreamWaves; NOR lists none. Placement unverified.
- **Not established:** SFX usage, pauses, music cue timing, voice identity, AV sync precision.

## 8. Packaging DNA

**Title formulas (OBSERVED):**
1. "The [Event] in N Minutes (Casual Economics)" — GDP, C08; variant "Japan's Lost Decade: An Economic Crisis in 16 Minutes".
2. "How [Place] Got/Became [Rich/…]" — SWI ("INSANELY Rich Without Any Resources"), DXB ("How Dubai Got So Rich | History of Dubai"), SGP ("How Lee Kuan Yew Built Singapore in One Generation").
3. "How [X] Ruined/Actually Happened" — GRC, BSH.
4. Question — NOR ("Did Norway Create a Perfect Economy?"), ROM ("…Worst Economic Collapse in History?"), EUR ("How Did The Great Depression Affect Europe?").

**Thumbnail system (OBSERVED from current CDN images):** huge white-outlined subject headline across the top (GDP, C08, SWI, NOR, ROM, EUR, DXB) or black-on-white (JPN, SWI); one large distressed/expressive character foreground left; a strong symbol in the middle (red zigzag down arrow: GDP, C08, EUR; cracked rising-sun with Fuji: JPN; Swiss cross mountain: SWI; ship with money: NOR); small secondary characters bottom right (protest sign in GDP/C08; worried office workers in JPN). BSH is the exception: stacked title + four portrait cut-outs in arrow shapes, no stakes number. No thumbnail uses a numeric stat.

**Template evidence:** GDP and C08 thumbnails are the same layout — "THE" + title, red zigzag arrow, large panicked figure left, small protest group bottom right. EUR uses the same arrow + map + panicked figure. These are the three crisis "Great Depression/2008" videos.

**Mobile legibility (historical review at 320×180 and 160×90):** country/event headline and main face stay readable in every reviewed case; small props lose detail (SWI, NOR, ROM, EUR, DXB, SGP PACKAGING.md).
