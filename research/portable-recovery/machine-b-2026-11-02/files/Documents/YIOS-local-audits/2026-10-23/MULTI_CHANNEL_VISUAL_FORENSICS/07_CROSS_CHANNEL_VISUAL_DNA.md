## General production principles (original-strategy level; copy no art or characters)

Each is supported by at least one timestamped example above. They remain hypotheses about effectiveness, because no retention or CTR data exists:

1. **Signal section changes visually.** The four videos do it four ways: Primate title cards, Monke background colours, Barely numbered survival cards, Primitive's persistent title bar.
2. **Make abstract numbers physical.** Examples: Barely's scale bars, thermometers and shrinking population bar; Primate's ownership pies.
3. **Break the house style for jokes.** Live-action, real logos or meme photos appear in 3 of 4 videos.
4. **Return to earlier scenes as callbacks** (Monke 190–225 s).

## Risks seen

- **Third-party IP inserts:** real brand logos, film footage, another show's character, and a photo of a real person. A new channel should not imitate these.
- **On-screen numbers that depend on narration:** Barely's "10°F (6°C)" and "27°F (15°C)" show temperature *differences*. The uncertified ASR has the narration say "dropped by", but the card alone lacks a minus sign.
- **Spoken-only disclaimer and visual-only promotion:** Primate's "not financial advice" is spoken but not shown. Monke's own-app QR segment is shown but not spoken (uncertified ASR).
