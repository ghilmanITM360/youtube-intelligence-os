## 1. Actual end-to-end workflow

1. `build_baseline.py` reads the frozen source packages and registry, normalizes title, publication date, views, format and transcript/thumbnail availability, and computes the same-channel age-peer performance tier. `audit-baseline/MANIFEST.json` freezes the original IDs and 26 source hashes.
2. `TRANSCRIPT_COVERAGE.csv` records the canonical stored-text hash, caption type and archive locator. `THUMBNAIL_COVERAGE.csv` records the registered thumbnail URL and baseline availability, but later local image receipts live in individual audit batches.
3. A researcher creates `audit-batches/<BATCH>.json` with title, thumbnail, script, topic, virality, event and status fields. The findings are authored research; they are not generated by `build_audit_966.py`.
4. A companion `audit-batches/<BATCH>_READ_RECEIPTS.json` records transcript ranges, hashes, image inspection sizes, source receipts and reproduced calculations. For the reviewed videos, the ranges cover the complete canonical string and the archive and image hashes reproduce.
5. `build_audit_966.py` overlays batch findings onto the frozen 966-video master and writes `VIDEO_AUDIT_966.jsonl`, the completion matrix and six component CSV projections. It never generates semantic findings.
6. `validate_audit_966.py` checks identity, frozen hashes, required fields, local thumbnail hashes, transcript hashes against the registry and projection parity. Its own report correctly labels semantic validation `STRUCTURAL_ONLY`.
7. `build_combined_workbook_data.py` joins the authoritative 1,612-video universe, the 966 audit ledger and approved expansion ledgers. `build_workbook_1612.mjs` produces the combined workbook. Expansion records remain metadata-only unless separately audited.

## 4. What is genuinely analyzed

- **Title:** The exact stored title is reviewed for hook, promise, audience cue, specificity, emotion, script alignment and accuracy. These criteria appear in prose rather than separate scored fields.
- **Thumbnail:** The actual preserved image is opened. The reviewed records document native dimensions, 320×180 readability, text, visible objects, composition, contrast and title-thumbnail relationship. The local file hash is enforced by the validator.
- **Script:** The receipts claim complete sequential reads of the canonical stored text and preserve first/last witnesses, beat maps, opening/ending text and material claim audits. The three canonical and archive hashes independently reproduce. This establishes stored-text integrity, not narration completeness.
- **Topic and audience:** Individual records identify the audience problem, economic mechanism, demand type, narrative angle, novelty and same-channel competition.
- **Performance:** Public frozen views are divided by age in days and compared with the median lifetime views/day of up to ten nearest-age videos from the same channel, format and snapshot, constrained to 0.5–2.0 times target age. At least five peers are required. Ratio thresholds are STRONG at 2.0 or above, WEAK below 0.5 and TYPICAL otherwise. The five-peer sensitivity uses the first five of the same ordered cohort.
- **Virality and credibility:** The researcher combines public outcome, packaging, topic, timing, competition, format and alternative explanations. Claim checks use dated external sources and calculation receipts. Records label performance explanations as descriptive, not causal.

## 5. Automated versus researcher-reviewed

Automated: frozen identity checks, source hashes, transcript hash comparison, thumbnail file hash, schema/rubric presence, status projection parity, peer selection, age normalization, ten-peer ratio/tier, workbook row counts and expansion completion protection.

Researcher-reviewed: full-text reading, title and thumbnail interpretation, narrative and pacing analysis from text, material-claim selection, source relevance, numerical recalculation, topic positioning, virality hypotheses and recommendation boundaries. The five-peer result is reproduced and stored in receipts but is not part of the canonical baseline tier.

## 6. Missing or insufficiently verified

- No MP4 frames or audio were inspected for the three examples. Editing cadence, visual pacing, transitions, on-screen evidence, sound effects, music, delivery and audiovisual retention devices are unverified.
- Auto-generated plaintext has no timestamps and is not certified complete narration. Pacing observations are structural text observations, not measured delivery pace.
- CTR, impressions, traffic sources, watch time, audience retention, revenue, packaging history and A/B tests are private/unavailable. Public likes and comments are stored, but the core method does not calculate an independent engagement benchmark.
- The validator cannot prove a human or model read every transcript segment. A contiguous range receipt is an auditable assertion, not proof of cognition.
- The validator does not verify that cited sources entail each claim, that every material claim was extracted, or that receipt arithmetic is correct.
- Publication-date applicability is analyst-reviewed, not mechanically enforced.

## 7. Ranked methodological weaknesses

1. **Critical — semantic quality is not machine-verifiable.** Current tests can prove structure and evidence integrity but not completeness, reasoning quality, source entailment or actual reading.
2. **High — no audiovisual audit.** `COMPLETE_LIMITED_EVIDENCE` can be correct only if it clearly excludes editing/audio conclusions. The Finance workflow does not resolve registered media through `YIOS_MEDIA_ROOT`.
3. **High — receipt content is weakly enforced.** Before this review, completion did not require the validator to reconcile read ranges, archive hashes, image inspection sizes, first/last witnesses or calculation receipts.
4. **Medium — performance is exposure-confounded.** Lifetime views/day controls age approximately but not channel growth, impressions, traffic, seasonality, topic demand or upload timing. Five-peer sensitivity can change tiers.
5. **Medium — engagement is descriptive only.** Likes/comments are retained but no cohort-normalized engagement calculation or missing-count policy is part of the completion contract.
6. **Medium — rubric criteria are prose.** Title, thumbnail and topic subcriteria are usually present, but fields are not independently required, making omissions harder to detect.

## 8. Improvements required before continuing

1. Add a strict receipt validator for the current audit standard: exact canonical hash/length, contiguous full-string ranges, first/last witnesses, archive member hash/size/line count, local image hash/size, native and mobile inspection sizes, explicit audio status, primary-source receipts and calculation receipts.
2. Keep `COMPLETE_LIMITED_EVIDENCE` only when all six research dimensions are complete and limitations explicitly exclude unavailable audio/video/private analytics. Never use it to imply audiovisual verification.
3. Preserve exact ten- and five-peer cohorts and calculations. Keep the ten-peer result canonical and the five-peer result a sensitivity test.
4. Add explicit methodology fields for claim-source applicability and unverified audiovisual dimensions in future batches. Do not retroactively invent evidence for completed records.
5. Continue to require primary sources, independent arithmetic and recommendation-versus-rule boundaries, while acknowledging that source entailment still needs expert review.

## Completion-standard conclusion

`COMPLETE_LIMITED_EVIDENCE` currently means all six ledger statuses are complete with an actual-title review, a hash-bound actual thumbnail, a full stored-text review, topic analysis, public-performance analysis and individual virality/credibility analysis, while private analytics and audiovisual evidence may remain unavailable. The status can coexist with auto-generated captions and no audio/video review because the limitation is explicit. It must not be used when stored text is unread, the thumbnail is uninspected, or required evidence fields are absent. Existing tests validate the record contract and evidence integrity; they do not certify analytical truth.
