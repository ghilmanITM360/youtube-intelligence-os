## Descriptive pattern worth testing (not a finding)
At 16 videos, the count of videos with a headline figure that disagrees with its own stated inputs rose as tier fell: 0 of 2 STRONG, 6 of 8 TYPICAL, 4 of 4 WEAK. **At 26 videos the pattern weakens**: the 10 added videos show a NOT_MET numeric or dated claim in 3 of 10 (TYPICAL 2 of 4, WEAK 1 of 3, INSUFFICIENT 0 of 3), against 12 of 16 in the first batches, and the order is not monotone (the TYPICAL `08z1JYUQQnM` has five wrong figures and a similar-tier `0Ca3uV6lgos` has none; the WEAK `0bnMucPlugA` has none). Combined counts are STRONG 0 of 2, TYPICAL 8 of 12, WEAK 5 of 7, INSUFFICIENT 2 of 5, but the first and second sets used the review state at different times and a second-pass reviewer reduced some "wrong figure" findings to convention mismatches (for example the $1.54M compounding figure in `12kPA19t_Jw`), so the counts are not comparable at the margin. Viewers rarely recompute figures, so any association would more plausibly reflect topic breadth, packaging, production effort, channel size and recommendation reach than the arithmetic itself.

## Counterexamples and limits
- **-y7_DWddSK8 (WEAK, 0.33x, 247 views) has features of its STRONG sibling**: same channel, numbered "quietly rich" list title, frame story, thumbnail that matches the script item for item, and a well-placed end subscribe plus next-video pointer.
- The numbered-list title appears in a 24x, a 1.6x and a 0.33x video; an open loop and an early ask also span tiers.
- Tier is peer-set dependent for three videos (-DIT32sBkAk and -nTyzYhIoBc rise to STRONG with five peers; -y7_DWddSK8 rises to TYPICAL).
- The two Carl Invests videos with 247 and 359 views sit at 0.33x and 0.64x because the channel's own peers are larger; the K01 ratio is peer-relative, so a low-view TYPICAL on one channel can have fewer views than a WEAK on another.
- -fuCWtCi4Mc and 06PIGRNWJJY are the same clip-reaction format at different ages (23 and 0.14 days); 06PIGRNWJJY is too new to tier.
- Single snapshots only: **when any video broke out, and how fast, is UNAVAILABLE.** No CTR, impressions, retention, audience or audio data exist for any of them.

## Alternative explanations not testable here
Channel size and prior audience, topic demand (a payday routine and "signs of wealth" are broader topics than Japanese bonds, UK vehicle exports or clip reactions), release timing and promotion, thumbnail and title click behaviour, recommendation placement and retention are all unmeasured. Nothing above supports a causal statement about views.
