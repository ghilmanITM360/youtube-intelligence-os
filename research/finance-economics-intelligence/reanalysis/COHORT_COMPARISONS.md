## Counterexamples and limits
- The two STRONG videos have no major arithmetic inconsistency and a thumbnail that matches the title promise; two TYPICAL videos (-ZcdgylpeJg, -fuCWtCi4Mc) also have no headline error, and four TYPICAL videos with errors still sit at 0.7-2.0x, so an error does not prevent a typical result and its absence does not make a strong one.
- The STRONG pair come from two channels (Carl Invests 24x; Nick Invests 4.3x), while five other Nick Invests videos with similar voice and structure are TYPICAL, so channel alone does not explain the STRONG result for Nick.
- The STRONG videos are among the oldest in the sample for Nick (319 days) and Carl (154 days); age is only partly controlled by the K01 lifetime-rate ratio.
- Early ask-before-value, an open loop and a numeric list title appear in STRONG and TYPICAL videos alike; none separates tiers here.
- -DIT32sBkAk changes tier under five peers; any "strong" statement about it depends on the peer set. Both Lucas Grant videos are POV stories and sit at or below the channel's weak range, but one channel and two videos cannot separate format from channel, topic, audience size or thumbnail.
