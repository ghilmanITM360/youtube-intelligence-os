# Performance and virality investigation

[VIDEO_MASTER.csv](VIDEO_MASTER.csv) contains 966 canonical saved observations. **49 STRONG, 71 TYPICAL, 56 WEAK, 790 INSUFFICIENT_DATA** under the conservative historical age/format method. This is not a live leaderboard. [METHODS.md](METHODS.md) specifies thresholds, dates and exact peer construction. Missing source dates, null format and malformed dates stay missing.

Only 142/176 labels agree when the baseline changes from up to ten to five nearest-age peers: **34 change**. [PERFORMANCE_SENSITIVITY.csv](PERFORMANCE_SENSITIVITY.csv) is the sensitivity register. Near-threshold or shifting labels warrant low confidence. Day-level dates, launch bursts and changing channel audiences remain confounders. No view-based score isolates script, title or thumbnail effects.

## Six-dimensional hypothesis audit

| Hypothesis | Support | Counterexample | Alternatives | Confidence / causal evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A concrete audience problem improves topic appeal | Bank treatment, small contributions, airline layout outliers | Related family/doctor treatment and investing videos weak | Topic size, channel audience, recommendation history | Moderate descriptive; matched original topic series with traffic/impressions |
| Timely explanation adds relevance | Reused housing breakout follows a dated Redfin source | Reused MicroStrategy warning weak; evergreen crash strong | Existing audience and changing market attention | Low causal; repeated event-window uploads plus traffic-source data |
| Familiar explanatory grammar reduces uncertainty | Repeated why/how and analogy titles | Same formula across all tiers | Subject demand, notoriety, thumbnail and audience | Low causal; concurrent title experiment |
| Mobile clarity helps packaging | 27 observed images have legible large forms | Clear banana chart and salary image weak | Historical thumbnail changes and distribution | High legibility observation, UNKNOWN performance effect; native experiment |
| Mechanism chains and payoff aid engagement | Bubble feedback loops and POV callbacks | Investing analogy and weak POV also coherent | Narration, novelty, duration, prior viewers | Moderate structure observation; retention and comprehension tests needed |
| Consistent promises create audience expectations | Repeat formats within Primate and POV | Similar episodes receive different reach | Saturation, release history, small launch samples | Hypothesis; returning-viewer and series-level cohorts needed |

Private impressions, CTR, retention, traffic sources, subscriber conversion and revenue are **UNKNOWN**. Likes/comments are not substitutes. Current official guidance describes personalization, viewing response, satisfaction, topic interest, competition and seasonality as relevant factors. [YouTube discovery](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225), checked 2026-10-16.
