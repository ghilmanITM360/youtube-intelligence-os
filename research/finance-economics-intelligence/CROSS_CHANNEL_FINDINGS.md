# Cross-channel findings

The practical recommendation is to test **well-sourced mechanisms behind everyday financial contradictions**. This is an editorial positioning proposal, not a discovered universal growth law.

1. **The same package family produces different outcomes.** Primate's banana explanations include strong bubbles `37C7fXr8XSY`, typical credit cards `QvaHDxdxQFg`, weak investing `lW29lVYi2uI`. Sonny's `$5/day` `vb7UojsINvg` is strong while Shaami's `ENUs_Bzh2lI` is typical. Subject, audience history and distribution remain alternatives to a script/title explanation. Confidence: high on observed differences, low on causes.
2. **Concrete mechanisms recur among useful outliers, but not all mechanism videos win.** Airline layout `m5WPi3LX9z0`, bank treatment `2LXED3ENhck` and inherited historical-crash examples are promising. Private-credit `7uu6qaCGZsY` is an inherited weak example despite a hidden-industry promise. “Underexplained” needs audience testing, not just analyst interest. Confidence: moderate editorial inference.
3. **Script reuse can become visibly repetitive without exact duplication.** Money Life POV's reviewed trio uses different text hashes but near-identical scene progression. Zero exact transcript duplicates does not mean 955 independent creative approaches. Whether repetition hurts audience response is untested. Confidence: high within three full reads, not corpus-wide.
4. **Clear imagery is compatible with both strong and weak videos.** The banana growth chart and salary contradiction remain readable at 320px and still have weak historical tiers. Current image versus old performance is additionally confounded by possible thumbnail changes. Confidence: high descriptive; effectiveness unknown.
5. **News demand is neither necessary nor sufficient.** Historical Great Depression success coexists with a weak Europe follow-up during elevated query interest. Inherited Trends normalization and query mismatch prevent cross-query volume claims. Confidence: moderate descriptive.
6. **An attainable quality gap is inspectable evidence.** Unit inconsistency in `QvaHDxdxQFg` and raise-versus-salary comparison in `DAwQLnBO94U` suggest a source/date/unit/comparator check. These are stored-text issues pending audio verification, not allegations about intent or whole-channel accuracy. Confidence: high for textual discrepancy.
