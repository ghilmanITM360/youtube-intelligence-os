## Credibility and packaging lessons

- **Accurate scenarios can coexist with misleading guarantees.** w7Xu8O5TNHQ's 1,000/month milestone arithmetic is consistent with 8%/12 compounding, but a 4.7% withdrawal scenario is not indefinite guaranteed income. nWlaR6AnbuQ provides defensible monthly targets while its 15-year alternative fails to reach the same 689k destination.
- **High reach does not certify factual accuracy.** rnbgKswe2nA's 447k/339k growth requires 108k contributions, not the stored 18k. Its Lisa example assigns 99% of wealth to the later period where the modeled balance difference is 75%. Potential ASR omissions remain unresolved without audio.
- **Separate population and balance-sheet concepts.** SCF family net worth is not individual cash savings; provider account-holder medians are not every American's retirement resources. s_G83pcoOhE cannot infer debt by subtracting Empower and Fed medians from different populations, or rank a savings-only balance against net worth.
- **Reconcile clocks and cash flows.** t_QZNEvHLu8's final 438k illustration uses ten years of coffee deposits after describing adoption seven years ago. Its return examples are largely reproducible but the one-thing morning guarantee and 99% statistic are unsupported. Three missed employer matches must not become a full 20-year annuity. Several earlier cases switch balances or deposit frequencies without explanation.
- **Check the actual image.** qs58N_4cFh4 displays **$18M**, not $1.8M, beyond its script scenario. qf8A5durdfw's unlabeled 600-to-near-zero chart is not verified IPO trading history. nd3jnb8HN-o juxtaposes net worth with salary badges. Actual image receipts and hashes preserve what was inspected; historic upload-day artwork is not certified.
- **Current official facts matter.** June17 morning-routine video quotes 3.8% CPI after the June10 release reported May 4.2%; NY Fed May12 reported 1.25t card balances rather than 1.4t. x-elojnSWFg uses outdated 146k Roth threshold and $100 Global Entry fee: 2026 single MAGI phaseout is153–168k, Global Entry $120 since October2024.
- **Tax/legal and health correlations need scope.** Roth conversion does not universally close at RMD age; inherited property basis, qualified Roth withdrawals, nonspouse heir distribution rules, funding trusts and insurance limits matter. Health/income, charitable giving/income, AI wage premiums and millionaire habit surveys do not prove that buying a course, donating a dollar or changing a habit produces the asserted return. In the identified1994 sleep study NK activity fell **to72%** of baseline, not **by72%**; the exact script's underlying protocol is not certified.
- **Reverse valuation needs assumptions.** qf8A5durdfw's 566b future revenue scenario assumes roughly8x terminal sales to support its4.54t future capitalization.
