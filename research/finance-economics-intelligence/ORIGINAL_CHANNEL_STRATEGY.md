# Original channel pilot strategy

**Positioning hypothesis:** “Understand the mechanism behind the money decision.” Explain ordinary financial contradictions using an original worked scenario, a visible cashflow model, dated sources and a meaningful exception. Target English-speaking adults who want to understand household finance and economic headlines without needing trading experience. This audience choice is editorial; audience geography/demographics were not measured.

Evidence basis: familiar-stakes outliers plus counterexamples in [performance research](PERFORMANCE_AND_VIRALITY.md), six current specialist competitors in [landscape](LANDSCAPE_RESEARCH.md), and specific script QA gaps in [script intelligence](SCRIPT_INTELLIGENCE.md). Do not clone competitor characters, scripts, layouts or sensational conclusions.

## Four-episode pilot

| Pilot / cluster | Original sample title | Thumbnail concept (brief only) | Research and falsifiable hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Household liquidity | “A Million-Dollar Home, an Empty Bank Account” | House versus almost-empty cash drawer; “WEALTH ≠ CASH” | Source housing/retirement cashflow definitions; label fictional household and assumptions. Hypothesis: contradiction attracts qualified viewers who can correctly explain asset versus cashflow after viewing. |
| Borrowing costs | “Why Your Payment Barely Shrinks the Balance” | Payment split into interest/principal with honest proportions | Use a specific disclosed illustrative loan/card schedule and primary terms. Hypothesis: progressive arithmetic improves comprehension relative to an opening that only warns about debt. |
| Business-model explanation | “The Cheap Subscription That Gets Expensive When You Use It” | Price tag versus accumulating usage meter | Choose one documented pricing model; don't invent universal fees. Hypothesis: concrete total-cost example earns more qualified engagement than a generic corporate-greed frame. |
| Historical mechanism | “How a Small Loss Becomes a Forced Sale” | Asset, loan and shrinking equity cushion | Research one well-documented historical event and clearly separate analogy from original evidence. Hypothesis: causal steps sustain attention beyond the opening better than a chronology-only treatment. |

Pair each with one self-contained Short: wealth versus cash; one payment calculation; fixed versus variable cost; leverage arithmetic. A Short must deliver its own answer. No videos, animations or generated images were produced in this research phase.

## Editorial operating rules

One episode = one answerable question. Opening shows the contradiction and states the promised answer. Middle establishes definitions, two/three mechanism links, a reproducible example and an exception. Ending answers the question and states limits. A human editor checks source authority, date, unit, denominator, comparison and whether fiction is clearly labeled. Do not state projections as guaranteed outcomes or impersonate a credentialed financial adviser.

Current YouTube policy requires original substantive value and rejects generic/repetitive low-value content; the page also addresses AI personas presented as human experts giving sensitive-topic advice. Use a clearly authored educational voice and materially distinct episodes. This is a design constraint, not a judgment of competitor eligibility. [Official policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392), checked 2026-10-16.

## Test plan

A four-episode pilot plus four Shorts is a feasibility experiment, not a statistically powered study. Suggested cadence: one researched long-form every two weeks if the team can maintain verification quality; publish the related Short after the full explanation is ready. This schedule is an editorial capacity assumption, not an optimal timing claim.

For eligible long-form, test distinct honest title/thumbnail promises concurrently with native A/B when available. It optimizes watch time and may be inconclusive; Shorts are ineligible. [Official testing guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400). Collect own-channel impressions, traffic source, CTR, first-30-second retention, key explanation drop-offs, completion and qualitative comprehension. Until data exists, every baseline is UNKNOWN.

Pre-register one hypothesis per episode. Compare within format and comparable age windows, separate source mixes, and report uncertainty. Do not adopt a universal CTR target. Continue a cluster when evidence supports audience understanding and sustainable production; revise if viewers click but the central explanation loses them; do not call a single breakout proof.

Production feasibility is deliberately modest: original diagrams and a restrained visual vocabulary; no new forensic imitation. Costs in the [backlog](TOPIC_IDEA_BACKLOG.csv) are editorial relative ratings, not measured budgets or performance probabilities. Establish actual research/editing hours during the pilot before scaling.
