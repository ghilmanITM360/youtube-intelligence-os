## Statistical limits

The population is the 363 distinct Video IDs attached to channel UCXzNNA2ngRpRiWvyA7eGd6w in the reconciled Finance universe, joined one-to-one by exact Video ID to the frozen metadata and authoritative JSONL. Original/expansion membership is taken from population_layer, not guessed from filenames. All 363 are original; none belong to the 646 expansion records. Do not substitute the 1,612-video Finance universe for Nick's channel.

Frozen public metrics are from the source-declared 2026-09-11 scrape day; timezone and exact collection time are unknown. DS-000022 ingestion on September 12 is not the metric date. No current YouTube counts were collected. The immutable workbook A2 explicitly says Scraped 11 Sep 2026. The registered corpus is a supplied snapshot, not proof of the channel's complete lifetime or October population.

Raw views measure cumulative recorded reach. Lifetime views/day = frozen views / frozen Age_days. Frozen peer ratio = target lifetime views/day divided by the median of up to 10 nearest-age OTHER same-channel, same-format, same-snapshot videos within 0.5–2 times target age; targets and peers must be >=7 days old and at least five peers are required. STRONG >=2; WEAK <0.5; TYPICAL otherwise. These are descriptive analyst thresholds, not YouTube labels, present velocity, or measured causal effects. Reproduction uses the stored four-decimal ages and Video-ID tie-breaker; computing unrounded ages can change one tied peer set. All frozen Nick ratios reproduced at five-decimal precision.

Topic and title tags are transparent overlapping lexical classifications of actual titles. They are not newly validated full-script semantic labels for 363 videos. All rules and every group membership are supplied in the matrix and manifest. A video can occur in multiple groups. Counts must not be summed into a topic-exclusive population. Group medians use eligible frozen ratios; raw view medians use all known group view counts. Minimum/maximum examples are descriptive, not random samples. Groups with fewer than 10 eligible records are exploratory; small samples, overlapping tags, multiple comparisons, topic mix, channel growth, publication timing, distribution, packaging changes, and audience selection confound every association.

Sensitivity recomputes the descriptive ratio using five nearest peers and shifts all stored ages in the same snapshot +/-1 day. The latter is a day-precision stress test, not a claim about the true scrape timezone. Outlier sensitivity removes the four highest-view videos from aggregate summaries while keeping the original frozen peer ratios and denominators fixed; it does not rebuild a hypothetical outlier-free channel baseline. Separate age strata are disclosed. No significance testing or causal attribution is claimed.


Five-peer definitions change 58/363 stored labels (16.0%; 58/348 eligible = 16.7%). A one-day earlier snapshot changes 2/363 labels; a one-day later snapshot changes 1/363. No ranks are presented as invariant. Source population selection, unobserved thumbnail/title revisions, channel growth and impressions cannot be controlled from these data.
