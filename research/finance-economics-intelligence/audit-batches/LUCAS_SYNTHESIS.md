## Script and credibility findings

Ordinary locations, repeated objects and delayed reinterpretations often organize the stories: hospital balance, mature tree, folded index card and diner calculation. These also occur in typical or weak records, so emotional texture is not sufficient evidence of high retention. Several supplied endings are unfinished. Large wealth jumps, inconsistent account balances, mixed weekly/monthly units and tax-rule compression recur. Strong reach coexists with arithmetic errors; credibility findings must never be assigned as causes of reach without evidence.

The scripts themselves contain useful counterexamples to universal austerity: planned concerts and travel, paying for repairs, useful outsourcing, hobbies, selective share sales for family needs, transparent partner conversations and explicit two-income/employer-match advantages. The final secrecy stories also admit social distance and loss of connection.

## Original tests and uncertainty

Test one household decision at a time with reconciled deposits, withdrawals, debt balances, taxes and return assumptions. Compare a practical trigger with an abstract promise while keeping the delivered lesson accurate. Separate liquid emergency reserves from equity portfolios. Date legal/statistical inputs and label fictional scenarios. Measure our own appeal, retention and satisfaction; competitor CTR, impressions, traffic, revenue and surveys are unavailable. No historical demand series or exhaustive cross-channel exposure census establishes why any Lucas upload won. The event register supplies narrow historical or retrospective checks, not invented search-volume evidence.
