# Production pipeline (reconstructed, PLAUSIBLE, tested only at small scale)
1 concept -> 2 pick entering classes with the rule table (14_...) -> 3 draw original layered assets (code or vector) -> 4 timeline by frame: cuts, 1-frame pop-ins, pose swaps, text swap, optional 15-frame ease-out push-in -> 5 render with `tools/render_original.py` -> 6 re-measure with `tools/measure_motion_windows.py` logic and compare to 11_... -> 7 human review. Tested: steps 2-6 on one 14 s clip. Untested: rigged characters, audio, captions, long-form (8-10 min) scale, Shorts layout, export/packaging.
