# Stage 6 — Final Topic Ranking and Validation Sheets (2026-11-19)

## Ranking rule (stated, not weighted)
Decision tier from Stage 5 first (GO, then GO-CONDITIONAL, then HOLD). Inside a tier: number of four evidence legs present (Y/N: competitor winner exists; Trends demand at least ~0.2 of the 'mortgage rates' anchor; competition not High-saturated; a live news/data hook in 2026-11), then the Trends ratio. No numeric weights are invented. A leg is a binary reading of Stage 5 evidence and inherits its limits (visual Trends, secondary news).
