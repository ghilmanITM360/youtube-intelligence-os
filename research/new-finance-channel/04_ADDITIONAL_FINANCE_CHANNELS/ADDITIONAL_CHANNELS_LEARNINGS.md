## What each is useful for (INFERRED, with the evidence in the table)
- **Patrick Boyle, How Money Works, Economics Explained, Money & Macro, Modern MBA**: the large analytical channels. They post about 1 to 5 times a month in 17 to 38 minutes, with plain statement titles (3 to 11% numbers). Use them for topic depth and how a real source is shown, not as a starting-size benchmark.
- **Primate Economics**: animated ELI5 with a character; strong history (top item 15.7M, which is a 56-second Short; its best long video is 1.8M) but only 1 upload in 30 days. Evidence of the format's ceiling, not of current momentum.
- **Neu** (40.7K subs, 45.6K median long views): transport and logistics economics, 9 minutes, 2% numbers in titles, 73% "Why/How/What" openers, 7 videos a month. Closest long-form benchmark in size and cadence with a topic area no primary channel covers.
- **Built On You** (16.6K subs, 22.6K median, 3.5K views per day): company histories; every title has a number and 86% use capitals; 20 minutes. A small channel with unusually fast views per video; only 14 videos, so treat as a signal.
- **PENNY** (9K subs, 21 items in 30 days, 13.8K median): animated explainers; 76% of titles have a number, 53% are questions; the Shorts here average about 1.1K views, so Shorts did not carry it (INFERRED).
- **Econ in a Nutshell, Business Decoded, Finance ANALYST, The Expensive Part, Brimm.**: small channels with low medians (1.5K to 8K). Useful as proof that a handful of topics break out (Econ in a Nutshell 78% outliers) but weak as benchmarks.
- **Mr. Ranks and The Wealth Cortex**: POV or story drama channels with low reach per video; not our category.

## Cross-checks against the primary six
- High-view channels are not the ones using the most numbers in titles: Patrick Boyle 7%, Economics Explained 3%, versus Capital Case 77% at a lower median. Number-in-title is a style choice here, not a clear driver (INFERRED, correlation only).
- Cadence and median views are not related in this set (Business Decoded 15.7 per month, median 2.6K; Patrick Boyle 4.3 per month, 1.5M). Reach is tied to the channel's size and its topic, so cadence alone is not a plan.
- Small channels with high views per video (Built On You, Neu, Paper Billions) pair a specific news or company subject with a strong thumbnail; sample sizes are small.
