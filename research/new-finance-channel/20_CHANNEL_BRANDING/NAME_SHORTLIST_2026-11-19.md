# Channel name shortlist (2026-11-19)

Owner brief: simple but fresh and brand new, like Econ in a Nutshell, Casual Finance, Primate Economics, Crayon Capital, MonkeyExplains, Economics Explained, Capital Case. Style left to my judgment; owner wants a finance/economics word, a numbers/accounting hint and/or a small animal or object, and a serious, trustworthy feel; only names with a free .com and a free @handle.

## What the checks mean (read this first)
- **@handle free** = `youtube.com/@name` returned HTTP 404 on 2026-11-19 (a taken handle returns 200). Not a reservation.
- **.com free?** = no DNS name servers found. That is a hint, NOT proof: a domain can be registered without DNS. Confirm at a registrar.
- **YouTube exact-name search** = logged-out search for the exact phrase, channels filter, returned "No results found".
- **Trademark: NOT checked.** The owner or a lawyer must check USPTO and other marks before using any name.

## Why not plain names
"Margin Notes" and "Ledger Lens" each already have at least six small YouTube channels with that exact name. Of 33 names tried, 17 had the @handle taken (HTTP 200) and 19 had a .com with DNS records (examples taken on both: Ledger Lynx, Yield Yak, Basket Badger, Figure Fox, Number Nest, Data Dollar).

Index Ibis, Median Marten, Margin Magpie, Quota Quail, Census Crane, Tariff Tern, Pivot Pelican, Sum Sparrow, Tally Tortoise, Rates Room, Price Pangolin, Tallied Capital.
