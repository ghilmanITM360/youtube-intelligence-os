3. **News topics decay.** Airfares needs a primary release and a fast, original video; a read-out is not monetizable.
4. **Evidence is selected for success** (we only studied channels that perform) and is single-rater.
5. **Capacity, voice, name, licences are all undecided.**
6. **One market-rent primary series is missing**, and a newer CPI (2026-11-10) exists than the one the titles use.
7. **Competitor Studio data is permanently UNKNOWN.**
