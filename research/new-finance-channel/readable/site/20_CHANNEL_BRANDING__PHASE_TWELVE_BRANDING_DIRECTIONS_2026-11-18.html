Phase Twelve: channel branding and basic identity (master "Phase Twelve"), 2026-11-18

Status: three directions for owner choice. Nothing is chosen, no logo drawn, no name registered. No name, handle or trademark availability check was done (no tool or approval for it): every name below is UNKNOWN for availability and must be searched (YouTube handle, domain, trademark) before use. Avoid names that imitate government agencies or a reference channel. Fonts named are common open-licence families; their licence must be confirmed (UNKNOWN until checked). Colours are placeholders (hex), picked to differ from the references seen in Phase B: Casual Finance and Monkey channels use hand-drawn flat looks, Crayon, Capital Case and Paper Billions use dark, high-contrast cartoon characters.

Brief carried from Phase F and G: positioning "One price, one official number, one mechanism: why things cost what they cost"; named human or clearly labelled narration; real charts with source and date; no mascot.

Direction A: "Ledger" (paper and ink data journalism)

Item Proposal
Name ideas The Number Behind; Line Item; Price Tag Files
Tagline "One price. One number. One mechanism."
Positioning the channel that shows the official number on screen
Logo direction a small ruled-ledger line with one highlighted figure; no coins, no arrows-up
Banner off-white paper with one big number and a thin rule; channel name small
Colours paper #F6F1E7, ink navy #14213D, amber #E09F1F, alert red #C1352B (used only for the key number)
Typography headline: a heavy condensed sans (Barlow Condensed or similar); body and chart labels: IBM Plex Sans or Inter
Thumbnail identity one real chart or object on paper background, 1 to 4 words, key number in red
Visual storytelling identity stamped source tag in a corner of every chart ("BLS, Sep 2026"); data lines drawn on screen
About section (draft) "Every video starts with one price people complain about and one official number. We show where the number comes from, who sets the price, and what the data does not say. Sources are on screen. No financial advice."
Content categories Prices and who sets them; How official statistics are built; Two numbers that disagree; Industry mechanisms
Risks generic "data channel" look; looks like Casual Finance's chart-led thumbnails if the paper style is not distinct
