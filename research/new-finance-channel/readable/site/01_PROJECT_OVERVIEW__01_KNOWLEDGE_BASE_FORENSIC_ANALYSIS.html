New findings that matter for a finance channel (not in Part D): 1. AI-generated personas presenting as human experts who give financial advice are not monetizable (the page's example is AI podcast hosts offering financial guidance). So we must not present an AI voice or avatar as an adviser; frame as explainer, not personal financial advice, and decide the voice and persona policy now (Phase F/J). 2. Content that only reads out text from websites or news feeds is not monetizable; news-driven finance videos need original analysis and sourcing. 3. Reviewers judge the whole channel: main theme, top and newest videos, titles, thumbnails, About. A templated 30-video calendar is itself a risk.
