1. BLS rent methodology (VERIFIED, factsheet read in full; calculation page only first ~40k of 111k chars verbatim)

Source: bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/owners-equivalent-rent-and-rent.htm (last modified 2026-02-13), bls.gov/opub/hom/cpi/calculation.htm. - Each sampled rental unit is priced "every 6 months" (reason given: rents are locked for a lease term). - Sample is split into six panels; panel 1 is collected in January and July, panel 2 in February and August, and so on. - Monthly change = sixth root of (current average rent / average rent 6 months earlier), chained monthly. - Vacant units get an imputed change from newly occupied units; each repricing applies an aging adjustment. - NOT found: any BLS sentence saying "CPI rent lags market rents". So "6-month collection cycle" is VERIFIED; "therefore CPI lags market rents" is still INFERRED (the lag size and direction depend on the data; do not state a number of months as fact).
