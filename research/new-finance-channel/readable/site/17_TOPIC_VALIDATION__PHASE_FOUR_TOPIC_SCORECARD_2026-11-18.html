1. Challenge to the master weighting (Rule 5)

The master model (competitor evidence 15, demand 15, YouTube 15, suggested 15, search 10, evergreen 10, visual 10, gap/originality 5, feasibility 5) has four weaknesses for a new channel: 1. Competitor evidence is survivor-biased (we only studied channels that perform) and cannot show CTR or retention: 15 points is too many. 2. Suggested relevance cannot be measured from public data (no Studio data, UNKNOWN): 15 points on an unmeasurable item is mostly guessing. 3. Gap and originality gets only 5, yet it decides look-alike risk and the "inauthentic / mass-produced" monetization exposure (official page 1311392) and Phase D found every high-demand topic already served. It should weigh most. 4. Feasibility gets 5, yet capacity is UNKNOWN and each video needs primary-source work. Revised weights (my proposal, HYPOTHESIS): competitor evidence 10, current demand 12, YouTube demand and competition 15, suggested relevance 10, search opportunity 10, evergreen 8, visual 5, gap and originality 20, feasibility 10. Both sets are shown; the order of the top topics barely changes, which is itself a result.

Hard gates (a topic cannot reach A or B unless it passes all): G1 original angle possible with primary sources; G2 not a news read-out; G3 the key numbers are VERIFIED from a primary source; G4 no recent substantial video already owns the exact angle.

5. What the scorecard says (INFERRED, LOW to MEDIUM confidence)

  1. The two Phase D conditional topics are also the two top scores under both weightings (67 and 62/63). That agreement is partly circular, because they were the topics we researched most deeply (more evidence raises scores). Treat the order as "best of a thin pool", not "best topics in the market".
  2. Re-weighting moves generic airlines from 64 to 56: the master weights would have rewarded a topic that Phase D rejected for having no gap. That is the strongest argument for the revised weights.
  3. One experimental slot (T6) tests Concept 2 (industry mechanism) without claiming demand.
  4. Not scored because there was no evidence: "how CPI is built" (Concept 1 pillar), jobs/wages, central-bank rates. They need a Phase D pass first.
