## F. Script and hook structure (VERIFIED text, INFERRED structure)

All eight top videos open with a hard fact, a statistic or a dated event, then the contradiction: "Last month, there were 58% more people trying to sell a house than buy... So you'd think home prices are falling." or "The bond market has been the biggest story... But those two stories have something in common." There is no "welcome back", no mascot opener. The first 15 seconds create a puzzle (expected outcome vs actual) and the title is the answer's promise. Videos then walk through chart-by-chart, with a "so what happens next" ending. Median runtime 12 min 56 s.
