It names the familiar promise and the unexpected backward-looking charge. “Can” keeps the claim conditional. Thumbnail A isolates the contractual “IF”; the script explains the resulting mechanism. Titles are editorial proposals, not tested winners.

## Thumbnail concepts

**A — The condition. Recommended.** A concerned original shopper inspects an oversized offer; the coral “IF” dominates the paperwork. Navy, teal, warm cream and coral establish consistent roles. Headline “NO INTEREST?” complements the backward-charge title. The payoff condition is legible in the artwork, not represented as invisible legal print.

**B — The backward clock.** The same shopper reacts to a receipt and reverse clock. “BACK TO DAY ONE” expresses the retrospective mechanism. This offers a stronger time metaphor but depends more heavily on the title to establish finance context. A clock here is an explanatory symbol, not a quantitative timeline or an allegation that an issuer changes terms retroactively.
