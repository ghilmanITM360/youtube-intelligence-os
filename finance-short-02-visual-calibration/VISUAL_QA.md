## Style surfaces and limits

- **REFERENCE_SUPPORTED:** White/light field; flat cream/green/red accents; black contours; small simple faces and stick limbs; finance paper/building props; persistent additive explanation; real evidence excerpts; selective source emphasis. Dark/logo/environment counterexamples remain documented; no new dark scene is needed here.
- **ART_DIRECTION_INFERENCE:** Specific host design, original pediment/bank/bill, exact placement, red numeric focus, handwritten-font orientation variation, conservative UI whitespace and proposed reading holds. Geometry and pen widths now vary modestly between features. This reduces uniformity but does not turn SVG paths into a professional artist's natural ink drawing.
- **NOT_YET_VERIFIED:** Creator tools/private workflow, pixel equality, independent human review, easing, continuous animation/contact, actual narration synchronization and audience comprehension. No animation was created to test these.
- Typography still uses editable macOS Chalkboard with Comic Sans MS/sans-serif fallback; letters have restrained individual orientation differences. This is **font-based lettering**, not a claim of individually hand-lettered glyphs. No proprietary font files are distributed. Owner must accept typography or request original lettering/a licensed portable font before full production.
- No navy/teal canvas, SaaS cards, glossy effects or scene-library redesign. Original host identity and all seven scene groups were retained. Building detail is not a trace of the channel's BoJ dome or particular illustration.
