## What was not used, and why

- **YouTube Studio Audio Library.**
  - It needs the owner's signed-in Studio session, and downloading a track needs the owner's approval. Neither was used in this session.
  - The brief allows an original bed in that case, so none was downloaded.
  - To use a Studio track instead:
    1. Download it from Studio and note its licence line from Studio, including any attribution requirement.
    2. Run `python3 animation/tools/sound.py --music <track.wav>`.
    3. Re-render with `node animation/tools/render.cjs --out …`.
    4. Record the track and its licence here.
    5. Keep the track out of Git until its distribution terms are confirmed.
- **"No copyright" websites.** Not used. No licence is claimed from any website.

## Caveats

- The bed and effects were generated by code written in an AI-assisted session. They contain no third-party material. How copyright applies to procedurally or AI-generated audio can vary by jurisdiction; this is not legal advice.
- **Content ID** matches are not expected, because the audio comes from no library. This was **not** tested against YouTube.
- No one has listened to the mix. See `V3_AUDIO_MIX_QA.md`.
