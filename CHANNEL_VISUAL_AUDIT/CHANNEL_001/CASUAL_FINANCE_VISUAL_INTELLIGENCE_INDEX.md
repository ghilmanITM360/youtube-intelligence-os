## 6. Confidence limits
- Evidence classes: [M] measured, [O] observed, [I] inferred, [U] unavailable.
- The "4.8 s callout lag" is RETRACTED (label proximity, not a delay). Narration alignment comes from local ASR (never human-verified); no sound/music verdict; no audience data; creator tools unknown; rights status of screenshots unknown.
- Coverage (physical files 216, 215 unique SHA-256): 17 visually gated (13 long, 4 Shorts), 11 partial, 188 coarse (see `COVERAGE_MATRIX.csv` tier column). Inherited tiers measure different levels of coverage. Targeted windows and recovered legacy evidence are tracked separately in the adaptive corpus classification.
- V1 Rent is one production experiment, owner-rated about 60-70% (subjective); publication approval and audio seam approval remain with the owner.
