## Transfer to original storyboards

When the spoken job is “who said it,” show the person and role. When it is “what did they say,” show the actual attributed statement, adjacent context and date. When it is “what does that change,” build a separate visible mechanism or identity-preserving overlay. Example for an original insurance script: display the dated policy language beside its carrier identifier; mark the deductible clause while leaving coverage limits readable; draw a two-layer loss bar with the amount retained by the household and the remainder paid subject to the identified limit. Use plainly labeled hypothetical values. Hold until each side of the bar can be read. Reuse the same label/unit across the cut, then test the deductible and cap independently in QA.
