## Independent written exercise

Before the test I fixed six criteria: financial arithmetic and terms, same assumptions and explicit denominator, visual decodability and originality, clause-linked timing and holds, evidence appropriate to claim type, and feasibility with current tools. The fresh agent received only the frozen Index/Playbook and a new hypothetical bonds excerpt. It received no V1 information and did not render or browse. Its six-scene result is condensed below, with the independent review.

| Clause | Storyboard | Review |
|---|---|---|
| Two bonds identical except price | Original mirrored bond slips on white, both tagged Hypothetical example. One bracket groups common terms, separate slots for price. | Gives the viewer a controlled comparison. Preserve the left/right identities across cuts. |
| Each pays five dollars a year | Identical $5/year tiles appear on their clause and hold at the same height and size. | Explicitly dollars per year. Keep coupon rate out of the graphic. |
| Costs $100 or $80 | Reveal each purchase price under its own slip on the spoken number. Cue invariance by briefly bracketing both coupons on “still.” | Magnitude and comparison work without motion after the reveal. |
| Larger percentage of lower price | Transform the slips into two aligned fractions with $5 as numerator and price as denominator. Reveal 5% and 6.25%, underline $80 and hold. | Exact and readable. “Current yield” is the required label and a general definition needing the authoritative source. |
| Ignores maturity | Cut on the conceptual shift to a timeline. At the last tick place final coupon and maturity payment separately, then underline maturity payment. | Timeline makes the omitted cash flow visible. No redemption value is assumed from either price. |
| Full comparison needs today’s price, time and maturity payment | Preserve coupon schedule, emphasize the redemption, draw the time bracket and label price today at the origin. | “Full return” needs clarification if called yield to maturity. A real example requires remaining coupon schedule, settlement/price convention, redemption, transaction timing and a consistent annualization method. |

The same excerpt in a generic explainer could use independent icons, a paired percentage card and a definition slide. That is an illustrative baseline authored by the auditor, with no independent blind comparator run. It has fewer persistent relationships and can imply the actual payment changed. The test plan retains cash-flow identity across cuts and changes visual representation exactly where the explanation moves from one-year ratio to full term. The playbook supplies authentic-claim handling, restrained annotations, holds and layered asset decisions. The arithmetic, fixed-term assumptions, current-yield terminology and timeline layout come from finance and general visual literacy. Exact terms should be checked against a primary investor reference before production. All six written scenes are feasible as procedural originals. Production readiness still requires a timed proof and genuine-source capability.

The auditor independently verified the current-yield denominator against the [FINRA-hosted MSRB reference guide](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2019-10/2019_AR_Rules_Reference_Guide.pdf). The fresh agent did not receive this check when drafting.
