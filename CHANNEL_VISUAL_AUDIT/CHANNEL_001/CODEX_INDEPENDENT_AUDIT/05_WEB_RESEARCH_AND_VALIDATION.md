# Web research and validation

Accessed 2026-11-22 using web search and direct opens. External sources establish methods or corroborate individual claims. Channel production software and original layer structure remain unknown.

| Primary source | Application and limits |
|---|---|
| [PySceneDetect API](https://www.scenedetect.com/api/) | Content and adaptive detectors score adjacent-frame changes. Large overlay changes can resemble cuts and low-contrast cuts can be missed. Audit adjacent decoded frames and stable composition rather than promoting every peak to an edit. |
| [FFmpeg filters](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#scdet) | scdet reports scene-change scores and timestamps. showinfo supplies decoded presentation timestamps. Thresholds are candidate generators. PTS is preferable to nominal frame-rate multiplication for variable-rate media. |
| [Whisper paper](https://cdn.openai.com/papers/whisper.pdf) and [WhisperX paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.00747) | Whisper generates ASR with timestamps. WhisperX combines voice activity segmentation with forced phoneme alignment for word boundaries. Test numbers and proper nouns against actual audio, retain unaligned words explicitly and measure error on a hand-timed subset. The local small.en JSON does not establish WhisperX use. |
| [Adobe track mattes](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/using/track-mattes-and-traveling-mattes.html) and [shape and mask path animation](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/desktop/animate-in-after-effects/animate-shape-paths-and-masks/animating-shape-paths-masks.html) | Alpha/luma mattes, keyed shapes, masks and interpolation support the visible effects. Hold keys or layer-visibility cuts can produce replacement drawings. A vector path reveal and pre-rendered replacements can yield similar pixels. |
| [scikit-learn grouped cross-validation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html) | Grouping by source avoids train/test leakage from same-video recurrence. Video-level resampling does not correct convenience-sample bias, validate labels or establish causation. |
| [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) | Record the captured entity, publisher/capture activity and crop/annotation derivation. Hash proves identity of stored bytes. A capture can still quote the wrong vintage or omit a qualifier. |
| [U.S. Copyright Office Fair Use Index](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/) | Permission, license and context matter. Public availability and attribution alone give no blanket reproduction permission. Document the asset-specific rights basis and scope of use. |
| [Private Credit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7uu6qaCGZsY) and [America Is Quietly Drowning in Debt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7K991LgY3tc) | Publisher pages match the local bracketed IDs and titles, credit Casual Finance, and point to its casuallyfinance account. The direct [channel-ID page](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCrNfRFWnLC3tCy1TnyhVQ7g) was throttled. The exact ID is also recorded in the local coordination registry. This check does not resolve every legacy filename. |
| [EIA Annual Energy Review](https://www.eia.gov/totalenergy/data/annual/pdf/sec5.pdf) | 2006 net imports 12,391 thousand b/d and estimated consumption 20,697 thousand b/d, ratio 59.87%. Corroborates the oil figure with the **net imports of petroleum** definition. Exact on-screen widget vintage unverified. |
| [New York Fed 2025 Q4 Household Debt and Credit](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/interactives/householdcredit/data/pdf/hhdc_2025q4.pdf) | Verifies $1.28T credit card balances outstanding at the snapshot date. An aggregate balance does not by itself prove individual distress. |
| [FDIC Quarterly Banking Profile glossary](https://www.fdic.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/qbp_0.pdf) and [Federal Reserve terms](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2025-december-supervision-and-regulation-report-data-sources-terms.htm) | NIM uses interest income less interest expense relative to average earning assets. This rejects the transfer scene's two-rate-gap equivalence. |
| [U.S. Courts 2025 filings release](https://www.uscourts.gov/data-news/judiciary-news/2026/02/04/bankruptcy-filings-rise-11-percent) | Confirms 24,737 versus 23,107 business filings and 7.1% rounded annual growth. This verifies the number even though the exact dark card's publisher and the separate decade-high characterization remain unresolved. |
| [BLS rent and OER factsheet](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/factsheets/owners-equivalent-rent-and-rent.htm) | Corroborates the sixth-root operation for the monthly index and the underlying six-month comparison. Relevant to the R9b spoken/visual distinction. |
| [FINRA-hosted MSRB guidance, current yield](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2019-10/2019_AR_Rules_Reference_Guide.pdf) and [FINRA TRACE](https://syndication.finra.org/content/what-trace-and-how-can-it-help-me) | Confirms the annual dollar interest to purchase price definition and provides a source for real eligible bond trades. A historical trade may differ from executable price or need an accrued-interest convention. |
