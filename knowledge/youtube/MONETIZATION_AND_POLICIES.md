# Monetization and policies

**OFFICIAL_CURRENT — checked 2026-10-15; recheck before publication/application.** YouTube distinguishes repetitive/mass-produced “inauthentic content” from reused content lacking meaningful original contribution. Reused-content eligibility is separate from copyright permission. A reusable format alone does not certify monetization; original educational/entertainment value matters. The fetched policy also includes restrictions on AI personas presenting sensitive-topic expert advice, including financial guidance. Preserve that distinction when planning finance content. [Channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392).

For ad-revenue eligibility, the documented routes are 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months, or 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days. Shorts Feed watch hours do not count toward the watch-hour route. Meeting thresholds still requires eligibility and review. [YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851).

Expanded YPP, where available, offers earlier fan-funding/Shopping access at 500 subscribers, three public uploads in 90 days, and either 3,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months or three million qualified Shorts views in 90 days. This is not the full ad-revenue threshold; check country and feature availability. [Expanded YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240).

**Temporal limit.** These are page observations on the check date, not an archived policy history. The overview also displays a future February 2027 terms notice; do not treat future-dated provisions as already effective. Older DS-000011 compilation claims and third-party revenue estimates remain unverified. [Training manifest](../../data/manifests/DS-000011.md).
