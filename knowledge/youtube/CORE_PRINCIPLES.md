# Core principles

1. **OFFICIAL_CURRENT, checked 2026-10-15:** evaluate appeal, engagement and satisfaction together. Packaging sets expectations; the opening should deliver on them. [YouTube performance guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650).
2. **SOURCE_OPINION:** the DS-000031 feedback-loop framework treats publishing as hypothesis → measurement → adjustment. Use it to design learning, not to claim knowledge of the recommender's internal weights. [Creator-strategy register](../../data/intelligence/global/creator-strategy-knowledge-DS-000031.json).
3. **PROJECT METHOD:** preserve a claim's original evidence layer, dataset, video identity, date and exact source locator. [Provenance](../../docs/PROVENANCE.md).
4. **AI_INTERPRETATION:** a popular video is a candidate for explanation, not a controlled experiment proving a particular thumbnail, hook or edit worked. Compare same-format, age-aware peers and disclose confounders. [Existing finance playbook locators](../../research/finance-economics-intelligence/EXISTING_DATA_INVENTORY.csv).
5. **PROJECT METHOD:** channel and niche evidence stays separate. Cross-niche confidence requires independent coverage, not repeated summaries of two channels. [Calibration artifact](../../data/intelligence/system/cross-niche-strategy-calibration-2026-09-11.json).
