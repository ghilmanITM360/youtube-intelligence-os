# Title and thumbnail

**SOURCE_OPINION.** DS-000011 contains a Packaging SOP and natural-language Title Analyzer. They can prompt review of clarity, curiosity and the combined promise. The SOP's 10%+ CTR goal is a source target, not a universal benchmark or platform threshold. [Original source manifest](../../data/manifests/DS-000011.md).

**OFFICIAL_CURRENT — checked 2026-10-15.** Titles/thumbnails communicate value and set viewer expectations. [YouTube performance guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650).

YouTube's native A/B tool compares up to three title/thumbnail options and optimizes for watch time, rather than CTR alone. It is desktop Studio only, requires advanced features, and excludes Shorts, scheduled live streams, pre-completion Premieres, private, made-for-kids and mature-audience videos. Archived live streams can qualify. Tests can take up to two weeks and may be inconclusive. [A/B eligibility and interpretation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400).

**PROJECT METHOD.** A thumbnail URL is not a locally verified asset. A current CDN image does not prove the upload-time design. Pair any visual claim with the exact image, collection date and video ID. Reuse the deferred legacy-thumbnail plan and validated subset, preserving their scope.
