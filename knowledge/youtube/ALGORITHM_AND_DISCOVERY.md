# Algorithm and discovery

**OFFICIAL_CURRENT — checked 2026-10-15.** Recommendations use viewer personalization and video performance. Average view duration, average percentage viewed, likes and post-watch surveys are among the described signals. Topic interest, competition and seasonality also affect distribution; good metrics alone do not ensure impressions. [YouTube search and discovery](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225).

**Existing source reuse.** DS-000018 separates claims about per-viewer selection, subscribers and Shorts from recommendations and ambiguous assertions. Start with its exact ALG26 claim IDs and tab/timestamp locators, then consult the older validation as historical context. [Claim register](../../data/intelligence/global/youtube-algorithm-source-intelligence-2026-09-02.md), [2026-09-04 validation](../../data/intelligence/global/youtube-algorithm-primary-validation-2026-09-04.md).

**OFFICIAL_CURRENT — checked 2026-10-15.** The Shorts guidance states there is no minimum posting cadence required for performance and that Shorts performance does not negatively affect long-form recommendations. This does not establish that every channel has the same audience for both formats. [Shorts guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts).

**Unverified opinions.** The recovered algorithm workbook's warnings about mixing formats, dead channels and email-specific treatment remain creator claims. Do not use them as mandatory operating rules. [Drive review](DRIVE_REVIEW.md).
