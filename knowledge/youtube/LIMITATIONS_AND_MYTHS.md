# Limitations and myths

| Claim found or commonly implied | Evidence decision | Reusable correction |
|---|---|---|
| Mixing Shorts and long-form necessarily kills growth | Creator opinion in recovered algorithm workbook | Official Shorts guidance says Shorts performance does not hurt long-form recommendations; audience fit still needs study. |
| Every video needs 10%+ CTR | Goal in DS-000011 Packaging SOP | No universal threshold established here; native tests optimize watch time. |
| A channel stops growing because its videos end sessions | Claim in the recovered Revival & Growth Plan | Unsupported causal diagnosis; no owner experiment supplied with that claim. |
| High CTR/watch time/SEO/upload frequency guarantees virality | Unsupported single-factor inference | Personalization, competition, interest and satisfaction matter. |
| A report named “Verified Creator OS” is official | False attribution | It is a compiled framework with mixed provenance. |
| View-based performance labels prove a hook or animation caused success | Causal overreach | Public snapshots support association only. |
| Two summaries/exports corroborate each other | Duplicate-evidence risk | Group by original source/video/claim family first. |
