# Dr Rehan Brief 2 source evidence

Public, signed-out YouTube Browser observations on 16 Nov 2026, with the original UTC times embedded in individual captures. PK was the initial location. The US pass set United States in the YouTube UI and used `gl=US&hl=en` where applicable. The year window and Popularity controls were applied in the public search interface. Search rank is the visible order within the captured query and region. Rank can include off-topic material. The specific title autocomplete check used PK. No Data API, paid tool, account sign-in, or image downloads were used.

- `search-*.json`, `shorts-search.json`, `region-us-parameter.json`, and `youtube-autocomplete-query-search.json` record public results and settings. `title-search-and-autocomplete.json` and `autocomplete-alternates.json` preserve visible suggestions in order and keep alternate key phrases separate.
- `youtube-watch-observations.json` records public watch metadata and whether a transcript panel yielded segments. Expanded watch views are exact where shown. K/M counts are rounded. Relative publication labels and any unavailable fields remain visible. Caption panel hashes cover the captured JSON list of timestamp/text segments serialized in UTF-8 with Python default JSON separators and `ensure_ascii=False`. The full transcripts were only used for internal review, and the figures in 09 are counts or interpretation from them.
- `seed-db-index.json` records observed historical metadata and caption pointers/hashes. The unmodified source is `origin/research/senior-health-apify-2026-11-11` at `9b23e0612ab0723d794fdfb00085e7e45c816513`, `data/catalog/senior_health/senior_health_core.db` and `senior_health_transcripts_{1,2,3}.db` in the same directory. Clone evidence uses section 2 of `research/senior-health-forensics/SYNTHESIS_44_channels.md`. Repeated templates and multiple uploads from the same channel are observations with limited independence.
- `sample-provenance.json` links selected and extra seeds to watch URLs, caption source and search context. Six examples per long-form topic include high-ranked relevant public results, seeds and clinician/institution comparators. Comparator format can be shorter and the title can cover a broader topic. Delivery, story type, first payoff and checklist flags in 09 are inferred. Where captions have time cues, timings are estimates against displayed runtime. Rehook counts measure explicit phrase matches and undercount nonverbal or other implicit rehooks.
- `thumbnail-observations.json` contains analyst transcription and descriptions of covers shown by the browser. There are no image copies. Some covers or exact words were unavailable.
- `clinical-source-index.json` and `clinician-source-index.json` contain URLs consulted on the date. The facts and judgments drawn from them are in 08. `google-news-7d.json`, `fda-current-views.json`, and `fda-registry-check.json` contain observed headlines and agency checks. Headline appearance is a discovery observation. Future agency status and the Brief 1 T1 capture are time-dependent.
- `reference-framework-applications.json` records the applicable v1 guidance and exceptions with reference member, section and source IDs. The workspace structural gate was run separately.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` verifies the files in this dated folder. Check with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt` from this directory.
