Let's start with activities of daily living or ADL's.
ADL's are foundational self-care skills that we usually learn as very
young children.
They're also sometimes called basic ADL's or BADL's.
They include walking,
or otherwise getting around the home or outside,
also feeding,
as in being able to get food from a plate into
one's mouth,
dressing, as in selecting appropriate clothes for the weather and the
occasion and being able to put them on,
then there's grooming,
which means adequately managing your personal appearance,
such as managing your hair,
doing your makeup or shaving.
Next toileting,
which means getting to and from the toilet,
using it appropriately,
and cleaning oneself.
And then there's bathing,
which means washing one's face and body in the bath or
shower.
