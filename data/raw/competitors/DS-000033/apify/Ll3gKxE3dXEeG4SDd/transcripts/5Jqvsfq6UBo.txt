as a geriatric medical doctor one of the top 
questions I get from families is this how do
I get my aging parent with memory loss to accept 
or understand that they need help so here's the
truth based on my years of experience as 
a geriatrician in the early stages of this
memory loss Journey many older adults won't be 
able to accept or understand that they need help
so if you want to be able to help your parent you 
actually need to do what we do as geriatricians
stop the explanations and stop focusing on getting 
them to accept or understand and when you do this
two things will happen first you'll have fewer 
arguments with them and next you'll be freed up to
practice a better way of speaking to them in which 
you work with and around their reality instead of
fighting to change their reality if you'd like to
