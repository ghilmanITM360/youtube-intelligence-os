So what causes high blood pressure? Most
high blood pressure is what we call
primary hypertension. That means it's
not due to an easily identifiable
medical condition or medication. So the
causes and risks of primary hypertension
include advancing age. That's because
getting older tends to make blood
vessels stiffer and that increases blood
pressure. Obesity, genetics, family
history, high sodium intake, high
alcohol intake, physical inactivity,
insufficient sleep, social stressors,
and noise and air pollution are all
factors that have been identified as
risk factors for primary hypertension.
And then some people do have what we
call secondary hypertension, which means
it's due to medications, drugs, or
certain medical conditions. And I wanted
to highlight this because as you'll see
part of the treatment of high blood
pressure is to check for these causes of
secondary hypertension.
