What would happen if you literally 
had all of the world's money?
I don’t know how you would actually gather 
all the world’s money –let's just assume you
invented some kind of money-summoning magic spell.
Most of the world’s wealth isn’t physical money:
it’s land and houses, bank accounts, businesses, 
intellectual property, cryptocurrency, and so on.
Getting all the world's cash is much 
more straightforward. At any given time,
there's a certain amount in the world 
–right now it's worth somewhere north of 10
trillion dollars– and you want it all.
So you cast your magic spell and
summon all the money.
The pile of cash is taller
than the statue of liberty and heavier than 
the empire state building. You probably don't
want to be standing under it, so let's assume 
you're standing over here, off to the side.
In total, US coins and bills are responsible 
for about 20% of the pile's weight, while the
European Union contributes roughly 25%.
Unfortunately for you, the pile doesn't
stay a pile for long, and what seems 
like a safe distance isn't so safe.
As it collapses, the pile spreads outward, a wave 
of money carrying a staggering amount of momentum.
The pennies, quarters, loonies, 
and euros scour the landscape in
an expanding ring. Within seconds, the 
wave of coins engulfs you and you die.
There are ways to avoid this. You could, say, 
build a wall to contain the coins and then summon
them. Unfortunately, now you might face a problem 
worse than death: Building code violations.
