There are some vitamins and nutrients
found in those which are missing from
the narrower range of mammal, skeletal
muscle, and fat in the typical Western
diet. The US experienced meat shortages

social scientists, and food scientists

bigger problem. contaminated food. Even
if you cooked your meat, it would be
hard to avoid all kinds of disease
exposure as you worked your way through
the remains of the human population. In
a small enough population, every
outbreak is a pandemic. It wouldn't take

Let's consider a different scenario, one
perhaps more in line with what Quinn was
imagining. What if half the population
ate the other half? If the average human
