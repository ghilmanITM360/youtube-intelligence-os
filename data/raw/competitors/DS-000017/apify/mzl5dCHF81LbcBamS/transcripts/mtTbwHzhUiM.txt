If we ever build a Moon base, I think we should 
absolutely build a big swimming pool there. Sure,
sending a swimming pool's worth of 
water to the Moon's surface would
be expensive. But on the other hand, this 
lunar base is going to have people on it,
so you need to send some water anyway.
And it's not really that hard, or expensive,
in the grand scheme of things. A large backyard 
swimming pool weighs about as much as four Apollo
lunar landers. We don’t have any functioning 
Apollo Saturn V rockets, but NASA’s SLS launch
system is ultimately intended to carry around 
the same amount as a Saturn V for the cost
of a few billion dollars per launch, and a 
private space company would be even cheaper.
For comparison, the budget for the summer olympic 
games is typically upwards of ten billion dollars.
So maybe the next step, if you really want 
a swimming pool on the Moon, is to invite
the heads of NASA and the International 
Olympic Committee to your next pool party.
