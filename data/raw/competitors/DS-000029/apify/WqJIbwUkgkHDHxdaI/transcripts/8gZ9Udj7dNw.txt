Exercise two, suriashi, sliding step

This gliding step comes from traditional

What makes this different from ordinary

Stand with your knees softly bent and
your weight lowered slightly as if

Slide one foot forward along the floor

Continue this gliding step across a

slow and deliberate.
Because your foot stays in contact with
