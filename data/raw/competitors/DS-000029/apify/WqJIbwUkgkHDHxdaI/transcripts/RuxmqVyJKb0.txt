Exercise two.
Weight shifting side to side.

Stand behind a sturdy chair with both

Exercise four, standing marches.
When you trip over something, a rug, a
step, a crack in the sidewalk, your body

Stand tall behind your chair, hands

Hold for 2 seconds at the top, then

Repeat on the left side.
That is one rep.
