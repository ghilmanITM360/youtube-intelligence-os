The 10-Second Balance Test Every Senior Should Try
Can you pass the 10-second balance test?
Doctors often use balance as a simple 
way to check mobility and stability.
Here’s the test.
Stand up.
Lift one foot slightly off the ground.
Now try to balance for 10 seconds.
If you can hold it comfortably, your 
balance and leg strength are likely good.
If it feels difficult, it may mean your 
muscles or coordination need strengthening.
The good news?
Balance can improve
with regular movement and simple exercises.
