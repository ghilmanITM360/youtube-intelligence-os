Coffee can temporarily raise your blood pressure 
as well as heart rate. [music] So if you drink
coffee and check your blood pressure at the same 
time, you might not be getting your actual blood
pressure. You might be getting a falsely high 
reading. For an appropriate blood pressure
monitoring, avoid caffeine for at least 30 minutes 
before checking the blood pressures. In that case,
you'll get the correct numbers. But 
here's the important part. Coffee is
not responsible for causing high blood pressure 
or hypertension. It only temporarily raises the
blood pressure because of its stimulant 
effect on the brain. Should you actually
reduce the amount of coffee after the age 
of 50? That depends on your requirement,
your underlying conditions, your body 
habits and your craving for the coffee.
