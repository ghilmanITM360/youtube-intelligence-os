Do you get out of breath doing simple tasks? 
This could be your heart sending a warning.
Many people blame aging—but it 
might be something more serious.
Shortness of breath during light activities—like 
walking or climbing stairs—can happen when the
heart isn’t pumping blood efficiently.
This reduces oxygen supply, making you
feel breathless faster than normal.
Experts say this is a commonly
missed early sign of heart problems.
Doctors advise: if breathlessness is new or
worsening, don’t ignore it—get it checked early.
Your body may be giving you more warning signs.
