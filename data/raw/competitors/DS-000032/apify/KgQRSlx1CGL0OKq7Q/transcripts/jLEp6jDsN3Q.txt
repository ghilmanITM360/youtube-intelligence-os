Cardiovascular diseases are the number one causes 
of mortality in the world and the most important
presentation of cardiovascular disease is heart 
attack. So in order to get immediate help from
the healthcare providers, one should know what are 
the features or clinical features of heart attack.
So if there is persistent central 
chest pain that is not localized,
that is associated with sweating, diaphoresis or 
with shortness of breath, radiation of pain to
the jaws or the arms and that is accompanied by 
anger amnii which is the fear of impending death
that most likely indicates underlying heart 
attack or ongoing ischemia or ongoing angina
and the patient should be immediately rushed to 
a hospital which has cardiovascular facilities.
