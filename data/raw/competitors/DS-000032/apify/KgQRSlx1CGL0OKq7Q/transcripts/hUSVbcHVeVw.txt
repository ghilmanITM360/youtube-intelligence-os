5 Foods That Clean Your Arteries
These 5 foods help support healthy arteries.
Arteries slowly narrow when cholesterol and 
inflammation build up inside blood vessels.
But certain foods contain nutrients that 
support circulation and heart health.
Here are five cardiologist-recommended foods:
1️⃣ Fatty fish like salmon – rich in omega-3 fats
2️⃣ Berries – packed with antioxidants
3️⃣ Leafy greens – support blood vessel function
4️⃣ Nuts – help maintain 
healthy cholesterol levels
5️⃣ Olive oil – a key part of 
the Mediterranean heart diet
No single food can magically fix arteries.
But these foods can support
long-term cardiovascular health.
