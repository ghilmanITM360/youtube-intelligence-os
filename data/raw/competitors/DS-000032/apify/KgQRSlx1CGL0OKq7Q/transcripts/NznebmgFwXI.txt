Circulation plays a crucial role in delivering
oxygen and nutrients throughout the body. When 
blood flow becomes less efficient, extremities,
especially the feet, are often affected first.

symptoms like cold feet. Cold feet sometimes are

Your feet are the farthest part of the body
from your heart. Blood must travel through long 
arteries to reach them. When circulation slows
or arteries narrows, less warm blood reaches the 
feet. Healthy arteries allow blood to move freely
throughout the body. But as people age, arteries 
can gradually become less flexible or narrower.
This may slow blood flow to the lower legs and 
feet. Reduced circulation can sometimes cause
symptoms such as cold feet, numbness, tingling, 
fatigue while walking. Many people ignore these
signals. But sometimes the body is trying to
