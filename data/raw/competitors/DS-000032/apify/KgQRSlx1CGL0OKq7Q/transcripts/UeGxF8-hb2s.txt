If you're over 60, how many times should
you be able to stand up from the chair
in 30 seconds? 10 times?
20 times?
30 times? You may have seen one number
online.
However, there is no one single passing
score for everybody. This is the
30-second chair stand test that
clinicians use to assess the endurance
and lower leg weakness in patients who
are at the age of 60. You sit down
properly, put your feet down, cross your
arms across your chest, and then stand
up and sit count one, then stand up and
sit count two, and you do that for the
30 seconds when you put your timer on.
The number will dictate the degree of
your mobility, degree of your strength,
your endurance, and your fitness. If you
want to know more about 30-seconds chair
stand test, watch my video in the link
below.
