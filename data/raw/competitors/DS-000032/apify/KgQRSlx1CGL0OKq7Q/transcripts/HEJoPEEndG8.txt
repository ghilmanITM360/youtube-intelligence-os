Are you feeling tired all the time… even 
without doing much? This could be an
early warning sign from your heart.
Most people ignore this symptom—but
doctors say it can appear weeks or even 
months before serious heart issues.
When your heart isn’t pumping efficiently, 
your muscles don’t get enough oxygen.
This leads to persistent fatigue—even after rest.
According to heart health experts, unexplained
fatigue is one of the most overlooked early 
signs of heart disease, especially in Seniors
Cardiologists emphasize fatigue without a clear 
reason should never be ignored—especially if it’s
new or getting worse.

warning signs your body might be showing.
