Have you Noticed swelling in your ankles? Don’t 
ignore it—it could be more than just fatigue.
Many seniors think it’s normal… but in some 
cases, it may signal an underlying heart issue.
When circulation becomes less efficient, fluid 
can build up in the lower legs and ankles.
This condition, known as fluid retention, may 
be linked to how well your heart is working.
Health experts warn that persistent or 
worsening swelling should always be checked.
Doctors say early detection of these signs 
can help prevent more serious complications.
