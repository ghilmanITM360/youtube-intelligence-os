Number two, salmon with the skin. Salmon

flow and protect joint health. Practical
tip: grill or bake salmon with the skin

olive oil. Aim to eat it two to three
times per week. Number three, eggs.
Don't skip the yolk. While the egg white

vitamins essential for the collagen
synthesis process. Practical tip, start
your day with two boiled or poached egg

for a collagen boosting breakfast.
Number four, citrus fruits. Your body

Oranges, lemons, and grapefruits are
packed with this vital nutrient, which
acts as a catalyst to bind protein
molecules into strong collagen fibers.
Practical tip: squeeze fresh lemon juice
into a glass of warm water each morning.
It's a simple habit that kickstarts your
day and your collagen production. Number
five, berries. Strawberries,
blueberries, and raspberries are not

proteinpacked snack that fights collagen
loss. Number six, avocado. This creamy

your joints. Number seven, leafy greens.
Spinach, kale, and Swiss chard are
