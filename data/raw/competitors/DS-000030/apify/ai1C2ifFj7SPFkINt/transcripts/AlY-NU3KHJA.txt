This recipe is simple, warm, light, and
designed to support a more balanced meal
pattern. Recipe: Corn porridge with
broccoli for vascular support
ingredients. 3 Tbsp cornmeal or fine
corn grits, 1 and 1/2 cups water. A
small pinch of salt, 1 tsp extra virgin
olive oil.
Half a cup broccoli florets, finely
chopped.
Optional, a spoon of plain yogurt on the
side.
Optional, a little garlic or black
pepper for flavor.
How to make it. First, bring the water
to a gentle boil and add a very small
pinch of salt.
Then, slowly pour in the cornmeal while
stirring continuously.
This helps prevent lumps and gives the
porridge a smooth texture.
Lower the heat and cook for about 8 to
12 minutes, depending on the grind of
the cornmeal.
Stir often so it stays creamy and
doesn't stick. At the same time, steam
the broccoli for about 4 to 5 minutes,
just until tender but still bright
green.
Do not overcook it into mush.
Overcooked broccoli loses texture and
becomes much less appealing.
Once the porridge is ready, add 1 tsp of
olive oil and stir.
Place the porridge in a bowl and top it
with the steamed broccoli.
If you want, add a spoon of plain yogurt
on the side or tiny bit of garlic and
black pepper for extra flavor.
