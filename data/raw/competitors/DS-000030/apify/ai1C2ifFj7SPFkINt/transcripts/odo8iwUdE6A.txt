One, the morning rise from floor to
standing. This isn't just about standing

Japanese homes traditionally have low

Two, the spinal wave. Cat stretching. In
Japan, even school children begin their

based on the natural stretching you see

for maintaining spinal health.
The practice.
Kneel on all fours, hands under
shoulders. Inhale as you drop your
belly. Lift your chin and tailbone.
Exhale as you round your spine, tucking
chin and tailbone.
Flow smoothly between these positions
eight to 10 times. The hidden benefit,

Three, the foot awakening, ankle and
toe, mobility. Japanese elders

It's crucial. This prevents stiff

Four. The balance walk. Heel to toe
tandem walking. You'll see Japanese
seniors practicing this along garden
paths and park lines. It looks simple
but challenges your balance profoundly.
How to practice safely. Find a clear
path or use a line on the floor. Place
the heel of one foot directly in front
of the other foot's toe. Walk slowly,
arms out for balance if needed. Start
with 10 to 15 steps. build to one to two
minutes. The science. This activates the
same balance systems used when
recovering from slips and trips. Just 60
seconds daily can reduce fall risk by up
