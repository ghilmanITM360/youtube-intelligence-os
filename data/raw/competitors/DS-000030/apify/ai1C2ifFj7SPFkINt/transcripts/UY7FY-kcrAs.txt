Correct clove water recipe.
Ingredients: two whole dried cloves, not
ground, not more.
One cup hot water just below boiling,
around 200° F / 93° C.
Method:
One,
place the two whole cloves in a mug.
Two,
pour the hot water over them.
Three,
cover the mug immediately with a small
plate or lid. This step is
non-negotiable.
It traps the volatile oils that would
otherwise evaporate.
Four, steep for exactly 10 minutes, not
five, not 15.
10 minutes is the sweet spot for
extracting beneficial compounds without
releasing excess essential oils that can
irritate the stomach.
Five, remove the cloves or strain the
liquid. Six, drink it warm, sipping
slowly over 5 to 10 minutes. Timing:
drink this 1 hour after your evening
meal, not immediately before bed. This
allows the compounds to work during the
overnight repair window without
interfering with sleep or clashing with
food digestion.
Frequency:
three to four times per week, not daily.
Continuous daily use of potent
botanicals can lead to sensitization or
cumulative effects. Cycling, using it
every other day or a few times weekly,
prevents tolerance and allows your body
to reset.
