## Original-content workflow

INTERPRETATION: This blueprint applies general explanation techniques observed in the corpus. It is not a verified growth recipe or permission to reproduce source art/scripts.

1. Define one audience question and intended answer. Start with a dated, bounded contradiction that evidence can support; compare SpaceX0–43s or bond0–18s for structure, not wording.
2. Build a claim ledger before scripting: exact source, date, units, denominator, observed number, interpretation and forecast condition. Check cash burn versus net loss and adjusted EBITDA; SpaceXC18/C20 demonstrate the cost of imprecise labels. Resolve/flag conflicts rather than choosing the most dramatic number.
3. Write an original hook, promise, prerequisite explanation, evidence, caveat and payoff. Use a familiar comparator whose measurement basis matches the main statistic. A future outcome keeps if/target/projected wording.
4. Map each semantic beat to an original diagram or character action. Begin with one object/relationship, add only the next needed dependency, then isolate the consequence. Borrow the general pension-obligation or eligibility-metaphor technique, not literal drawings or dialogue.
5. Create reusable original assets: narrator, functional financial props, source-card layout, annotation marks, charts and captions. Keep cross-channel asset/style decisions separated. Appearance evidence does not prescribe Adobe/Blender/AI tools.
6. Animate state changes with intentional reveals, source highlights and short pose changes. Estimate readability before setting shot length; the pooled rates in EDITING_DNA describe this corpus and are not targets. Keep a timing sheet with separate shots, states and motions.
7. Record/mix original narration. Conduct real listening for intelligibility, music masking and confirmed effects; this source study cannot choose a verified voice/music style. Check visual triggers against playback, not nearest-ASR timestamps alone.
8. For a sponsor, make the interruption identifiable, avoid importing demo examples into the main evidence argument, and return to the promised mechanism. SpaceX465–557s/557sreturn illustrates continuity but not optimal placement.
9. Draft complementary title and thumbnail with one legible phrase/relationship. Inspect actual320/160derivatives; remove tiny data labels or enlarge only the crucial figure. Packaging must not assert a stronger financial outcome than the script establishes.
10. Validate source ledger, transcript numbers, caption legibility, timeline coverage, event links, original-asset rights and per-format outputs. Store evidence separately from interpretation. Ask a second reviewer to inspect crucial visual/audio claims.
11. Evaluate after publication using real impressions,CTR,retention,traffic and matched topic/age cohorts when available. Pre-register what would falsify the hook/pace hypothesis. No universal view guarantee.
