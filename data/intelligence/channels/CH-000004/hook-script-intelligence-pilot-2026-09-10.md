# Hook Hypotheses

- `CH-000004-OP-001-HOOK-A` linked to `CH-000004-OP-001-PKG-A`: Could you survive one nanosecond in every layer of the Sun? The answer changes depending on which layer you enter.
- `CH-000004-OP-001-HOOK-B` linked to `CH-000004-OP-001-PKG-B`: One nanosecond is one billionth of a second. Inside the Sun, that can still mean three very different exposures.
- `CH-000004-OP-001-HOOK-C` linked to `CH-000004-OP-001-PKG-C`: The Sun's hottest layer may not be the layer that hurts you most in one nanosecond. Temperature is only one part of the exposure.
- `CH-000004-OP-002-HOOK-A` linked to `CH-000004-OP-002-PKG-A`: Could Earth ever produce a magnitude 10 earthquake? The scale allows it, but the planet may not.
- `CH-000004-OP-002-HOOK-B` linked to `CH-000004-OP-002-PKG-B`: Earthquake magnitude has no mathematical maximum. Earth has a finite size, so the real question is what fault the planet can supply.
- `CH-000004-OP-002-HOOK-C` linked to `CH-000004-OP-002-PKG-C`: To make a magnitude 10 earthquake on Earth, start with the fault. How long would it need to be before the planet itself becomes the constraint?
