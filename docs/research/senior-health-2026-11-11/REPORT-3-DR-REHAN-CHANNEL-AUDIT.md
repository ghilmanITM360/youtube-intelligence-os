### 2.3 Script / opening (28 LF transcripts parhe: opening ~75 words + ending)
- **Do alag styles (OBSERVED):**
  - (a) **Unscripted lecture**, jaise "Hi, a very important point of discussion lipid profile…" (`BCNWQ3uuSuE`) aur "Hi, today I'm going to discuss a very very important topic…" (`3dRcNqiLHgY`).
  - (b) **Scripted hook-first**, jaise "That's the number staring back at you before you've even had your breakfast…" (`EB3_A2b4a6o`).
  - Channel ka best video (Lp(a)) style (a) mein hai. Isliye **topic > hook style** (INFERRED).
- **Voice mixing:** kuch videos third-person narrator use karti hain ("Here's Dr. Rehan Omar… That means he opens blocked heart arteries" `sBi5nhTtUQ8`; "Let's hear from Dr. Rehan Umar" `-fY4QrGAlrY`), aur baqi first-person hain.
- **Naam inconsistent hai:** description mein "Omer", transcripts mein "Omar/Rahan/Bhanor/Umar". Inmein ASR errors bhi hain.
- **Endings:** zyada tar generic hain ("click the bell icon… subsequent videos with different topics" `EB3_A2b4a6o`). Sirf 5 mein specific next-video loop hai (`5oPzZjfNgII`, `NznebmgFwXI`, `qC8BKb8Fh5Q`, `xI-KdZ96xcI`, `t1j-p9pkw3Y`). Yeh baad ki videos mein behtar hua hai.
- **Depth:** 535 words mein ek topic ki explanation, mechanism, aur "kab doctor ko dikhaein" sab cover nahi ho sakte. Competitor ka 3,000+ words wala listicle spine (5 points + stories + sources) bilkul alag product hai.

## 4. Competitive weaknesses Rehan exploit kar sakte hain (INFERRED)
1. Network channels ke doctors unverified hain, aur unke titles packaging-copy hain (Report 2 K.2). Ek **verifiable cardiologist** "trust" niche mein alag nazar aayega.
2. Test/number explainers competitors mein kam hain. Network zyada tar foods, warnings aur habits par hai.
3. Competitor scripts lambe aur story-padded hain. Rehan ka clinical directness 8–12 min ke focused format mein strength ban sakta hai (HYPOTHESIS).
