## 5. YouTube Analytics measurement framework
Official categories (Help Center 9717005, fetched 2026-11-11): Reach, Interactions/Engagement, Audience; special reports (age & gender, retention, first 24 h, real-time); filters: new/casual/regular viewers.

| Question (CS-023) | Metric | When |
|---|---|---|
| Koi dekh raha hai? (No traffic) | Impressions, traffic source (Browse/Suggested/Search) | Day 7 / 28 per video |
| Click karte hain? (No click) | Impressions CTR (sirf same traffic source ke andar compare karein) | Day 7 / 28 |
| Dekhte rehte hain? (No watch) | AVD, APV, 30-second retention, retention curve dips | Day 7 |
| Wapas aate hain? | Returning / regular viewers, subs per 1k views | Monthly |
| Audience sahi hai? | Age, geography | After 10 videos |
| Quality/safety | QA checklist pass %, corrections count | Har video |

- **Learning log (CS-009):** har video ke baad 5 cheezein likhein: kya expect kiya tha, kya hua, kyun (hypothesis), agla test, aur kya dohraana hai.
