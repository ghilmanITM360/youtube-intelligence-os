## 4. Recommended positioning
**Direction A primary + C ek recurring format (20–30% experiments).** Tagline idea: "Know what's safe after 60."

Wajah:
- Sab se strong independent evidence (Report 2 I #1–3).
- Competition kam hai.
- Network ka "pills increase risk" fear-angle saturated hai, jabke **comparison aur "should I take it?" decision-guide** underused hai.
- Credentialed presenter ke saath policy-safe hai.

B ko dusri experiment lane ke taur par rakhein. Agar presenter physio ho to B primary ban sakta hai. Yeh **presenter availability** par depend karta hai, jo user ka faisla hai.

**Confidence: Medium.** Evidence mostly ek channel (BHWA) se hai. Search demand (Trends/YouTube search) ka validation baqi hai.

## 9. Script templates
**Decision-guide spine** (Hook guide: Context Lean → Scroll-Stop → Snapback; aur BUT/THEREFORE):
1. **Hook (0–30 s):** asli scenario + common belief + "but" turn + promise. Misal: "Most people take [X] for years. But after 65 the body handles it differently — here's when that matters." Koi fake patient story nahi; story ho to "composite" disclose karein.
2. Kaun log yeh lete hain aur kyun.
3. Is umar mein kya badalta hai (mechanism, 1 simple analogy).
4. Evidence: guideline ka naam on-screen.
5. Kis ke liye risk hai (checklist).
6. **Safer alternatives / doctor se poochhne ke 3 sawal.**
7. Red flags (emergency).
8. Specific next-video loop.

**Comparison spine:** use-case → A ka profile → B ka profile → side-by-side table → "kis ke liye kaunsa" → doctor ke sawal.

**Fact-check spine:** claim (neutral quote, link nahi) → kahan se aaya → evidence grade → kya sach hai → safe alternative.

## 10. Medical sourcing process
1. **Source hierarchy:** guidelines aur health authorities (FDA/NIH/CDC/NICE, AGS Beers Criteria, USPSTF), phir systematic reviews, phir RCTs. Anecdote ko saboot ke taur par nahi.
2. Har script ke saath ek source sheet ho: claim → source → date.
3. **Clinical reviewer sign-off** publish se pehle (naam aur credential description mein).
4. Policy check: YouTube medical misinformation categories (prevention, treatment, denial). Dawai band ya shuru karne ki direct advice nahi.
5. **Correction log:** galti par pinned correction.
6. AI tools ka istemal: research aur script drafts ke liye theek hai. Final medical content insaan review kare. Synthetic/altered realistic content ho to YouTube disclosure ka istemal karein.
