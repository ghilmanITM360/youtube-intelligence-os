# External Small-Channel Breakout Research — Senior Health Niche (Raw Findings, Pass 2)

Research date: 2026-09-25
Method: live, read-only YouTube browsing (search + channel pages + watch pages), no sign-in, no scraping tools, no paid API.
Scope: Senior Health / healthy-aging / senior fitness / senior nutrition / cardiovascular- and brain-health-for-seniors niche, external to the YIOS canonical channel roster.

This is a **second pass**, building on a prior pass that had already documented: `TopHealthTips` (1.3M-view neuropathy-exercises breakout), the `@Dr.AlizaWrightSeniorHealth` persona-clone case (941K vs. 160 views), and light touches on `Fit Lit Health` and `Gentle Health`. Those are **not** re-documented in depth here except where new detail was found. This pass focuses on **genuinely new** channels/cases.

## Comparison / Failure Cases (same script template, no breakout)

The "Surgeon REVEALS: 5 Science-Backed Exercises that REVERSE Aging!" and "Surgeon REVEALS/WARNS: Do THIS 1 Exercise..." templates (identical or near-identical wording to SeniorHood's successful videos) are reused by a large number of small channels that never break out. Four were investigated in depth as required comparison cases:

### Failure Case 1: Senior Mobility
- **Handle:** `@SeniorMobility` — https://www.youtube.com/@SeniorMobility
- **Subscribers:** 198. **Videos:** 13 — all posted within a single ~1-month window (7mo–8mo ago), then the channel went silent (no uploads in the last 7 months as of this research).
- **Best-performing video:** "After 60 A Surgeon Explains the One Move That Improves Leg Circulation" — 5K views (8mo ago) — the channel's only video to clear 1K views; everything else sits between 17 and 2.1K views.
- Its "Orthopedic Surgeon Reveals: 5 Science-Backed Exercises That Most Seniors Are Missing" video (the same script template as siblings below) got only 1K views.
- **Significance:** Same content template and niche as SeniorHood, similar apparent production quality, but the channel stalled at 13 total uploads — never even reached the 20–40-video range where breakouts cluster in the successful cases, and was abandoned. This suggests that reaching a large enough sample of uploads (and/or persisting long enough) may itself be a precondition for a breakout, separate from packaging quality.

### Failure Case 2: Health 365
- **Handle:** `@Health365-DailyGuide` — https://www.youtube.com/@Health365-DailyGuide
- **Subscribers:** 311. **Videos:** 54 (uploaded steadily over ~5 months, 3mo–5mo old in the visible window plus presumably more recent ones not fully re-checked).
- **Best-performing video:** 241 views ("Your Doctor NEVER Told You THIS Snack Lowers Creatinine Fast" was in a different, unrelated search — within this channel's own catalog the ceiling is 241 views, e.g. "SENIORS: The ONE Exercise & Vitamin for Stronger Legs & Knees" type videos). Most videos sit between 27 and ~200 views.
- **Directly comparable content:** This channel has videos titled "One Chair Exercise That Relieves Neuropathy in Feet & Legs After 60" (53 views) and "Surgeon Reveals: THIS 1 CHAIR Exercise YOU Need to Relieve Neuropathy in Feet & Legs FAST" (42 views) and "Surgeon Reveals: Do This 1 Exercise for Poor Leg Circulation After 70" (56 views) — **the same exact topics (neuropathy, leg circulation) that produced Amos Boone's 207K breakout and two of SeniorHood's three breakouts** — with zero breakout effect. This is the strongest same-topic, same-format, no-breakout control case found.
- **Significance:** With 54 total videos, this channel's catalog fully spans the 20th–40th-upload range with no breakout anywhere in it — a clean negative control.

### Failure Case 3: Senior Strength Secrets
- **Handle:** `@SeniorStrengthSecrets-n7y` — https://www.youtube.com/@SeniorStrengthSecrets-n7y

## Methodology

**Searches run (YouTube search box, logged out, default region/language as served):**
1. `neuropathy exercises for seniors`
2. `brain health exercises for seniors`
3. `senior nutrition tips over 60`
4. `senior balance exercises fall prevention`

For each search, results (standard videos, Shorts rows, and "From health sources" rows) were scanned for channel names that looked unfamiliar/small; candidate channels were opened directly via their `/videos` (and where useful `/about` or the "...more"/"More info" panel) pages, sorted "Oldest" to reconstruct visible chronology, and cross-checked against subscriber/video counts shown in the channel header or "More info" panel.

**Channels opened and checked in full (beyond the 6 documented above):** Dr. Yonatan Whitten (`@painfixprotocol`), Her Health 60+ (`@HerHealth60`), Healthy Aging - Senior Health Tips (`@HealthyAging.60`), SENIOR FOOD BLOG (`@SeniorFoodBlog`), Senior Vital Health (`@SeniorVitalHealth101`), Dr Todd Sullivan (`@drtoddsullivan`), Senior BeWell (`@SeniorBeWell`), Seniors Circle (`@SeniorsCircleYT`), Gentle Health (`@GentleHealth`), Aging Senior Health (`@AgingSeniorHealth`) — 10 channels opened and examined beyond the 6 in the main tables, all excluded either for being too large or not fitting the breakout-relative-to-own-catalog pattern.

**Channels identified only from search-result snippets (view count + channel name), not opened as full channel pages**, because their single visible view count in the "Surgeon REVEALS" search results was already too low to plausibly host a breakout, and time/effort budget did not allow opening all ~20 siblings in full: Senior Health Official (132 views), Senior Vitality (168), Senior Life Extend (61), Smart Senior Care (10), Senior Wellness Fit (40), Ever Fit Guide (756), Sixty Secrets (2.7K), Senior Health Care (1), Healthy Life Secrets (4), Senior Health Prime (1.2K), Imperial Fitness Hub (128K — this one is a possible miss and could be worth a follow-up check in a future pass, as 128K on what may be a small channel would qualify as a breakout; it was not opened due to time constraints).

**What could not be accessed / hit walls:**
- The dedicated YouTube **transcript side-panel** ("Show transcript" button) was present in the DOM on watch pages but could not be reliably clicked/opened in this logged-out browsing session — consistent with the prior pass's finding that it is effectively gated without sign-in. This was **not worked around or bypassed**; per instructions, no sign-in was attempted.
- However, **live closed captions rendered directly on the video player** during autoplay on both the Amos Boone and SeniorHood breakout videos, without sign-in, and these were read directly off-screen (a small number of caption lines each, from what happened to play during page interaction — not a full transcript). This is reported as a partial, incidental workaround (regular viewing, not a bypass of any access control) and is noted plainly wherever used.
- Exact "channel joined" dates were only retrieved for Amos Boone (May 18, 2019, via the "More info" panel); this was not chased down for every channel given time constraints, since upload-date-relative-to-video ("Xmo ago") data was available directly in every channel's video list and was sufficient to establish visible chronology and breakout position.
- No attempt was made to view Shorts-only breakout patterns in depth; this research focused on standard long-form videos as instructed by the task's emphasis on script-opening/hook analysis, which is not meaningful for Shorts.

**Total distinct channels opened and inspected as full channel pages in this pass: 16** (6 documented in depth above + 10 excluded/noted above), across 11 searches.
