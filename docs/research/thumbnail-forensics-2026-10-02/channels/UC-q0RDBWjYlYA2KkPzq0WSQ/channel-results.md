## Bounded findings

Large readable labels and coherent illustration are recurring strengths. Broad overview promises and unexplained dates or force counts are recurring weaknesses. The strongest diagnostic ratings favor concrete social or military tensions, while the best public performer is a geographic overview. Treat proposed edits as hypotheses requiring testing.
