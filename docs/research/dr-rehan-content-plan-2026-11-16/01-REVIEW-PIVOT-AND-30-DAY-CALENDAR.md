### 2.4 Format (har long-form)
- **Length:** 8–12 min. Abhi 3 min hai; 20 min tak foran na jayein, pehle 8–12 test karein. Report 4 ka length experiment yahi hai.
- **Title:** age marker ("After 60" / "Over 65"), specific sawal, aur asli payoff.
  - "SHOCKING", "INSTANTLY", "never" aur fake numbers na likhein.
  - 50–70 characters. Hashtags title mein nahi.
- **Thumbnail:** Dr Rehan ka asli chehra + ek object (BP cuff, goli ki botal, ankle, stopwatch) + 3–4 words. Cartoon ya AI face nahi.
- **Script spine:**
  1. 0:00 Hook: roz ka manzar, ya "aap ki report/botal par yeh number/naam"
  2. 0:30 "Main cardiologist hoon, 30 saal…" ek line mein, phir promise
  3. Mechanism, jo seedhi zabaan mein samjhaya jaye
  4. 3–5 points, har ek ke saath guideline ka naam screen par
  5. "Kab foran doctor/emergency"
  6. Doctor se poochhne ke 3 sawal
  7. Disclaimer + specific next video
- **Medical QA (Report 4 checklist):**
  - Koi absolute claim nahi
  - "Dawai khud band na karein, doctor se baat karein"
  - Emergency symptoms ke saath "call emergency services"
  - Description mein sources
- **Cadence:** 2 long-form/hafta + 2 Shorts har LF se katay hue.
  - Agar 2/hafta mumkin na ho to sirf ⭐ wale topics karein (1/hafta).
