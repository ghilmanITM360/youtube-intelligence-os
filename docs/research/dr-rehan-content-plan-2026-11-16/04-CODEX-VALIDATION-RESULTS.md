## 3. Medical aur news claims

- **T01 · STREAM.** Rodondi N, Haller ML, Bardoczi JB, et al. *Discontinuing Statins in Older Adults without Cardiovascular Disease*. NEJM, online 7 Nov 2026. 1,880 participants, age 70+, kam az kam do chronic conditions aur established CVD ke baghair. Do saal par event-free restricted mean 96.5 vs 98.5 hafte (band vs jaari), difference −2.0 hafte, 95% CI −3.7 se −0.3, predefined noninferiority margin −5.2 hafte. Primary endpoint ka criterion meet hua. Puri follow-up mein, jo 4 saal tak chali aur jis ki median 103 hafte thi, composite heart attack, ischemic stroke ya any-cause death 119/938 (12.7%) vs 87/942 (9.2%) tha. In percentages ko fixed 4-year absolute risk ke taur par use karne ke liye paper ki time-specific estimate chahiye. Investigators shared decision-making kehte hain. Video mein apni statin jaari rakhne aur change doctor se discuss karne ka message dein. [NEJM paper](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2606634) · [ACC trial summary](https://www.acc.org/Latest-in-Cardiology/Articles/2026/11/06/14/37/sat-445pm-statins-aha-2026) · [AHA author statement](https://newsroom.heart.org/news/discontinuing-statins-generally-safe-for-older-adults-without-heart-disease)
