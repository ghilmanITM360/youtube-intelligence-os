# Example: original traffic-signal explainer

This is a skill validation example, not source-video research or a rendered video. The fictional script below is original to this test. Every timing value is an editorial estimate pending a real voice take.

> A city wakes to find every traffic light frozen green. At first movement seems faster. At the first junction, drivers enter together; emergency vehicles cannot pass. Engineers isolate signals, set temporary crossing guards, and restore timed phases. The city learns flow needs coordination, not constant permission.

## Asset prompts

1. **City grid:** Original 9:16 vector-ready overhead traffic schematic, four streets, geometric vehicle blocks, separate signal tiles, generous caption-safe space. Deliver editable road, vehicle, signal, route and text layers. Distinct visual identity; no xkcd characters, copied frames, lettering, logos or third-party assets.
2. **Junction recovery:** Original 9:16 four-way junction matching the grid's line weights. Separate layers for four approach lanes, stopped vehicles, emergency route, guard, engineer, controller and three phase bands. Keep routes readable in grayscale; use newly drawn shapes.

## Motion and validation

Green tiles activate on opening sentence; vehicles advance. Cut to junction at “first junction”; animate paths toward the center, stopping short of collision. Reveal emergency route at “cannot pass”. Cut at “Engineers”, then show isolation, guard control and timed lane release in spoken order. Reveal final caption after “coordination”. Final keyframes must be aligned to an approved recording.
