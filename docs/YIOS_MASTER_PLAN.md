## Mission and present boundary

Turn empirical YouTube research into evidence-traceable intelligence that helps create **original videos** from an owner-provided script, voiceover and assets, using the production techniques of an explicitly selected reference channel. Channel techniques are evidence to test, not permission to copy another creator's expressive work.

**Existing:** V1 Topic, Packaging, Hook and Script intelligence feed the production-plan orchestrator. Editing/Visual guidance is available only where registered forensic evidence supports it; missing evidence is explicit. Channel Strategy is GENERALIZED_WORKING with limited cross-niche calibration. These are planning capabilities, not a finished autonomous video-production engine. See [project status](../PROJECT_STATUS.md), [architecture](Architecture.md) and [phase roadmap](Phases.md) for implementation boundaries and dated evidence.

**Planned:** a reviewed path from supplied inputs through asset and motion planning, editable timelines, rendering and human acceptance. No production engine, service, acquisition, infrastructure or storage implementation starts from this document alone.

## Original production path — planned

1. Select a channel and a topic hypothesis from internal evidence; validate factual gaps through separately authorized research. Preserve uncertainty, counterexamples and financial/scientific claim sources.
2. Accept an original script, supplied voiceover and owner assets; record versions, provenance and rights. Align to actual narration before treating estimated timing as measured.
3. Translate supported channel techniques into original storyboards, asset-generation briefs and motion plans. Specify composition, layers, transitions, pacing and exceptions without reusing reference characters, captures, music or protected expressive sequences as finished assets.
4. Build an editable timeline with portable asset locators and explicit tool/runtime requirements. Editing automation and rendering require a separately approved implementation and validation scope.
5. Validate source/asset resolution, timeline coverage, timing, legibility, audio synchronization, factual claims, originality and rights. Preserve rejected outputs and reasons. Human creative, listening and audiovisual review gates final acceptance.

## Git, binary evidence and portability

Git stores reviewed code, schemas, documentation, structured research and portable manifests. Original MP4s stay outside Git; large frames, audio, editable binary projects and renders require an owner-approved private storage policy. Historical tracked exceptions are documented in the [repository safety review](audits/WHAT_IF_REPOSITORY_SAFETY_2026-10-09.md); this plan authorizes no removal or history rewrite.

A future binary manifest should connect channel/video/project, original path, relative locator, SHA-256, bytes, role, rights, tool dependencies and storage object/version. Credentials stay outside Git. Use configurable roots for new consumers; retain historical absolute paths as provenance rather than silently rewriting archives.

No provider has passed private access, quota, upload and independent-restore acceptance in this preservation work. A published Git commit, local copy, checksum list or provider ETag is not proof of complete backup. Acceptance requires independent retrieval to an approved destination, recomputed SHA-256, complete dependency resolution and documented failures. LFS and recovery bundles require their own restore gates.
